# Wonderful (wonderful.ai)

## 1. Snapshot
Enterprise AI agent platform (voice, chat, email, back office) for regulated industries; founded 2025 in Tel Aviv by Israeli entrepreneurs, headquartered in Amsterdam; ~650 employees across 35+ countries as of September 2026; raised $550M Series C (Sept 2, 2026) at a $5B valuation, more than $800M total, and is actively opening its first Latin America offices, including Mexico.

## 2. Core narrative
Wonderful went from seed to a $5B valuation in about 14 months by betting that AI-agent adoption in banking, telco, healthcare, insurance, and utilities requires "local depth, not global scale": agents embedded in-language and in-context, delivered by forward deployed engineers who sit with the customer rather than a self-serve SaaS motion. It is now running that exact embedded-FDE playbook to open a fresh LATAM beachhead, with Mexico City as one of its first footholds and this CDMX FDE req part of that first wave.

## 3. Mission & product
- Platform has three named layers: Wonderful Agents (the AI agents themselves), Wonderful Systems (a unified operating layer for AI-native enterprises), and an AI Gateway (model-agnostic routing/integration layer that lets customers pick the best model per workload). (wonderful.ai homepage)
- Agents operate across voice, chat, email, and back-office systems, in any language or cultural context, for both customer-facing workflows (support, collections, sales) and internal workflows (onboarding, compliance, IT support). (wonderful.ai homepage; Index Ventures)
- Serves six regulated, mission-critical sectors: banking, telecom, healthcare, utilities, insurance, retail. (wonderful.ai homepage)
- Named case studies on the site: Banco Caja Social (collections across 2.5M clients), Telefonica (91.5% containment rate), Petrol Ofisi (internal IT automation), PPC Energie (billing). (wonderful.ai homepage)
- Reported production metrics: 70%+ of customers expand to additional workflows within three months; handling-time reductions up to 60%, containment rates above 80%. (The Next Web, Series B coverage)
- Partnership with McKinsey to co-deliver enterprise AI transformation "from strategy to scale," signaling a push into large, consulting-led enterprise deals alongside its own FDE motion. (McKinsey capabilities page; title/positioning verified, full article body not fetched)

## 4. Culture & voice
- Careers page frames the company as looking for "high-ownership, mission-driven builders and operators" who value shipped production results over theory or title, with explicit emphasis on on-site customer engagement. (wonderful.ai/careers)
- CEO Bar Winkler's framing of the model: "It requires marrying best-in-class technology together with flawless delivery, on the ground with customers." (TechCrunch, Nov 2025)
- CTO Roey Lalazar on platform philosophy: "Customers can adopt whichever parts of the platform make the most sense, integrate them with existing systems, choose the best models for each workload, and retain ownership of everything they build." (Series C coverage, HPCwire/Yahoo Finance)
- Insight Partners' investment thesis on Wonderful: "the next wave of AI adoption will come from local depth, not just global scale," each market gets a dedicated GM and local engineering presence rather than one global rollout. (Insight Partners, "AI that speaks every language")
- Company blog runs first-person, market-opening posts under founder bylines (the LATAM office announcement is bylined by CEO Bar Winkler), suggesting leadership writes/owns expansion narratives directly rather than routing them through PR. (wonderful.ai/blog-articles/latam)

## 6. Engineering & AI direction
- Platform is explicitly model-agnostic: the AI Gateway continuously benchmarks and routes to the best model per workload rather than locking to one vendor. (wonderful.ai homepage; The Next Web)
- Deployment model is "embedded agentic engineers": FDEs sit inside the customer's environment and integrate directly with existing CRM, billing, and ticketing systems, which the company says compresses rollouts to weeks rather than months, with each deployment feeding improvements back into shared language models and workflow templates. (Insight Partners)
- A company blog post, "FDE: The Engineers Owning The Last Mile" (Mar 3, 2026), is dedicated specifically to the FDE role as a discipline; full body text was not retrievable at research time, but the existence and framing of the post confirms FDE is a named, celebrated engineering identity at the company, not just a job title. (wonderful.ai/blog-articles/owning-the-last-mile)
- Careers page describes two FDE archetypes: broad integrations/agents/data generalists, and specialists (e.g. telephony solution architects), both centered on designing and deploying agents against real business problems. (wonderful.ai/careers)
- This posting's own "what you'll do" list: partner directly with customers to define success criteria, translate open-ended problems into technical designs, build agents that integrate with customer systems, own reliability and iteration of live deployments, and feed learnings back into shared tools/docs. (job id 45499 description)
