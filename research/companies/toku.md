## 1. Snapshot

Toku is a Latin American fintech (Payment Relationship Management / accounts-receivable
automation) that helps large enterprises collect recurring payments and automate the
customer-facing side of collections, including AI conversational agents. Y Combinator S21;
founded 2020 in Chile by Cristina Etcheberry, Francisca Noguera, and Enzo Tamburini; HQ
listed as Mexico City with hubs in Santiago and Sao Paulo; ~280 employees across Chile,
Mexico, and Brazil as of March 2026; raised a $48M Series A (April 2025, led by Oak HC/FT)
at a post-money valuation over $175M, total raised $55M+.
[YC](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/toku),
[Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/fundraising-news/toku-raises-48-million-series-expand-accounts-receivable-saas-across-latin),
[Fintechile interview, 2026-03-16](https://www.fintechile.org/noticias/cristina-etcheberry-ceo-y-cofundador-de-toku-el-objetivo-para-2026-es-duplicar-nuestra-operacion)

## 2. Core narrative

Toku is an AI-native fintech scaling fast (Mexico operation tripled and Brazil's processed
collections grew 30x in the 12 months to March 2026) that is deliberately rebuilding its
core collections product around AI agents rather than treating AI as a bolt-on: it shipped
a production AI collections agent (launched March 2026, ~9-month build) that autonomously
picks channel, timing and message per debtor across email, WhatsApp and voice, and now
automates roughly 80% of collection conversations, cutting bad debt by up to 50% for some
clients. This specific role exists to extend that bet: a "founder-mentality" forward-deployed
engineer who ships a conversational-agent MVP with a real client in weeks, not a generalist
backend hire.

## 3. Mission & product

Toku calls its category "Payment Relationship Management" (PRM): software that connects a
client's ERP/CRM to banks and payment rails to automate the full financial relationship with
their customers, from collection strategy and multichannel contact through payment
processing, reconciliation and dispute handling. Stated purpose (from their own site): "liberar
a las empresas del desorden invisible que genera la cobranza" (free companies from the invisible
chaos collections creates), on the philosophy that if something can be automated, it should be,
so teams can focus on higher-value work rather than manual chasing. B2B SaaS sold to large
enterprises with recurring-revenue billing: insurance, telecom, real estate, utilities,
education, highways/tolls, financial services, retail and healthcare, including named clients
Claro, Aguas Andinas, Chevrolet, Mapfre, Liverpool and MetLife. Over 550 large enterprise
clients across Chile, Mexico and Brazil; the platform processes on the order of USD $4-8B per
year (figures vary by source and date, see sources); ISO 27001 and PCI DSS Level 1 certified.
[trytoku.com](https://trytoku.com/),
[Financial IT](https://financialit.net/news/fundraising-news/toku-raises-48-million-series-expand-accounts-receivable-saas-across-latin),
[Mobiletime, 2026-07-06](https://mobiletime.la/noticias/06/07/2026/toku-expansion-en-brasil/)

## 4. Culture & voice

Toku's own engineering job postings describe the culture they select for: curious, proactive
people with strong ownership who "no tengan miedo de desafiar el status quo" (aren't afraid to
challenge the status quo), who value Engineering, Product and Business working as one integrated
team, and who are expected to actively use AI tools as part of the development cycle to "acelerar
[el] trabajo sin comprometer la calidad del software" (speed up their work without compromising
software quality). Toku's own hiring process for engineers explicitly asks candidates to describe
a project that challenged them, a creative solution they drove, or a change in how they build
software that meaningfully increased their impact, i.e. they screen for engineers with a story of
initiative, not just a stack checklist. CEO Cristina Etcheberry frames the company's ambition as
building "la infraestructura sobre la cual se construya el futuro de los pagos" (the
infrastructure the future of payments gets built on) in Latin America, and has spoken publicly
about scaling a 250+ person team across three countries while keeping "velocidad, flexibilidad y
mentalidad de crecimiento" (speed, flexibility and a growth mindset). No verified public social
media / brand-voice signal beyond the site and press was found in this pass; treat tone claims
beyond the above as unverified.

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago

**Best hook to lead with:** the FDE role's own framing, "founder mentality: scope a real
problem, ship an MVP in weeks, validate directly with the client before scaling," is close to
a literal description of how Santiago has already worked, not a translation exercise. He built
and shipped client-facing MVPs solo end to end (Eugenia.ai from concept to 20+ building

reporting, onboarding and, notably, **collections automation**, the same domain Toku's product
lives in. Toku's own "what we're looking for" language (comfortable with ambiguity, strong
ownership, translates open problems into concrete solutions, motivated to build AI-powered
products) reads like a description of his founder background more than a typical mid-level
engineer profile.

  HOA/property-administrator deployments, requirements gathering) and Pragma AI (client
