# Redzone (QAD | Redzone): Company Dossier

DRAFT for Santiago's review. Researched 2026-07-21, for job id 11884 ("Principal AI
Go-to-Market Operations Engineer", Ciudad de Mexico, Mexico, posted via Adzuna, source
listing at smartrecruiters.com/QADInc).

## 1. Snapshot

Redzone (branded "QAD | Redzone") is a connected-workforce SaaS platform for manufacturing
frontline teams (OEE/productivity, quality, maintenance/CMMS, learning management), sold to
mid-market and enterprise manufacturers (food and beverage, CPG, industrial, chemicals,
automotive, life sciences). Founded 2013, HQ Miami, Florida. Acquired by QAD Inc. in February
2023 for roughly $1B, QAD's largest acquisition ever. QAD itself (ERP for manufacturers,
founded 1979, HQ Santa Barbara, California) has been owned by private equity firm Thoma Bravo
since 2021 (~$2B take-private). Combined QAD entity has roughly 1,400 to 1,900+ employees
depending on source and date (estimates vary by data provider). This specific role is posted
by QAD Inc. (the parent), not by a standalone "Redzone" legal entity, and is tagged
`#LI-Remote` on the official posting, not explicitly hybrid/on-site CDMX.

## 2. Core narrative

QAD | Redzone is mid-transformation from an ERP and shop-floor software vendor into an
"agentic AI for manufacturing" company: it just launched Champion AI (built on AWS Bedrock
AgentCore and SageMaker), acquired an agentic procurement-AI startup (Kavida.ai) to accelerate
that roadmap, and signed a strategic partnership with TCS explicitly to pair AI-driven
transformation with faster ERP modernization for mid-market manufacturers. This specific role,
sitting inside QAD's own revenue organization rather than the customer-facing product, asks
someone to run that exact playbook (audit a tech stack, replace point tools with AI agents,
build LLM tooling that a non-technical team uses daily) on QAD's own Sales/RevOps/Marketing/CS
engine. The company is, in effect, asking a candidate to dogfood its own product thesis
internally.

## 3. Mission & product

- Redzone's platform connects production, quality, and maintenance teams in real time and
  layers AI ("ChampionAI") over that data to flag issues, predict run durations, automate
  routine tasks, and deliver daily operational summaries; positioned as augmenting frontline
  workers, not replacing them.
  ([rzsoftware.com/product](https://www.rzsoftware.com/product))
- Serves 2,000+ factories across 39 countries and 600,000+ frontline users at time of writing,
  per Redzone's own leadership page (earlier 2023 acquisition-era press cited "1,000+ plants,
  300,000 workers", so the installed base has grown materially since the QAD acquisition).
  ([rzsoftware.com/about/leadership-team](https://rzsoftware.com/about/leadership-team/),
  [finsmes.com](https://www.finsmes.com/2023/02/qad-acquires-redzone.html))
- Core modules: Productivity (OEE, average cited 20 to 26% productivity gain within 90 days),
  Compliance (digital QMS), Reliability (CMMS/TPM), Learning (LMS).
  ([rzsoftware.com/product](https://www.rzsoftware.com/product),
  [summitpartners.com](https://www.summitpartners.com/news/redzone-the-1-connected-workforce-solution-reports-record-growth-and-reinforces-its-mission-to-power-the-productivity-of-frontline-workers))
- Business model is enterprise subscription/SaaS, sold to manufacturing operations leaders
  (COO/plant-ops buyers), not a self-serve product. ([rzsoftware.com/product](https://www.rzsoftware.com/product))
- Parent QAD sells ERP, demand/supply-chain planning, global trade/transportation execution,
  and quality management to 2,000+ manufacturers in 100+ countries; the stated strategy is to
  combine QAD's "system of record" (ERP) with Redzone's "system of action" (frontline execution).
  ([qad.com press release, Feb 2023](https://www.qad.com/about/news/-/room/read/2023/qad-acquires-redzone-the-world-s-1-connected-workforce-platform-to-fortify-its-vision-of-the-adaptive-enterprise),
  [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QAD_Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QAD_Inc.))

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago

**Best hook to lead with**: QAD | Redzone is turning its entire business into an agentic-AI
company for manufacturing (Champion AI, Kavida acquisition, AWS build, TCS partnership) and
this specific role is that same bet applied inward, building an AI-first operating layer for
Sales/RevOps/Marketing/CS. That is nearly a mirror of what Santiago already built at Pragma AI:
an internal MCP "brain" service, 15 agent definitions, reusable routines, and internal
automations for quoting, reporting, onboarding, and collections, used daily by non-technical
operators. It is also what he built at Online Basics: an internal ops platform (pricing
dashboard, inventory admin, ETL pipelines) that ran a real $8M business day to day.

Supporting angles:
- Production LLM workflow engineering (Anthropic and OpenAI APIs, prompt engineering, RAG,
  multi-stage validation pipelines at Online Basics and Pragma AI) maps directly onto the
  posting's ask for "AI agent orchestration" and "prompt library / training dataset"
  development.
- Track record replacing manual, vendor-dependent workflows with in-house tooling (Online
  Basics: cut manual reporting from hours to minutes across 10+ third-party APIs) matches the
  posting's ask to "audit the tech stack and identify redundancies where AI can replace
  high-cost, low-utility vendors."
- Founder background (0-to-1 building, client/stakeholder management, being the person who both
  writes the code and owns the business outcome) fits a "Principal" scope role that sits at the
  intersection of engineering and revenue strategy rather than a narrow IC ticket-taker role.

Honest concerns / watch-outs:
- Real gap: no direct hands-on experience with the named RevOps/sales-engagement stack
  (Salesforce as CRM/data hub, Clay, Outreach, Gong/Chorus). His internal-tooling and ETL work
  is adjacent but not the same as GTM-stack administration; this is the one skill the match
  tool already flagged as missing and it should be owned honestly, not glossed over.
- The role reports into a Marketing/RevOps function at QAD's corporate level, not the Champion
  AI product engineering org; the "sexiest" AI work at the company (the actual manufacturing
  product) is a different team. Worth confirming in interview how much cross-pollination there
  is between the two.
- Location ambiguity: the job aggregator (Adzuna) tags this "Ciudad de Mexico, Mexico," but the
  employer's own posting on SmartRecruiters marks it `#LI-Remote`, and QAD has other Mexico
  City-tagged roles that are also remote. This looks like genuine Mexico-based remote hiring
  rather than a hybrid/on-site CDMX requirement, but it is worth confirming directly rather than
  assuming either way.
- No independent culture or engineering-blog signal was found; anything about day-to-day team
  culture, tooling beyond what's in the job ad, or working hours should be treated as unknown
  until asked in an interview.
