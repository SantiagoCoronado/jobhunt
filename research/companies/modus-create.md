# Modus Create

Draft dossier for Santiago's review. Researched 2026-09-01. Target req: job id 33694,
"AI Engineer (Python, Machine Learning, Generative AI/LLMs)", Mexico req, Greenhouse job
7977989003 (https://moduscreate.com/careers/7977989003?gh_jid=7977989003).

## 1. Snapshot

Digital product engineering consultancy, founded 2011, fully remote since founding, headquartered
in Reston, Virginia. Client-services firm: staffs and delivers engineering teams for enterprise
clients rather than building its own product. Took its first outside capital, a minority growth
investment from private equity firm JLL Partners, in December 2021, and has since acquired five
companies (most recently Clarisoft Technologies, January 2023) to add capabilities in ML/data
engineering (Tweag), Atlassian services (Twybee), and other practices. Headcount estimates vary
by source: the company's own about page says 350+ team members across 55+ countries; a
third-party data provider (Revelio Labs, via LeadIQ) puts it closer to 275 as of March 2026. Both
are external estimates and neither is a filed number; the range is close enough to treat as
"a few hundred people, distributed globally."

## 2. Core narrative

Modus Create built its identity as "remote-first before it was cool" (its own words) and has spent
the last four years using PE backing to roll up smaller engineering/ML/Atlassian shops into one
platform, then rebranding in November 2025 under a new CEO (Sharon Lynch, ex-Slalom Build) around
the pitch of uniting "human creativity, AI, and modern engineering" for enterprise clients. It is
mid-turnaround: new C-suite (CEO, Chief Growth Officer, Chief Customer Officer all appointed within
the last year), a brand refresh, and a fresh AWS Generative AI Competency, all aimed at
repositioning a decade-old staff-augmentation consultancy as an AI-era product engineering partner
for Fortune 500 clients. This is the story to lean into: a company betting its next chapter on AI
delivery capability, hiring the engineers who will make that bet real.

## 3. Mission & product

Modus Create does not sell a product; it sells engineering teams and delivery. Its practices span
AI and data, product engineering, design/UX, platform engineering and MLOps, developer experience,
and security, delivered through embedded teams that "work side-by-side with clients to design,
build, and scale custom solutions" (from the job posting itself). Stated mission: "to partner with
companies building for tomorrow and leading in the digital age." Named clients and case studies
include Bright MLS (product launch and support-load reduction during a merger), Wayfair (GitHub
migration), Kaiko (ML data framework for cancer-research facilities), and EVERSANA (GenAI-assisted
MLR approvals in life sciences); the about/careers copy also lists Burger King, Baskin-Robbins,
PBS, Uniqlo, McAfee/Intel Security, Pfizer, AARP, Audi, and Marriott as past clients. Technology
partners: AWS (Premier Tier Partner, holds both a Generative AI Competency and a Life Sciences
Competency, one of 36 AWS partners globally with the latter), GitHub, and Atlassian (award-winning
partner). Client industries per Clutch reviews: education, financial services, IT, media, medical,
and consumer products, roughly evenly split. Project sizes on Clutch run from $10,000 to $1.5
million, with $50,000 to $200,000 most common; billing is typically time and materials.

## 4. Culture & voice

Careers page states five values: "better together," "be curious," "lead with trust," "share with
clarity," and "passion for the craft," alongside a self-description of "innovation and
craftsmanship over compliance." The company markets its 10+ years of remote-first operating
experience and holds Great Place To Work certification plus a listed "Top 25 Companies in Greater
D.C. Area for Diversity" recognition. Tone in its own copy (job posting, blog) is confident and
plain, phrases like "We were fully remote before it was cool!" and "we want to hear from you,"
not irreverent, but not stiff-corporate either. Glassdoor aggregate is 3.6 out of 5 across 78
reviews, with 71 percent of reviewers saying they would recommend the company to a friend;
category breakdown skews toward flexibility and remote work (4.1 work-life balance) but weaker on
compensation (3.2), senior management (3.1), and career growth (3.1). Recurring negative theme
relevant to this hire: reviewers note "no internal development training or career path program"
and describe leadership as lacking structure in places. A specific and directly relevant point:
one review states that "global contractors don't receive benefits unlike US, Romania, and Costa
Rica based employees" and that short contract terms make financial planning difficult for that
population.

## 5. Recent news (last ~12 months)

- November 6, 2025: new leadership wave and brand refresh. Sharon Lynch (ex-Slalom Build, where she
  led 1,300+ people) installed as CEO, succeeding co-founder Pat Sheridan, who moves to Chairman.
  Kevin McClelland joins as Chief Growth Officer, Jon Allegre as Chief Customer Officer. The
  refreshed brand is framed explicitly around AI: "the future of digital business will be shaped by
  those who unite human creativity, AI, and modern engineering."
- Around the same announcement: Modus Create highlights newly achieved AWS Generative AI
  Competency and an AWS Life Sciences Competency (one of 36 global AWS partners with the latter),
  positioning the firm for regulated-industry AI work.
- January 10, 2023 (older but still the most recent acquisition on record): acquisition of
  Clarisoft Technologies, continuing the post-JLL-Partners acquisition run that also added Tweag
  (ML/data engineering, Paris) and Twybee (Atlassian services, France) in 2022.
- No new funding round since the December 2021 JLL Partners growth investment has surfaced in
  public sources; that remains the company's only disclosed outside financing.

## 6. Engineering & AI direction

Stack signals from the product-engineering page and the job posting: React, Angular, Node.js,
Python, TypeScript on the application side; AWS (Premier Tier Partner), Azure, and GCP for cloud;
Kubernetes and Terraform for infrastructure; AWS Bedrock and OpenAI named specifically for AI/data
work. The AI Engineer req (this one) asks for Python, PyTorch and/or TensorFlow, NLP, RAG pipelines
and vector-based retrieval, integration of AI/ML APIs and foundation models, and MLOps
(versioning, monitoring, CI/CD for ML). Bonus points call out LLM fine-tuning and prompt
engineering, vector databases and semantic retrieval, AI agents and tool/function calling and
multi-agent architectures, AI observability, guardrails, and responsible AI, and cost/latency
optimization for AI applications, a fairly current, agentic-AI-aware wish list, not just
"call an LLM API." Blog themes over the last stretch lean into AI adoption strategy and ROI
skepticism rather than pure engineering depth: titles like "Rushing AI, missing ROI," "The AI
ambition gap: why legacy systems can't keep up," "The AI bottleneck isn't coding, it's everything
after it," and "Output is abundant, judgment is not" (about agentic AI). Read together, the
engineering narrative is consultancy-flavored: less "we ship our own AI product," more "we help
enterprise clients get real ROI out of AI instead of chasing pilots," with MLOps, observability,
and governance treated as first-class, not afterthoughts.

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago

Best hook to lead with: Santiago has built and shipped the exact things this req lists, RAG
pipelines with retrieval and citation gating (Pragma Brain), LLM workflow automation on the
Anthropic and OpenAI APIs (Online Basics), and an agent with tool/function calling (the
personal-finance agent), and he has done it while owning client relationships and delivery, which
is what a staffing-model AI Engineer role actually needs day to day: someone who can sit inside a
client's team and be productive fast, not someone who needs months of ramp.

Second hook: Modus Create's current story is a turnaround bet on AI delivery credibility (new
CEO, new AWS GenAI competency, a rebrand built around AI). Santiago's founder background, building
and selling AI-powered products end to end rather than working one layer of a large team, matches
a consultancy that is trying to prove it can deliver real AI outcomes, not just headcount.

Honest concerns and watch-outs:
- This is a staffing/billable-consultancy model (the Greenhouse listing itself tags the role
  "Billable: true" under a client-facing organization), which means utilization, bench time between
  engagements, and client churn are real structural risks that do not show up in the job posting
  language. Nothing in public sources quantifies Modus Create's bench rate or attrition; treat that
  as unverified and worth asking about directly.
- Glassdoor reviews flag that non-US/Romania/Costa Rica contractors, which likely includes Mexico
  given the posting is a contractor-style req, do not get the same benefits as directly employed
  staff, and short contract terms make planning harder. This lines up with the profile's own
  contractor_note (a Mexico-based arrangement is usually contractor, not employment, and should be
  priced above an equivalent salary to cover self-paid benefits). Comp is not listed on the
  posting; no Mexico-specific rate could be verified, so any number should be treated as
  unverified until confirmed in a recruiter conversation, and the ask should follow the profile's
  70,000 USD annual floor at minimum, adjusted upward for the contractor gap.
- Career-growth and senior-management ratings on Glassdoor are the weakest categories (3.1 each),
  worth probing in an interview given how new the leadership team still is.

## 8. Application-question angles

Why this company: lead with the AI Engineer wish list matching what Santiago has actually built
(RAG with retrieval gating, LLM workflow automation, an agent with tool calling), not with the
company's own pitch language. One sentence acknowledging the timing, a consultancy rebuilding
its AI delivery story under new leadership, is enough; do not restate their mission back to them.

Why you fit: concrete project facts do the work. Pragma Brain's retrieval and citation gating maps
directly to the req's RAG and MLOps asks; the Online Basics LLM workflow (100+ listings a month,
multi-stage validation) maps to "production-ready AI/ML applications," not a prototype. Keep it to
one or two projects, named only if the question is specifically about a project; otherwise describe
what was built.

Anything else: if there is room, the founder-to-consultant framing, someone used to owning client
outcomes end to end, is a legitimate angle for a billable, client-facing role, but do not oversell
it into a full paragraph. One line is enough.
