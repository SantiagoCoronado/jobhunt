## 1. Snapshot

Wonderdog is a pre-seed, venture-backed startup building an AI-powered preventive health
platform for dogs: at-home blood draws (40+ biomarkers) reviewed by licensed vets, paired with an
"AI Health Companion" that tracks each dog's individual baseline over time. Founded by the team
behind Barkbus (mobile dog grooming); HQ Hermosa Beach, CA; fully remote engineering team; raised
$5M pre-seed (SEC Form D filed April 1, 2026) led by WndrCo (Jeffrey Katzenberg's firm) and
Maveron. Public launch/press coverage is very recent, within roughly the last month or two of
this research. Live in Los Angeles, New York City (Manhattan, Brooklyn), expanding to more
cities [wonderdoghealth.com](https://wonderdoghealth.com/),
[formds.com filing](https://www.formds.com/issuers/wonder-dog-health-inc),
[venturedailydigest.com / pitchbook indexed via search](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/1367005-69),
[Jeff Safenowitz LinkedIn launch post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jeffsafenowitz_wonderdog-the-preventive-health-platform-activity-7470444730917642240-jVsv).

## 2. Core narrative

Two second-time founders (Jeff Safenowitz and Pat Riley, who built Barkbus, the country's
fastest-growing mobile dog-grooming company, into a business Wilbur Labs acquired) pivoted from
"convenience for dogs" to "preventive medicine for dogs," betting that at-home diagnostics plus AI
trend-detection can catch disease (kidney disease, diabetes, lymphoma, pancreatitis, and more)
months or years before symptoms show, the way human preventive health is moving. It is a brand
new company (SEC filing dated this year, launch coverage only weeks old) that is explicitly
building its engineering org around AI-native, agentic coding from day one rather than retrofitting
it onto a legacy team [pulse2.com Safenowitz/Barkbus profile](https://pulse2.com/barkbus-jeff-safenowitz-profile/),
[wilburlabs.com Barkbus acquisition](https://www.wilburlabs.com/announcements/why-we-acquired-barkbus),
[wonderdoghealth.com/about](https://wonderdoghealth.com/about),
[Jason Stoffer LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jstoffer_wonderdog-the-preventive-health-platform-activity-7470539088765493250-RqdI).

## 3. Mission & product

- **Mission/tagline:** "More good years." Philosophy: "we're not here to replace your vet, we're
  here to make every visit count" and "blood tells the story long before symptoms do"
  [wonderdoghealth.com/about](https://wonderdoghealth.com/about).
- **Product flow:** build a dog profile in-app (medical history, prior bloodwork) to a
  telehealth consult with a licensed vet, then a certified vet tech visits the home for a ~20
  minute blood draw (40+ lab markers across blood health, organ function, metabolic health,
  hormones/endocrine, and inflammation/immunity). Results reviewed by a vet, then tracked over
  time by the AI Health Companion, which compares each new result against that specific dog's own
  baseline (not population averages) and produces a health score and a "what needs action, what
  needs monitoring, what doesn't need worry" care plan
  [wonderdoghealth.com/how-it-works](https://wonderdoghealth.com/how-it-works).
- **Business model:** consumer subscription. $249/year for one annual draw, $449/year for two
  (recommended), one dog per plan, cancel anytime; the app and AI companion are free for a
  "limited time" [wonderdoghealth.com](https://wonderdoghealth.com/).
- **Market/availability:** live in LA and NYC (Manhattan, Brooklyn) at launch, "more cities
  coming soon" [wonderdoghealth.com](https://wonderdoghealth.com/),
  [Jeff Safenowitz LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jeffsafenowitz_wonderdog-the-preventive-health-platform-activity-7470444730917642240-jVsv).
- **Medical credibility:** partnered with North America's largest veterinary reference lab; Dr.
  Evan Antin (DVM), a well-known practicing vet and the most-followed veterinarian on Instagram,
  is Chief Medical Officer [wonderdoghealth.com](https://wonderdoghealth.com/).

  [findwork.dev](https://findwork.dev/XO97YxQ/senior-full-stack-engineer-agentic-first-at-wonderdog),
  [Indeed: Agentic-First Software Engineer listing](https://www.indeed.com/viewjob?jk=1444b440674cae75).

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago

**Lead hook:** Santiago has already run the exact workflow this JD is hiring for, not just used
the tools. He is a founder-engineer who ships full-stack product (React front end, Node/Python
back end) solo or near-solo, and his most recent projects (Plutus, Kairos) are built with Claude
Code and the Claude Agent SDK as the primary engineering interface, including exposing tools via
MCP, which is a level deeper than "comfortable letting Claude write code," it is architecting
software so that Claude/agents are first-class collaborators. That is a rare, concrete match to
"agentic-first," most candidates only claim comfort with the tools; he can point to shipped
systems built that way.

- **0-to-1, founder-scope ownership matches a 2-person eng team.** He built and shipped
  Eugenia.ai (React + Node on AWS) end to end, owned the product lifecycle, and later ran a
  9-person team and $8M-revenue operation at Online Basics. Wonderdog's JD explicitly says "you
  don't need to be a CTO, you need to be smart, senior, and get a lot done," which matches a
  founder who is used to full ownership without a large team around him.
- **Full-stack + API design + monorepo comfort** lines up directly with the JD's stated
  requirements (web app architecture, git/monorepo, API design), and his React/TypeScript/Node
  background (Eugenia.ai, Kairos) covers the stated stack even though the exact backend language
  at Wonderdog (Python) is a slight shift from his most recent Node work, he does have Python
  depth from Plutus (FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, Celery).
- **Honest gaps / watch-outs:**
  - No healthtech, veterinary, or regulated-health-data experience. Should not overstate domain
    knowledge; the honest angle is "consumer product + API integrations experience," not "health
    industry experience."
  - The sibling Indeed posting for a related role at this company requires US business-hours
    real-time availability and rejects async tools; if the specific role Santiago is applying to
    (findwork id 8827) has similar expectations, he should confirm timezone/overlap expectations
    since he is Mexico City based (GMT-6), this is workable but worth clarifying, not assuming.
  - Compensation is not listed on the JD he is applying to; a related posting for the company
    lists $125k-$200k for a broader-scope role, so pay could vary a lot by scope, this should be
    clarified early rather than assumed.
  - Company is extremely early (weeks-old public launch, pre-seed), so this is a real
