# Kavak

## 1. Snapshot
Kavak is a Mexico City-headquartered used-car e-commerce and fintech platform (buy, sell, finance, and manage vehicles) operating across Mexico, Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Turkey, Saudi Arabia, Oman, and the UAE. Founded 2016, it was Mexico's first unicorn (2020) and is Latin America's most highly valued private startup. It closed a USD 300 million Series F led by Andreessen Horowitz in February 2026 (a16z's largest single-company LatAm investment) and reported its first month of consolidated global profitability in December 2025. Employee count is reported around 4,000 to 7,000 across sources (see Sources).

## 2. Core narrative
Kavak built its business (fair, AI-driven used-car pricing, inspection, and reconditioning at scale) on machine learning from early on, and it is now doubling down: after years of growth-at-all-costs and a 2025 down round, it just raised a large new round explicitly earmarked in part for "advancing technology and AI," reached its first profitable month, and says AI agents already serve the majority of customer demand. The pitch for this specific "AI Engineer" role in Mexico City is joining a newly profitable, newly re-funded LatAm unicorn at the exact moment it is rebuilding its core commerce and financing operations around LLMs and agents, not bolting AI on as a feature.

## 3. Mission & product
- Digital platform for buying, selling, financing, and managing new and used cars and commercial vehicles, for individuals and businesses (Wikipedia; kavak.com).
- Inventory of 6,000+ vehicles on the Mexico site; buyer trial period of 7 days or 300 km; seller appraisals with fast payment (kavak.com/mx).
- Reconditioning facility capable of servicing 3,500+ vehicles per month (Wikipedia).
- Fintech arm (Kavak Crédito, launched November 2025; also the Kuna financing brand) has financed over USD 1 billion cumulatively, reaching roughly USD 600 million annualized loan volume in Q4 2025 (Kavak newsroom Series F release).
- Nearly 120,000 transactions in 2025, about 40 percent year-over-year growth (Kavak newsroom Series F release).
- Market position: roughly 1.1 percent of Mexico's annual used-car market (Wikipedia), still small relative to the informal/dealer market it targets.

## 4. Stage & funding
- Founded 2016 by Carlos Garcia Ottati (CEO), seed round via Mountain Nazca (Wikipedia).
- Reached USD 1 billion valuation in 2020 (first Mexican unicorn); USD 8.7 billion valuation after a USD 700 million Series E in September 2021; valuation fell to roughly USD 2.2 billion by a April 2025 round (Wikipedia).
- February 17, 2026: closed a USD 300 million Series F led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z Growth contributed USD 200 million, its largest single-company LatAm bet and first Growth-fund LatAm deal), co-investors WCM Investment Management, Lingotto Innovation, Foxhaven, Galdana Ventures, Stelac, and Allen & Company. Post-round valuation was not disclosed in the release (Kavak newsroom; Bloomberg).
- Backers historically include SoftBank, Sea Ltd., Founders Fund (Wikipedia).
- December 2025: first full month of consolidated global profitability, with Mexico leading and Chile and the GCC also hitting profitability milestones (Kavak newsroom).
- Withdrew from Colombia and Peru in 2025, consolidating around fewer, stronger markets (Wikipedia).

## 5. Tech stack
- Public tech-stack listings show a general web/cloud toolset: Git, Node.js, VS Code, Docker, npm, Google Analytics, Cloudflare (StackShare).
- Machine learning workloads run on AWS: AWS Glue, AWS Lambda, AWS CloudFormation, Amazon Kinesis Firehose, and Amazon CloudWatch power model deployment for pricing, recommendations, credit scoring, and process automation (Mexico Business News / AWS case study, 2021; some detail may be dated but reflects the AWS-centric ML pipeline).
- Computer vision is used for vehicle inspection: 360-degree photos of each car, automated measurement and classification of cosmetic/mechanical details to make grading more objective (America Retail / Startupeable interview with former CTO Fernando Scasserra).
- Current GenAI hiring signals (per search results and the job's own listing) point to an internal "GenAI team" building LLM-based features for customer experience, consistent with the AI Engineer opening in Mexico City.

## 6. Engineering & AI direction
- Company-stated model (from the AI Engineer job posting itself): Kavak calls itself an "AI-first" business, applying AI across conversational commerce, generative marketing, and systems that extract insights from millions of customer interactions (Kavak Lever job posting, job id 9852).
- February 2026 funding release states Kavak has "advanced its use of AI to serve the majority of customer demand through AI agents" and plans to further automate internal workflows through 2026, with part of the new capital earmarked for "advancing technology and AI" (Kavak newsroom Series F release).
- Philosophy, per former CTO Fernando Scasserra (ex-Mercado Libre, led 350+ person teams): AI should work as an "Ironman suit" that augments employees rather than replacing them, with a human-in-the-loop model where automated systems are checked by people (Startupeable interview; America Retail).
- Concrete AI use cases discussed publicly: a 24/7 voice bot for customer service and appointment scheduling, computer-vision based vehicle inspection, ML-driven pricing and credit scoring, and internal process automation (Startupeable; America Retail; Mexico Business News/AWS).
- Org model: engineering teams are run as autonomous "mini startups" with an internal "AI Academy" to drive voluntary AI adoption across teams (Startupeable interview).
- Strategy pattern: new AI/product features are piloted first in Mexico, then adapted market by market based on local regulation and consumer behavior (America Retail).

## 7. Culture & voice
- Company values stated directly in the job posting: Deliver Impact, Engage Fast, Engage with Purpose, Play to Win Together (Kavak Lever job posting).
- General brand values described as disruptive, proactive, loyal, and ambitious, with the stated line "we like to imagine the impossible and work together to achieve it" (Built In company profile, sourced from Kavak).
- Team described as roughly 4,000 people spanning 40+ nationalities across 8 countries (Built In), though other data aggregators (PitchBook, ZoomInfo) put total headcount higher, around 7,000; treat the exact figure as approximate.
- Voice in its own job posting is high-intensity, hypergrowth language: "vivimos la disrupcion, la proactividad y la ambicion en un contexto de hipercrecimiento" (Kavak Lever job posting). Useful signal for matching tone if referencing the role directly, but should not be mirrored uncritically in a cover letter.
