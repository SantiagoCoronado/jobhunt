# Kavak

## 1. Snapshot
Kavak is a Mexico City-headquartered used-car e-commerce and fintech platform (buy, sell, finance, and manage vehicles) operating across Mexico, Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Turkey, Saudi Arabia, Oman, and the UAE. Founded 2016, it was Mexico's first unicorn (2020) and is Latin America's most highly valued private startup. It closed a USD 300 million Series F led by Andreessen Horowitz in February 2026 (a16z's largest single-company LatAm investment) and reported its first month of consolidated global profitability in December 2025. Employee count is reported around 4,000 to 7,000 across sources (see Sources). No newsroom-confirmed funding, exit, or market-entry news has landed since the March 2026 items below as of this refresh (2026-08-24).

## 2. Core narrative
Kavak built its business (fair, AI-driven used-car pricing, inspection, and reconditioning at scale) on machine learning from early on, and it is now doubling down: after a 2025 down round it raised a large new round explicitly earmarked in part for "advancing technology and AI," reached its first profitable month, and by August 2026 says it instantiates 100,000 to 200,000 dedicated AI agents a day, one per active customer interaction, with its own "AI CEO" pilot for at least one market. Two roles map to that story from different angles: an AI Engineer role (Santiago's earlier application, submitted 2026-07-14) sits inside that agent build-out directly, while this second role, Senior Full Stack Engineer on the FinOps / Core Bancario ("Kuna") team, is explicitly framed as a "Product Engineer" who ships end-to-end fintech systems (collections back office, OCR-driven credit dictámenes, postsale bots) using agentic coding tools with guardrails, not as a pure AI-research seat. The pitch for this role is joining a newly profitable, newly re-funded LatAm unicorn's core banking stack at the exact moment it expects every engineer, not just an AI team, to build with AI-assisted coding as a baseline skill.

## 3. Mission & product
- Digital platform for buying, selling, financing, and managing new and used cars and commercial vehicles, for individuals and businesses (Wikipedia; kavak.com).
- Inventory of 6,000+ vehicles on the Mexico site; buyer trial period of 7 days or 300 km; seller appraisals with fast payment (kavak.com/mx).
- Reconditioning facility capable of servicing 3,500+ vehicles per month (Wikipedia).
- Fintech arm operates under the brand "Kuna" internally (per the Senior Full Stack Engineer posting's team name, "Kuna, Core Bancario") and publicly as Kavak Credito (launched November 2025); it has financed over USD 1 billion cumulatively, reaching roughly USD 600 million annualized loan volume in Q4 2025 (Kavak newsroom Series F release).
- Nearly 120,000 transactions in 2025, about 40 percent year-over-year growth (Kavak newsroom Series F release).
- Market position: roughly 1.1 percent of Mexico's annual used-car market (Wikipedia), still small relative to the informal/dealer market it targets.

## 4. Stage & funding
- Founded 2016 by Carlos Garcia Ottati (CEO), seed round via Mountain Nazca (Wikipedia).
- Reached USD 1 billion valuation in 2020 (first Mexican unicorn); USD 8.7 billion valuation after a USD 700 million Series E in September 2021; valuation fell to roughly USD 2.2 billion by an April 2025 round (Wikipedia).
- February 17, 2026: closed a USD 300 million Series F led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z Growth contributed USD 200 million, its largest single-company LatAm bet and first Growth-fund LatAm deal), co-investors WCM Investment Management, Lingotto Innovation, Foxhaven, Galdana Ventures, Stelac, and Allen & Company. Post-round valuation was not disclosed in the release (Kavak newsroom; Bloomberg).
- Backers historically include SoftBank, Sea Ltd., Founders Fund (Wikipedia).
- Q1 2026: Kavak's Series F accounted for nearly a third of all Mexico venture dollars that quarter, and Mexico briefly overtook Brazil as the top LatAm venture-funding destination (industry roundup, Cuantico VP, citing the deal).
- December 2025: first full month of consolidated global profitability, with Mexico leading and Chile and the GCC also hitting profitability milestones (Kavak newsroom).
- Withdrew from Colombia and Peru in 2025, consolidating around fewer, stronger markets (Wikipedia). No further market exits or entries found in press since; the company's 8-country footprint (Mexico, Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Turkey, Saudi Arabia, Oman, UAE) appears stable as of August 2026.
- No newsroom press releases dated after March 10, 2026 found on the official newsroom listing as of this refresh (Kavak newsroom, checked 2026-08-24); the most recent notable public news is the AI-agent operating model story below (Aug 10, 2026), not a funding or corporate-structure event.

## 5. Tech stack
- Primary-source stack, confirmed directly from the Senior Full Stack Engineer job posting (Lever, verified live 2026-08-24): Node.js with TypeScript (primary backend), Python for APIs and data scripts, microservices architecture with integration patterns, and an event-driven environment where transactionality across services matters. The team explicitly expects "Agentic Coding" tool use (AI coding agents) as a productivity and code-quality multiplier, with guardrails and repo standards to limit errors and hallucinations.
- Concrete systems named in that posting: automation of credit "dictamenes" (assessments) using OCR/AI, a collections ("cobranza") back office, and a postsale bot named "Matilda."
- Older, more general listings: StackShare shows Git, Node.js, VS Code, Docker, npm, Google Analytics, Cloudflare (StackShare, undated, treat as generic).
- Machine learning workloads historically run on AWS: AWS Glue, AWS Lambda, AWS CloudFormation, Amazon Kinesis Firehose, and Amazon CloudWatch power model deployment for pricing, recommendations, credit scoring, and process automation (Mexico Business News / AWS case study, 2021; likely dated but still reflects an AWS-centric ML pipeline).
- Computer vision is used for vehicle inspection: 360-degree photos of each car, automated measurement and classification of cosmetic/mechanical details to make grading more objective (America Retail / Startupeable interview with former CTO Fernando Scasserra, 2024).

## 6. Engineering & AI direction
- August 10, 2026: a StartupHub.ai feature on Kavak's Head of AI, Ali Masa, describes a full operating-model shift: the company instantiates 100,000 to 200,000 dedicated AI agents daily, one "mega expert" agent per active customer with its own virtual machine covering financing, car advisory, and insurance in one thread, rather than task-specific bots. Masa cites AI sales agents converting at 2.1x the rate of human sales teams, and describes a company-wide "Jedi Academy" training program (a rename or evolution of the "AI Academy" a former CTO described in 2024) where staff, from the CEO to mechanics, are trained to launch production agents after roughly six weeks. The piece also describes a pilot placing an AI agent as "AI CEO" over one market operation, reported to have lifted profit in its first month; treat the exact market name and the profit figure as a single-source claim from this article, not independently confirmed (StartupHub.ai, Aug 10, 2026).
- February 2026 funding release states Kavak has "advanced its use of AI to serve the majority of customer demand through AI agents" and plans to further automate internal workflows through 2026, with part of the new capital earmarked for "advancing technology and AI" (Kavak newsroom Series F release).
- This specific Senior Full Stack Engineer posting is the clearest primary-source evidence that the agentic push is now an engineering-wide expectation, not confined to one AI team: the role explicitly asks for developers who use AI coding agents to multiply their own output, with guardrails, on core fintech systems (Kavak Lever posting, job id 20359 in local tracker).
- Philosophy, per former CTO Fernando Scasserra (ex-Mercado Libre, led 350+ person teams, no longer confirmed to still be at Kavak as of this refresh): AI should work as an "Ironman suit" that augments employees rather than replacing them, with a human-in-the-loop model where automated systems are checked by people (Startupeable interview; America Retail, 2024).
- Other concrete AI use cases discussed publicly: a 24/7 voice bot for customer service and appointment scheduling, computer-vision based vehicle inspection, ML-driven pricing and credit scoring, OCR-based credit-assessment automation, and internal process automation (Startupeable; America Retail; Mexico Business News/AWS; Aug 2026 Lever posting).
- Strategy pattern: new AI/product features are piloted first in Mexico, then adapted market by market based on local regulation and consumer behavior (America Retail, 2024).
