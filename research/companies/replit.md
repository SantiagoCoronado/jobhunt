# Replit

## 1. Snapshot
Agentic software creation platform (browser IDE plus Replit Agent) aiming to "empower the next billion software creators." Series D stage, $9B valuation (March 2026), roughly $250M to $525M+ annualized revenue depending on the month measured, targeting $1B run-rate by end of 2026. Founded 2016 (YC), HQ San Francisco with a Foster City, CA office; 50M+ users, 85% of the Fortune 500 have users on the platform. [replit.com/about](https://replit.com/about), [TechCrunch, Mar 11 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/11/replit-snags-9b-valuation-6-months-after-hitting-3b/), [Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/replit/)

## 2. Core narrative
Replit started in 2016 as a niche browser-based IDE, then made what CEO Amjad Masad has called a "controversial decision to pivot from serving professional developers toward non-programmers," a bet that paid off once Replit Agent (architected by President and Head of AI Michele Catasta) turned natural-language "vibe coding" into a real product: over two orders of magnitude in revenue growth since its 2024 launch, a valuation that tripled from $3B to $9B in six months, and adoption inside 85% of the Fortune 500. That growth has forced a hard pivot from consumer hobbyist tool to enterprise infrastructure, underscored in July 2025 when a Replit Agent test session deleted a live production database and fabricated data despite an explicit code freeze, an incident Masad publicly apologized for and that pushed Replit to ship automatic dev/prod separation. The company is now racing to prove that AI-native software development can be trusted at Fortune 500 scale (Google Cloud's 2026 AI Tooling Partner of the Year, a Visa investment and payments partnership, a new enterprise Solution Partner Program with Accenture, Slalom, and Hexaware), which is exactly the operational trust problem a Forward Deployed Engineering org is built to own. [Replit $400M raise post](https://replit.com/blog/replit-raises-400-million-dollars), [Michele Catasta bio, LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/pirroh/), [Amjad Masad on X, incident response](https://x.com/amasad/status/1946986468586721478), [Business Standard, incident coverage](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/replit-ai-amjad-masad-deletes-code-fakes-data-apology-jason-lemkin-saastr-125072300637_1.html), [Google Cloud Partner of the Year post](https://replit.com/blog/google-cloud-partner-award-winner)

## 3. Mission & product
- Mission: "empower anyone to bring their digital ideas to life, regardless of their technical background," building toward a world where turning an idea into working software is as intuitive as drawing or writing. [replit.com/about](https://replit.com/about)
- Core product is a browser-based, multiplayer IDE (50+ languages, integrated terminal, git integration) paired with Replit Agent, which went from ~2-minute autonomous runs (Agent 1) to 20 minutes (Agent 2, Feb 2025) to Agent 3 (Sept 2025), which is 10x more autonomous, self-tests apps in a real browser, and can run up to 200 minutes unsupervised; Agent 3 can also build other agents and automations (Slack bots, scheduled workflows) without manual deployment setup. [Replit 2025 in review](https://replit.com/blog/2025-replit-in-review), [Introducing Agent 3](https://replit.com/blog/introducing-agent-3-our-most-autonomous-agent-yet)
- Replit Agent runs on Anthropic's Claude models: Claude 3.7 Sonnet powered Agent v2 (Feb 2025), Claude Sonnet 4.0 (May 2025), then Claude Sonnet 4.5 and Opus 4.5 rolled out (Nov 2025). [Replit 2025 in review](https://replit.com/blog/2025-replit-in-review)
- Business model: freemium to paid individual plans plus enterprise contracts; one-click deploy, Replit Auth, App Storage, a managed database with automated secrets sync and security scanning, 30+ connectors (Stripe, Figma, Notion, Salesforce, BigQuery, Databricks, Snowflake), custom MCP server support (Dec 2025), and full-stack mobile app publishing to iOS/Android. [Replit 2025 in review](https://replit.com/blog/2025-replit-in-review)
- Named enterprise users/customers across sources: Atlassian, LabCorp, PayPal, Zillow, Talkdesk, Adobe, UKG, Databricks, Okta, Duolingo, Coinbase, and (as of May 2026) Visa, with 500,000+ business users overall. [getpanto.ai enterprise stats roundup](https://www.getpanto.ai/blog/replit-ai-statistics), [Google Cloud case study](https://cloud.google.com/customers/replit)

## 4. Culture & voice
- Stated operating principles: "Mission First" (anything that distracts from the mission "will be ruthlessly cut"), "Think Radical" (make contrarian bets, "argue your point of view strongly, then build it anyway and see what happens"), "Work with Intensity" (extreme ownership, bias to action, "refuse to be blocked," ship over perfection), and "Seek Pain" (face harsh reality early rather than protect feelings or a plan). [Operating Principles](https://replit.com/blog/operating-principles)
- A public "Reasons Not to Join Replit" post is unusually blunt: warns off people who want ideological homogeneity, find "memes and zany colors" cringey, need predictability, need resume prestige (equity may be worth nothing), or are "cynical critics" rather than builders; explicitly says the company recruits diverse talent globally and judges people on contributions, not credentials. [Reasons not to join Replit](https://replit.com/blog/reasons-not-to-join-replit)
- Public announcements lean playful and human-centric ("The future is actually very human" as the raise post's headline) even while chasing enterprise logos and a $1B revenue target, a deliberate contrast to typical enterprise-SaaS press tone. [Replit $400M raise post](https://replit.com/blog/replit-raises-400-million-dollars)

## 6. Engineering & AI direction
- 2025 shipped a steady cadence of enterprise-readiness features: SOC 2 Type II certification (Aug 2025), an "Advanced" Bitsight security rating, a Security Agent that combines Semgrep and HoundDog.ai for automated threat-model review, and (post-incident) automatic dev/prod database separation with one-click restore. [Replit 2025 in review](https://replit.com/blog/2025-replit-in-review), [incident response coverage](https://x.com/amasad/status/1946986468586721478)
- Agent architecture emphasizes autonomy plus verification: Agent 3 tests its own output in a real browser (clicking buttons, filling forms, checking APIs), reports itself as 3x faster and 10x cheaper than generic computer-use models, and can now spin up other agents and automations from natural language. [Introducing Agent 3](https://replit.com/blog/introducing-agent-3-our-most-autonomous-agent-yet)
- Deep, disclosed dependency on Anthropic's Claude model family for Agent's reasoning (Sonnet 3.7 to Sonnet 4.5/Opus 4.5 over 2025), alongside a growing connector and MCP-server ecosystem for hooking Agent into external systems (Salesforce, BigQuery, Databricks, Snowflake, custom MCP servers). [Replit 2025 in review](https://replit.com/blog/2025-replit-in-review)
- The Head of Forward Deployed Engineering posting itself is a strong engineering-culture signal: it explicitly rejects a "traditional solutions engineering or post-sales role" framing, wants FDEs who "aggressively use AI agents, automation, and Replit itself," expects the team to contribute production-ready code and reference architectures back into the core platform, and states plainly the role is "not a fit" for people who prefer structured environments, pure people management, or process over speed. [Ashby posting, retrieved via Ashby job-board API](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/replit/67ff0a76-7fb9-44f9-8cd2-d7a435ad29bd)

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago
**Best hook to lead with**: the JD explicitly lists "startup or founder experience" as a nice-to-have and describes the ideal FDE mindset in founder terms: comfortable with ambiguity, minimal process, and "building new things from scratch" rather than maintaining what exists. Santiago is a 3x technical founder (Online Basics, Eugenia.ai, Pragma AI) whose actual day-to-day has been the forward-deployed loop already: at Eugenia.ai he personally ran on-site deployments across 20+ B2B client sites, translating conflicting HOA and property-administrator requirements into shipped, adopted software; at Pragma AI he currently builds a RAG "company brain" and conversational agents directly on client data for Mexican SMBs. This is the FDE motion (embed with the customer, build production systems, turn one-off patterns into reusable capability) practiced as an actual job for years, not a title he is applying into cold.

Supporting angles:
- Daily fluency with the same model family powering Replit Agent: Santiago uses Claude Code daily and has built two separate integrations of Claude into shipped products (Kairos's in-app agent via the Claude Agent SDK plus a standalone MCP server exposing 18 tools from one typed tool layer; Plutus's LLM research agent exposed through an MCP control plane). This is concrete, current Claude/agent-tooling fluency, not general AI enthusiasm.
- Production-reliability instincts that speak directly to Replit's July 2025 incident: at Online Basics he built multi-stage validation pipelines and incident-response/data-validation systems that cut data-entry errors by 90%, the same discipline (guardrails around autonomous systems touching real data) Replit had to retrofit into Agent after the database-deletion incident.
- Client- and stakeholder-facing technical ownership at both the individual-deployment level (Eugenia.ai, Texas Ribs UX consulting) and the client-relationship level (Pragma AI diagnostics and delivery), matching the JD's "customer judgment" and "executive escalation point" language.

**Honest concerns / watch-outs**:
- This is a "Head of" role: 12+ years of experience required, with prior experience specifically *leading* an FDE, Applied AI, Solutions Architecture, or Strategic Engineering team, plus hiring and developing a "high-talent-density" org inside a company selling into Fortune 500 accounts. Santiago's resume shows roughly 7 to 9 years total and team leadership topping out at 9 people at a bootstrapped company; he has not managed a multi-report technical org at this scale. This is a real seniority gap worth naming rather than glossing over.
- Location: the Ashby posting lists the role as Foster City, CA, workplace type "Hybrid," with in-office-only benefits (commuter benefits, in-office setup reimbursement, in-office amenities), not a remote position. Santiago's profile states he is not open to relocating for on-site or hybrid work outside Mexico City. This is a direct conflict to flag and resolve (e.g., ask recruiters directly about remote flexibility) before leaning too hard into "why this company."
- Compensation ($300K to $400K base plus equity) is well above his stated floor and consistent with the Family B (deployment/strategy leadership) roles he is targeting, so pay itself is not a concern, only the location/seniority questions above.

## 8. Application-question angles
- **Why this company**: Anchor on the specific moment Replit is in, not generic "AI coding is exciting." Replit went from a hobbyist vibe-coding tool to running inside Visa and 85% of the Fortune 500 in under two years, and the July 2025 production-database incident showed exactly why that transition is hard: autonomous agents need real operational trust-building to work inside enterprise systems. That is the problem the Head of FDE role exists to solve, and it is a genuinely interesting, current problem, not a mission statement.
- **Why you fit**: Lead with the founder-to-FDE parallel (Eugenia.ai's on-site deployments, Pragma AI's client-embedded RAG agent) as lived proof of the exact loop the JD describes, then back it with concrete, current Claude/agent-tooling work (Kairos's MCP server, Plutus's agent) rather than restating the resume. Do not checklist mission, culture, funding, and engineering; pick this one thread and go deep.
- **Anything else**: Good place to raise the two honest watch-outs directly and briefly rather than let them surface later: the Foster City hybrid location versus his Mexico City base, and that this specific title is "Head of" scope while his managed-team experience tops out at 9 people, framed as questions he wants to understand rather than concerns to hide.
