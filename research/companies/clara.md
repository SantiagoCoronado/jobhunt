## Disambiguation (important)

"Clara" is a common name. This dossier is about the Mexico-founded, now Brazil-headquartered B2B
corporate spend management fintech at clara.com (claracc on LinkedIn), the poster of the target
job. It is NOT: Clara Labs (a separate US scheduling-assistant company), CLARA analytics (US
insurance claims software), Clara Capital (a New York lending firm), or the several unrelated
"Clara" AI-automation startups (tryclara.org, claraai.tech, clara-ai.net) that surface in AI
Business Operations searches [glassdoor.com search results, businesswire.com Clara Capital
release, search results for "AI Business Operations" queries].

## 1. Snapshot

Clara is a corporate spend management platform (cards, bill pay, expense management, AI-driven
spend analytics) for businesses in Mexico, Brazil, and Colombia, founded in 2020 in Mexico City by
Gerry Giacomán Colyer (CEO) and Diego García Escobedo (CTO), who met at Mexican startup Grow
Mobility [en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clara_(company)]. It became LatAm's fastest unicorn in December
2021 on a $70M Series B led by Coatue at a roughly $1B valuation [fintechnews.ch,
member.fintech.global]. Clara relocated its headquarters to São Paulo, Brazil in 2023 after
receiving Central Bank of Brazil authorization to operate as a payment institution
[en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clara_(company)]. As of this research (August 2026), Clara's own homepage
states "more than 40,000 growing companies" use the platform, while the AI Business Operations
Expert job posting itself (also current) describes "over 20,000 large and growing businesses";
both are Clara-authored and current, the discrepancy is unresolved here and likely reflects
different customer segments or counting methods [clara.com homepage; greenhouse posting]. Named
customer logos: Umbro, Toyota, New Balance, Birkenstock, and Magalu [clara.com homepage].
Cumulative funding disclosed publicly: $100M combined Series A/B (2021), a $150M Goldman Sachs
credit line (2022, renewed to $250M+ total debt capacity in Feb 2026), a $60M Series B extension
(2023), an $80M equity and growth round (April 2025), and $70M in structured debt (November 2025)
[en.wikipedia.org, financialit.net, clara.com press]. No new funding, product-launch, or
leadership news was found dated July or August 2026; the most recent dated newsroom post remains
the June 10, 2026 Clara Global launch [clara.com/resources/newsroom, checked live 2026-08-27].

## 2. Core narrative

Clara's distinctive story right now is speed at unicorn scale, on two fronts at once: it built the
fastest path to a $1B valuation LatAm had seen, and five years later it is trying to prove the same
speed advantage applies to how it ships AI-native product and runs its own internal operations. In
June 2026 Clara shipped Clara Global (a standalone product letting LatAm companies manage spend in
any country and currency) built "entirely with AI" by a three-person team in a matter of weeks, and
paired the launch with its own "Corporate AI Monitor" report tracking how 10,000+ Latin American
companies are actually spending on AI [clara.com/pt-br newsroom, latamfintech.co]. As of August
2026 that bet has moved from a single flagship launch into a standing internal function: Clara is
now hiring for at least two "AI Business Operations" roles reporting into a Global Head of
Operations, explicitly tasked with replacing manual cross-functional workflows with agent-based
automation and "leading a team of AIs" instead of a traditional headcount team
[greenhouse posting; builtin.com sibling posting]. CEO Gerry Giacomán frames this as "agility with
discipline": ship fast, but ground every decision in unit economics and local regulatory depth
across Mexico, Brazil, and Colombia [gend.co interview, March 2026]. The role Santiago is targeting
is the clearest current expression of that internal bet: a senior IC expected to architect,
deploy, and scale AI-powered operations across Product, Sales, Finance, and Engineering using N8N,
Claude Code/Codex, and direct LLM APIs [greenhouse posting].

## 3. Mission & product

- Core product lines: physical/virtual/single-use corporate credit cards, Clara Intelligence
  (AI-driven spend analytics), an expense management and approvals platform, Clara TravelPay,
  reimbursements, accounts payable automation, and a fleet card [clara.com homepage].
- Business model: B2B fintech monetizing corporate spend (cards, bill pay, financing) for
  businesses from startups to large enterprise across its three core markets [clara.com homepage;
  en.wikipedia.org].
- Product philosophy per CEO Gerry Giacomán: "agility with discipline," combining rapid shipping
  with unit-economics discipline, and "local depth wins," meaning Clara issues cards locally and
  manages tax compliance per-country rather than treating LatAm as one market; the platform bakes
  in "AI-driven insights and automated compliance" (real-time expense capture and automated tax
  categorization) [gend.co interview, published March 30, 2026].
- Customers: 40,000+ companies per the current homepage (a discrepancy with the job posting's
  "20,000" figure is noted in the Snapshot above); named logos include Umbro, Toyota, New Balance,
  Birkenstock, and Magalu [clara.com homepage].
- September 2025: partnered with Bitso Business to let companies use stablecoin holdings (USDC,
  MXNB) as collateral for Clara's card and payments products without liquidating crypto, aimed at
  mid-market/enterprise businesses in Mexico [clara.com press via pymnts.com, ffnews.com]. At Web
  Summit Rio 2026 Clara also announced upcoming stablecoin-backed corporate cards usable in any
  currency [latamfintech.co].
- June 2026: launched Clara Global, a standalone expense-management product for managing spend in
  any country/currency, its first product built entirely with AI tooling [clara.com/pt-br
  newsroom]. No successor product launch has been announced since.

  the bar," with product managers expected to prototype using AI tools before committing engineering

  automation-first instinct applied internally: the role is asked to "lead and scale a team of AIs,
  maximizing an unlimited token budget to drive exponential output, rather than focusing on
  traditional people management" [greenhouse posting]. This is a notably direct statement of intent

  posting].

  clara careers page], but the AI Business Operations Expert role is explicitly hybrid Mexico City:
  no fixed minimum office days for most roles, but presence is expected "organically" and more so
  during ramp-up [greenhouse posting].

## 6. Engineering & AI direction

- Security/compliance stack: ISO 27001, PCI DSS 4.0, and SOC 2 Type II certified
  [clara.com/platform/security].
- Stated fraud approach is proactive rather than reactive: "AI-powered fraud detection with active
  monitoring and real-time transaction validation" intended to block unauthorized activity before
  it happens, layered with biometric auth (Face ID/fingerprint), dynamic PIN generation, and
  real-time in-app spend guardrails [clara.com/platform/security].
- A separate, previously posted "Developer Experience Engineer (AI DevX)" role showed internal
  platform-engineering direction: building a developer portal (Backstage.io or similar),
  self-service environment provisioning, CI/CD optimization, and observability with "intelligent
  alerting and auto-remediation," explicitly asking the hire to apply ML to anomaly detection,
  capacity planning, and incident classification. Stack signals from that posting: AWS, Kubernetes,
  Docker, GitHub Actions/GitLab CI, Terraform, Python/Bash/Go, Prometheus/Grafana/Datadog
  [generalcatalyst.com AI DevX Engineer posting].
- New in this research pass: Clara has stood up a dedicated internal "AI Business Operations"
  function, reporting to the Global Head of Operations, sitting apart from product engineering.
  Its explicit toolset per the live postings: N8N (named first and repeatedly, effectively the
  primary workflow-automation platform), Make and Zapier as alternates, Metabase for operational
  dashboards and anomaly flagging, Databricks and Snowflake as the data-warehouse layer, HubSpot,
  Salesforce, and Attio as CRM systems of record, Zendesk for support triage, and direct use of
  OpenAI and Anthropic LLM APIs plus Claude Code/Codex as agentic coding tools for building
  automations. Documentation is expected in Google Drive and Slack [greenhouse posting; builtin.com
  sibling AI Business Operations Analyst posting].
- The function is explicitly framed as non-traditional management: the senior AI Business
  Operations Expert is asked to "lead and scale a team of AIs... rather than focusing on
  traditional people management," with an "unlimited token budget" as the stated resource
  constraint instead of headcount [greenhouse posting].
- Clara's own framing of its fastest AI-shipping win (Clara Global, 3 engineers, weeks) plus this
  new internal AI-ops function together are the clearest public signal that both product
  engineering and internal operations leadership are pushing AI-assisted, small-team delivery as
  the default working mode in 2026, not just a marketing line applied after a launch
  [clara.com/pt-br newsroom; greenhouse posting].

## 7. Target role specifics: AI Business Operations Expert (current target, job id 21752)

Per the live posting [greenhouse posting]:
- Reports to the Global Head of Operations; acts as strategic partner to senior leaders in Product,
  Sales, Finance, and Engineering. Owns the full automation lifecycle end to end: spotting the
  opportunity, architecting the solution, deploying it with AI tooling (N8N, Claude Code/Codex,
  LLM APIs), and measuring enterprise-wide impact.
- Core responsibilities: map high-friction operational workflows and prioritize them into a
  compounding automation roadmap; design and deploy agent-based or rule-based automations that
  scale the business without linearly adding headcount; architect Metabase dashboards to surface
  KPIs and flag anomalies before they hit the business; support product launches with automated
  operational readiness; act as Clara's internal expert on AI operations, setting best practices;
  work with Engineering on Databricks pipelines to close data-instrumentation gaps; document
  architectures in Google Drive and Slack.
- Must-haves: bilingual English/Spanish; 7+ years in operations, strategy, or a related field,
  ideally at a LatAm fintech, payments, or high-growth B2B SaaS company (a Bain/BCG/McKinsey or
  high-velocity startup background is also explicitly valued); proven, expert-level, already-shipped
  AI automation track record (explicitly "not aspirational"); advanced hands-on N8N/Make/Zapier
  fluency described as a "strict requirement"; deep familiarity with CRMs (HubSpot/Salesforce),
  support triage (Zendesk), and data warehouses (Databricks/Snowflake); high comfort working
  directly with LLM APIs; strong SQL/Metabase analytical skills; structured strategic thinking
  under ambiguity; strong bilingual technical-to-business communication.
- Nice-to-haves: independent Python analysis skills; a good sense of humor.
- Compensation: not listed in the posting. Benefits stated: competitive salary and ESOP equity from
  day one, a multicultural team working daily across Portuguese/Spanish/English, an annual learning
  budget, flexible vacation, and a hybrid work model (Mexico City) with no fixed minimum office
  days but presence expected "organically," more so during ramp-up.
