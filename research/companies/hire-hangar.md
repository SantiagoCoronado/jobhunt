# Hire Hangar (dossier draft, for Santiago's review)

**Important framing:** Hire Hangar is a recruiting/staffing agency, not a product company. Job id
8372 (Frontend-Focused Full Stack Developer, AI & Data Visualization) is a role Hire Hangar is
sourcing for an unnamed end client ("fast-growing global companies," "leaders," "fast-growing
teams"). Neither the job posting nor Hire Hangar's public site names the specific client for this
role, so the end client is unverifiable and this dossier does not guess at it. Sections below stay
minimal where facts run out rather than padding with generic staffing-industry filler.

## 1. Snapshot
Hire Hangar is an offshore/global staffing and recruiting agency that places pre-vetted remote
talent (engineering, GTM/sales, operations, marketing) with US-based, mostly venture-backed
companies on a flat monthly subscription, no-placement-fee model. Founded by Ashik Akthar,
headquartered in Sacramento, California. Per Tracxn, the company is unfunded (bootstrapped).
Claims 200+ client companies served, 125+ venture-backed teams, and a network of 1,000,000+
operators across 42 countries. (Sources: hirehangar.com, tracxn.com)

## 2. Core narrative
Hire Hangar's pitch is that traditional hiring is "slow, expensive, and focused on resumes rather
than actual talent ability," and it replaces that with an AI-plus-recruiter sourcing pipeline (an
"8-stage vetting process") that gets a vetted, offshore hire started in under 10 days on a
month-to-month subscription with no placement fee. It explicitly positions itself as premium, not
cheap: "we're not the cheaper alternative, we're the proper solution." That is the whole narrative:
speed and quality of offshore hiring as a service, not a product company Santiago would be joining
to build. (Source: hirehangar.com/about)

## 3. Mission & product
- **Service, not product:** Hire Hangar sells recruiting/staffing as a subscription. Client budget
  tiers for engineering roles run 2k to 5k+ USD per month per role, month-to-month, no placement
  fee, with a "7-day hire" promise. (Source: hirehangar.com/categories/engineering-it)
- **Who they place:** Software Engineer, Backend Developer, Frontend Developer, Full Stack
  Developer, Mobile Developer, DevOps, QA, Network/System Administrator, plus GTM/sales,
  operations, and marketing roles. (Source: hirehangar.com/categories/engineering-it)
- **Talent pool:** Sourced primarily from South America, Egypt, and South Africa per third-party
  aggregator Tracxn; Hire Hangar's own site claims a 1M+ operator network across 42 countries.
  (Sources: tracxn.com, hirehangar.com/about)
- **Client logos shown (not confirmed case studies):** the site displays a "trusted by" logo strip
  including names like TikTok, Zendesk, and Airbnb, with no accompanying case study or named
  testimonial explaining the actual engagement, so treat these as brand-recognition marketing, not
  verified client relationships. (Source: hirehangar.com/categories/engineering-it)
- **This specific role (id 8372):** posted as remote, Mexico-based candidate, US time zone
  (EST-PST) overlap, frontend-heavy full stack work: React, AI integrations, data visualization,
  dashboards. The end client is not named in the listing. (Source: local job record, Adzuna
  listing id 5781433265)

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago
Because Hire Hangar is the staffing intermediary, not the employer of record for day-to-day work,
fit angles should speak to what the role itself (frontend-heavy full stack, React, AI integrations,
data visualization, dashboards) asks for, which lines up cleanly with Santiago's two strongest role
families: frontend engineering and internal tooling.

- **Best hook to lead with:** the role wants exactly the shape of work Santiago has shipped
  repeatedly: React/TypeScript-heavy product UI paired with AI features and data visualization.
  Plutus (React 18 + TypeScript + Vite + Tailwind, 11 routes, 45+ components, TradingView-style
  multi-pane charting on lightweight-charts, an ECharts portfolio heatmap) is a near-literal match
  for "modern, scalable product experiences, dashboards, and AI-powered" frontend work. The Hydrel
  energy-operations portal (React Flow node-graph canvas, CFO-grade dashboards, PDF/CSV export) adds
  a second, client-delivered proof point of exactly this pattern at production scale (~30,000 lines,
  a real design system, Vitest and Playwright test suites).
- **Secondary hook:** internal-tooling depth. The Online Basics internal operations platform
  (React front end on AWS, SQL/Python ETL, used daily by operators to run an 8M USD business) shows
  he has built and maintained the kind of internal dashboard tooling this role implies, not just
  demo projects.
- **AI integration angle:** the role explicitly wants "AI integrations." Santiago's LLM workflow
  work (Anthropic/OpenAI API prompt pipelines at Online Basics, the Pragma Brain RAG system, the
  Kairos ElevenLabs voice layer) gives him real, defensible AI-in-product experience beyond
  frontend-only candidates.
- **Honest concerns / watch-outs:**
  - The end client is unnamed and unverifiable. Santiago cannot tailor a cover letter to a specific
    company's mission or product because there is not one to research; any application should be
    honest that it is speaking to the role and to Hire Hangar as the hiring partner, not to a named
    employer.
  - This is a staffing-agency placement, so employment terms (who is the actual employer, contract
    vs. direct hire, benefits, stability) are unclear from the listing and worth clarifying early in
    the process.
  - Salary band from the DB record (18,000 to 36,000 MXN/month, roughly 900 to 1,800 USD/month, or
    about 11k to 22k USD/year) sits well below Santiago's profile.yaml floor of 70,000 USD/year.
    This is a real gap: either the listed range is a placeholder/junior band Hire Hangar posts
    broadly, or the role is genuinely below his target compensation, and it should be confirmed
    directly before investing much more time.

## 8. Application-question angles
- **Why this company:** Lead with the role, not a company mythology that does not exist. Frame it
  around the specific ask (frontend-heavy full stack with AI integrations and data visualization)
  matching his two strongest lanes, and note plainly that Hire Hangar is the hiring partner
  surfacing this opportunity, so the honest answer is "why this role" more than "why this brand."
- **Why you fit:** Anchor on Plutus and the Hydrel portal as concrete, recent, production-grade
  proof of React/TypeScript dashboard and data-visualization work shipped with AI features baked
  in, then point to the Online Basics internal platform as evidence he has done this under real
  operational stakes (an 8M USD business), not just in side projects.
  In other words, lead with C (frontend) and back it up with D (internal tooling), per his own
  self-rated strongest lanes.
- **Anything else:** Worth naming, briefly and non-defensively, that the listed salary band is
  well below his target and asking for clarity on the actual compensation and employer structure
  (agency-managed contract vs. direct placement) before going further, since the public listing
  does not resolve that.
