## 1. Snapshot

EPAM Systems is a global digital engineering and IT consultancy: it builds custom software,
digital platforms, and AI-native systems for enterprise clients rather than selling an
off-the-shelf product. Founded in 1993 by Arkadiy Dobkin and Leo Lozner, headquartered in
Newtown, Pennsylvania, publicly traded on the NYSE since 2012 (ticker EPAM). Large and mature:
2024 full-year revenue of $4.728 billion; roughly 62,850 total employees and 56,650 delivery
professionals as of Q2 2026 (June 30, 2026). Global delivery model with major engineering hubs
across Central/Eastern Europe, and an actively growing Latin America footprint that includes
Mexico.
[Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPAM_Systems),
[EPAM Corporate Overview PDF](https://www.epam.com/content/dam/epam/free_library/EPAM_Corporate_Overview_Q4_EOY.pdf),
[EPAM Q2 2026 results, StockTitan mirror](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/EPAM/epam-reports-results-for-second-quarter-0ooac03qy7gy.html).

## 2. Core narrative

EPAM has spent over 30 years building its identity around deep engineering delivery discipline
(not staff augmentation), and it is now spending that credibility on an explicit, funded bet to
become what its own leadership calls "the leading AI-native strategic service provider": an
internally built agentic software-delivery platform (CodeMie, and the open-source DIAL GenAI
orchestration layer now on its third major release), turned outward in May 2026 into a
multi-year strategic partnership with Anthropic covering Claude models, Claude Code, the Claude
Agent SDK, and Claude Security, with a stated goal of certifying over 10,000 EPAM architects on
Claude. That AI-native push is already showing up in the numbers (AI-native revenue over $160
million in Q2 2026, its sixth straight quarter of double-digit sequential growth) even as the
company's broader growth has slowed. In parallel, EPAM has spent the last five years turning
Latin America, including Mexico, from a small footprint into a real regional delivery hub through
repeated acquisitions (S4N in Colombia in 2021, Vates in Argentina/Chile in 2024), growing LATAM
headcount from roughly 1,500 to 3,000-plus engineers.

[EPAM Vates acquisition, PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/epam-expands-its-reach-in-latin-america-with-acquisition-of-vates-302098623.html),

## 3. Mission & product

- EPAM designs and builds enterprise applications, digital products, platforms, and software
  systems for clients that need tailored solutions rather than off-the-shelf tools, delivering
  through engineering, strategy, design, consulting, education, and innovation services across the
  full product lifecycle.
  [PitchGrade company overview](https://pitchgrade.com/companies/epam-systems).
- Business model: enterprise software engineering services and digital consulting, billed to
  large corporate clients (financial services, healthcare, insurance, consumer/retail, travel,
  and more per its AI-native materials), not a self-serve SaaS product company.
  [PitchGrade company overview](https://pitchgrade.com/companies/epam-systems).
- Its flagship AI product line is DIAL (Deterministic Integrator of Applications and LLMs), an
  open-source GenAI orchestration platform that connects large language models (including AWS
  Bedrock-hosted Anthropic Claude) into client workflows with governance and vendor-lock-in
  avoidance in mind; DIAL 3.0 shipped June 24, 2025, and DIAL was added to the AWS Marketplace AI
  Agents and Tools category in July 2025. It is used by global clients in financial services,
  healthcare, insurance, CPG, and retail to operationalize generative AI.
  [EPAM DIAL 3.0 release, StockTitan mirror](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/EPAM/epam-anthropic-team-up-to-build-the-future-of-enterprise-0kn2ry6z99c2.html),
  [DIAL platform case study, DialX](https://dialx.ai/case-studies/epam-case).
- Financial scale: $4.728 billion full-year 2024 revenue; Q2 2026 (quarter ended June 30, 2026)
  revenue of $1.415 billion, up 4.5% year over year (3.4% organic constant currency); management
  lowered full-year 2026 guidance citing slower North America growth and AI-related contracts
  pushed from H2 2026 into H1 2027.
  [EPAM Q2 2026 results, StockTitan mirror](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/EPAM/epam-reports-results-for-second-quarter-0ooac03qy7gy.html).

## 4. Culture & voice

- Stated values center on engineering excellence/craftsmanship, client focus, continuous
  learning, collaboration ("Act as a Team," cross-regional knowledge sharing across 35-plus
  countries), and integrity; heavy institutional investment in upskilling through EPAM Academy, a
  formal Competency Matrix, certification support, and mentorship with stated advancement
  criteria.
  [Built In: EPAM culture & values](https://builtin.com/company/epam-systems/faq/culture-values).
- Employee-review aggregation (secondary source, not company-authored, treat as directional
  sentiment rather than fact) describes real tradeoffs: strong, structured learning and clear
  career pathways set against workload spikes and inconsistent management on certain client
  engagements, and a risk of feeling disconnected from the wider company when embedded full-time
  at a single client site (a "body shop" dynamic some reviewers cite). Glassdoor's aggregate
  career-opportunities rating from EPAM employees is 4.0 out of 5 (15,026 reviews, as of July
  2026).
  [Built In: EPAM culture & values](https://builtin.com/company/epam-systems/faq/culture-values).
- EPAM Mexico specifically has been recognized as a "preferred employer" and one of the Top 10
  Best Companies for Young Professionals in Mexico; it also runs "EPAM Without Borders," an
  internal relocation program that explicitly includes Mexico as a destination.
  [EPAM LATAM expansion, AiThority mirror](https://aithority.com/technology/analytics/business-intelligence/epam-expands-presence-in-latin-america-enhancing-global-delivery-and-growing-top-engineering-talent-in-the-region/).

## 6. Engineering & AI direction

- EPAM built its own internal AI-native software-delivery platform, CodeMie (part of its "AI/Run"
  product line), an end-to-end SDLC agentic platform for engineers, business analysts, testers,
  PMs, and architects: agent-building tools, multi-agent workflows with a visual designer, and
  knowledge-base integration (indexing Git repos, Jira tickets, Confluence pages, and documents)
  so agents work with real project context, plus native MCP (Model Context Protocol) support and
  integrations with Git, Jira, Confluence, AWS, Azure, GCP, Kubernetes, and SQL databases. A
  documented enterprise case study cites an insurance client cutting migration-analysis cost from
  $75,000 to $550 per instance using CodeMie.
  [CodeMie overview via search summary of epam.com/insights](https://www.epam.com/insights/ai/blogs/codemie-to-enable-ai-native-software-delivery) (blocked to direct fetch, cited via search-result summary; treat as directionally accurate, not verbatim).
- EPAM maintains a genuinely public engineering presence around this stack: the
  `epam/EPAM-AI-RUN-Marketplace` GitHub repository (Apache 2.0 license, actively maintained) ships
  Terraform and Helm infrastructure-as-code for deploying its AI/Run platform on AWS, wired to AWS
  Bedrock with Amazon Nova and Claude 3.5 Sonnet as supported models.
  [github.com/epam/EPAM-AI-RUN-Marketplace](https://github.com/epam/EPAM-AI-RUN-Marketplace).
- DIAL, EPAM's separate open-source GenAI orchestration layer, is explicitly built to plug
  commercial and open models (again including Anthropic Claude via AWS Bedrock) into client
  systems with governance controls, positioned to avoid vendor lock-in; now on version 3.0 (June
  2025) and listed on AWS Marketplace (July 2025).
  [EPAM DIAL 3.0 release, StockTitan mirror](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/EPAM/epam-anthropic-team-up-to-build-the-future-of-enterprise-0kn2ry6z99c2.html).
- The May 2026 Anthropic partnership put Claude Code and the Claude Agent SDK, agentic coding
  tools, at the center of EPAM's forward engineering strategy, backed by a dedicated
  Claude-certified architect practice (10,000-plus target) and 250 specialized "Black Belt"
  engineers deployed directly with clients.
  [EPAM and Anthropic press release, PR Newswire mirror](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/epam--anthropic-team-up-to-build-the-future-of-enterprise-transformation-with-safe-applied-ai-302763463.html).

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago (Frontend Engineer, job id 20089)

Lead hook: **the exact tools EPAM just bet the company on in its May 2026 Anthropic partnership,
Claude Code and the Claude Agent SDK, are tools Santiago has already shipped production work
with, not just read about.** He built Kairos's Claude integration on two surfaces from one
codebase (an in-app agent using the Claude Agent SDK, and a standalone MCP server exposing the
same 52 tools via a typed schema layer), and at Pragma AI he built and operates a hosted,
authenticated MCP "brain" service with per-operator tokens and rate limits over the company's
knowledge vault [resume/master_resume.yaml]. That is a rare, concrete overlap with a company-level
strategic bet rather than a generic "I use AI tools" claim, and it gives an EPAM conversation
something more specific to anchor on than the frontend req alone would.

Second hook, more directly tied to this specific req: **real, shipped React/TypeScript frontend
work at meaningful scale**, which is what the posting actually asks for. Eugenia.ai's React
front end reached 20-plus buildings and 10,000-plus users; Nextia's React apps served 50,000-plus
users; more recently, Plutus is an 11-route, 45-plus component React 18/TypeScript/Vite/Tailwind
app with real-time WebSocket and SSE layers, and the Hydrel client project (co-built at Pragma AI)
is a roughly 30,000-line React 18/TypeScript portal with its own design system and a real
Playwright/Vitest test suite [resume/master_resume.yaml]. Frontend is one of the two role families
Santiago rates as his strongest positioning per his own profile [config/profile.yaml].

Honest gaps and watch-outs:
- The req's tags list Next.js specifically. Santiago's documented frontend stack is React plus
  Vite (Plutus, Kairos's renderer), not Next.js; his resume shows no direct Next.js project. This
  is a real gap to name plainly rather than imply Next.js experience he does not have; React and
  TypeScript fundamentals transfer, but Next.js-specific conventions (App Router, server
  components, etc.) would be new ground.
- EPAM is a large IT-services consultancy (62,850-plus employees), and employee-review
  aggregation flags a real risk of engagement-to-engagement variability: which client account this
  req actually staffs onto will shape day-to-day work far more than EPAM's corporate AI narrative
  does, and that account is unknown from the posting [Built In: EPAM culture & values].
- The job description itself (one sentence, no team, no client, no interview process disclosed)
  is thin; before leaning hard into any EPAM-specific narrative in an interview, it is worth
  confirming with a recruiter which account or practice this req sits in, since "AI-native"
  is a company-level, not confirmed team-level, fact for this exact role.
- No location-eligibility statement is visible in the stored job data beyond "Remote"; EPAM's
