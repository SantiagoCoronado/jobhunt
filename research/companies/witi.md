# WiTi (Innovación y Desarrollo Tecnológico WiTI SpA)

## Snapshot
Chilean IT staffing, software factory, and IT consulting firm founded in 2017, roughly 51 to 200
employees, headquartered in Santiago (Huerfanos 1160), with additional offices in Montevideo,
Uruguay and Bogota, Colombia. Privately held; no public funding rounds found. This role (Full
Stack Next/React, job id 7000) is fully remote and, based on WiTi's business model, is most likely
staffed onto an enterprise retail or ecommerce client project rather than an internal WiTi
product.

## Core narrative
WiTi is not a product company, it is a technology partner and IT staffing/software factory that
embeds engineers into large Chilean and LatAm enterprises (Cencosud, Starbucks, Sodimac, Kaufmann,
Colmena) to build custom software, cloud, and AI/ML solutions. Its public pitch to talent leans
hard on retention and career development ("Talent-Centered Culture") rather than a flashy product
story, and its tagline "Tu desafio tecnologico se transforma en el nuestro" (your tech challenge
becomes ours) signals a service mindset: understand the client's business first, then build.

## Why-fit angles for Santiago
- **Best hook: retail/ecommerce operations engineering.** WiTi's named clients (Cencosud,
  Starbucks, Sodimac) and this posting's explicit retail/ecommerce requirement line up directly
  with Santiago's strongest, most concrete track record: he built and ran Online Basics on Amazon
  and Mercado Libre to $8M USD in revenue, including the internal React/AWS/SQL/Python ETL
  operations platform (pricing dashboard, inventory admin, cross-marketplace matching) used daily
  to drive real-time retail decisions. That is retail/ecommerce domain experience plus internal
  tooling, both in one story, exactly the two role families (frontend, internal tooling) his
  profile marks as strongest.
- **Frontend and full-stack TypeScript match.** The posting's stack (React 18 with Hooks/Context,
  TypeScript across the stack, Zustand/Redux, NestJS microservices, Postgres, AWS Lambda) maps
  closely to his recent build work: Plutus (React 18, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind frontend on a
  FastAPI/Postgres backend with AWS-style service architecture), the Hydrel client portal (React
  18 + TypeScript, ~30k lines, design system, real test suite), and Eugenia.ai (React front end,
  Node.js backend on AWS).
- **ETL and AWS data-pipeline experience.** His Online Basics work orchestrated 10+ third-party
  APIs processing 100K+ daily data points with hourly sync, direct evidence for the "procesos ETL"
  requirement.
- **Honest gaps and watch-outs.** He has no confirmed NestJS or Terraform/CloudFormation IaC
  experience in his resume (the posting marks IaC as excluyente/required); this is a real gap to
  flag rather than paper over. WiTi is a staffing/software factory, meaning the day-to-day team,
  manager, and project could belong to the end client rather than WiTi itself, worth surfacing in
  an interview to understand who he would actually report to and build with. No confirmed salary
  is listed. WiTi's public narrative is retention-and-culture-first rather than product-mission
  driven, so a cover letter should stay grounded in the concrete engineering and domain fit rather
  than trying to invoke a big-vision company story that WiTi has not publicly made.

## Application-question angles
- **Why this company:** Lead with the retail/ecommerce and internal-tooling overlap, not with
  WiTi's mission (which is thin and service-oriented). The honest hook is "I have already built
  the kind of system your clients need," referencing the Online Basics internal operations
  platform and multi-marketplace ETL work, and pivot to being drawn to a role where that domain
  experience plus modern React/TypeScript/AWS skills are directly reusable.
- **Why you fit:** Anchor on the specific stack overlap (React 18, TypeScript end to end, AWS
  Lambda-based services, Postgres, ETL) demonstrated across Online Basics, Plutus, and the Hydrel
  portal, and be upfront that NestJS/Terraform would be a fast ramp rather than existing depth,
  framed as low-risk given the adjacent Node.js and AWS experience already in hand.
- **Anything else:** Worth asking, given WiTi's staffing/software-factory model, which end client
  or internal team this specific role sits on, what the day-to-day reporting line looks like, and
  whether the retail/ecommerce requirement maps to a specific active client engagement. This
  keeps the conversation concrete rather than generic.
