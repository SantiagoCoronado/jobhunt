# GitLab

Draft dossier for Santiago's review. Researched: 2026-08-24.

## 1. Snapshot

GitLab (NASDAQ: GTLB) makes the GitLab DevSecOps platform, a single application spanning
planning, source control, CI/CD, security, and now agentic AI orchestration (GitLab Duo). Public
company, founded 2014 (started as an open-source project in 2011), all-remote since inception
with no physical HQ (nominal San Francisco address for legal/IR purposes). FY2026 (ended Jan
2026) revenue was $955M, up 26% YoY, ARR crossed $1B. Roughly 2,000 employees worldwide after a
14% workforce reduction in mid-2026 (see Recent news).

## 2. Core narrative

GitLab is mid-pivot from "the AI-assisted coding tool" to "the orchestration layer for AI agents
across the whole software lifecycle," and it is restructuring the company itself, not just the
product, to get there: a June 2026 reorg cut 14% of staff, exited about 22 countries, flattened
management, and reorganized engineering into 60 teams, with the CEO framing it explicitly as
reinvestment in "the agentic era," not cost-cutting
([TechCrunch, 2026-06-03](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/03/gitlab-cuts-14-of-staff-as-it-scales-its-platform-to-serve-ai-workloads/)).
In parallel, GitLab has made internal AI adoption a formal, staffed program: every major function
(Product & Design, Marketing, the field/CRO org) now has a dedicated "AI Transformation Owner"
role paired with a central Enterprise AI team, explicitly modeled as a product-management job
where "the product is your org's way of working"
([Greenhouse posting, accessed 2026-08-24](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/gitlab/jobs/8716179002)).
That combination, an all-remote company known for radical transparency now formally
institutionalizing AI transformation function by function while cutting headcount and country
footprint, is the distinctive story here.

## 3. Mission & product

- GitLab's mission: "enable everyone to contribute to and co-create the software that powers our
  world" ([Comparably summary of GitLab handbook mission page](https://www.comparably.com/companies/gitlab/mission), accessed 2026-08-24).
- Product: the GitLab DevSecOps platform, one application for planning, source code management,
  CI/CD, application security testing (SAST/DAST/secret detection/SCA), and value stream
  management, used by 50M+ registered users and more than 50% of the Fortune 100
  ([Greenhouse posting boilerplate](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/gitlab/jobs/8716179002), accessed 2026-08-24).
- Business model: tiered subscriptions (Free/Premium/Ultimate) plus GitLab Flex, a newer annual
  commitment model that bundles seat licenses with usage-based AI credits so customers can shift
  spend between seats and AI usage without renegotiating contracts ([about.gitlab.com](https://about.gitlab.com/), accessed 2026-08-24).
  GitLab Ultimate is now 53% of total ARR ([Yahoo Finance, Q2 FY2026 earnings recap, 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/gitlab-inc-gtlb-q2-2026-070335039.html)).
- Customers: enterprise and public-sector engineering orgs (financial services, telecom,
  automotive, aerospace, education called out as named verticals on the site)
  ([about.gitlab.com](https://about.gitlab.com/), accessed 2026-08-24).

## 4. Culture & voice

- All-remote since founding, and famous for it: the ~2,000-page public handbook documents every
  process and policy, and GitLab actively publishes remote-work playbooks for other companies
  ([GitLab's Guide to All-Remote](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/company/culture//all-remote/guide/), accessed 2026-08-24).
- Values are the "CREDIT" set: Collaboration, Results for Customers, Efficiency, Diversity
  Inclusion & Belonging, Iteration, Transparency
  ([GitLab Values handbook page](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/values/), accessed 2026-08-24).
  Collaboration is described internally as "short toes" (nobody can step on them because
  collaboration is always welcome); Iteration is framed as "you're doing it right if you're
  slightly embarrassed by the minimal feature set shipped in the first iteration"
  ([search summary of GitLab values handbook, corroborated against the handbook TOC above](https://slab.com/blog/short-toes-how-gitlabs-values-shape-company-documentation/), accessed 2026-08-24).
- Transparency is handbook-first by default: internal docs, process, even much of engineering
  planning are public.
- Current internal AI mandate, stated directly in job postings: "we embrace AI as a core
  productivity multiplier, with all team members expected to incorporate AI into their daily
  workflows" ([Greenhouse posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/gitlab/jobs/8716179002), accessed 2026-08-24).

## 6. Engineering & AI direction

- GitLab Duo Agent Platform (GA January 2026) is the flagship product bet: agentic chat,
  pre-built "foundational agents" (Planner, Security Analyst), a catalog for building custom
  agents, and integrations with external coding agents (Claude Code, Codex CLI), plus governance
  (model selection, group-based access control, usage visibility)
  ([IR press release](https://ir.gitlab.com/news/news-details/2026/GitLab-Announces-the-General-Availability-of-GitLab-Duo-Agent-Platform/default.aspx), accessed 2026-08-24).
- The company describes a "generational rebuild of git" underway to handle machine-scale agent
  traffic, per the CEO's June 2026 restructuring memo
  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/03/gitlab-cuts-14-of-staff-as-it-scales-its-platform-to-serve-ai-workloads/), accessed 2026-08-24).
- Internally, GitLab runs a formal cross-functional AI transformation program: a central
  "Enterprise AI" team (inside an "Enterprise Technology & AI" org) provides technical delivery,
  platforms, and patterns, while each function gets an embedded "AI Transformation Owner" plus a
  partnered AI Engineer. Building happens at the no-code/low-code layer (tools named in the
  posting: Glean, Workato), with MCP servers connecting agents to business systems and "skills
  files" defining agent behavior ([Greenhouse posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/gitlab/jobs/8716179002), accessed 2026-08-24).

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago

**Best hook to lead with**: GitLab has turned internal AI adoption into a structured,
product-managed discipline (embedded owner plus a central AI Engineer, no-code build layer, MCP,
Champion network for adoption) rather than a top-down mandate. That is almost exactly the shape of
what Santiago has already built and run at Pragma AI: he co-built a hosted, authenticated MCP
"brain" service over the company knowledge vault, defined 15 agent definitions and 10 reusable
routines, ran an adoption-blocker analysis that reshaped onboarding, and treated teammates as
internal users of an AI system he owned end to end. He has also shipped this exact "AI engineer
partners with a business owner" split himself: Kairos exposes 52 tools through both an in-app
Claude Agent SDK agent and a standalone MCP server, and Plutus wraps an LLM research agent behind
an MCP control plane. That is direct, credible, hands-on evidence for a role that explicitly wants
a "power user, not a software engineer" who can configure MCP servers, author skills files, and
run a Champion network, not a coder.

Supporting angles:
- Founder background (Online Basics, Eugenia.ai) gives him real stakeholder management and
  0-to-1 product ownership, which maps to the role's "product management mindset" requirement
  (intake, backlog, prioritization, adoption) and its need to build a peer Champion network.
- His internal-tooling work (Online Basics' internal ops platform used daily by 4 operators;
  Kairos as a personal command center) is direct precedent for owning "your org's way of working"
  as the product, the framing the posting itself uses.
- He is already fluent in the tools the role names as its stack: MCP, prompt engineering, RAG,
  no-code/low-code agent building, and daily use of Claude Code and Cursor.

**Honest concerns / watch-outs**:
- **Location is a real problem.** The canonical Greenhouse posting lists this specific role as
  "Remote, Canada; Remote, United Kingdom; Remote, United States" only, and the salary range is
  explicitly scoped to US residents
  ([Greenhouse posting header and comp section](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/gitlab/jobs/8716179002), accessed 2026-08-24).
  This contradicts GitLab's general "all-remote, hires worldwide" branding and the boilerplate
  "Country Hiring Guidelines" text at the bottom of the same posting, which says GitLab "hires new
  team members in countries around the world." For this specific req, the top-of-page location
  tag is the more reliable signal (Greenhouse location tags gate which countries a candidate can
  select in the application form) and it does not include Mexico. Santiago lives in Mexico City
  and will not relocate; per `config/profile.yaml`'s location_rule this is a dealbreaker unless a
  recruiter confirms otherwise during screening. Worth a direct, early question rather than
  assuming eligibility. Note the internal job-matcher scored this "location_ok: true" based on the
  aggregator mirror, which does not surface this restriction, so that score should be treated as
  stale pending confirmation.
- GitLab is mid-restructuring: a 14% headcount cut and exit from about 22 countries landed only
  weeks before this posting, which raises real questions about near-term hiring stability and
  whether newly created "AI Transformation Owner" roles like this one could be reorganized again.
  Worth asking about directly.
- The role is explicitly non-engineering ("power user, not software engineer"); Santiago's
  strongest resume evidence is engineering-heavy, so the application should lean on the product
  and adoption angles above rather than on coding depth.

## 8. Application-question angles

- **Why this company**: Lead with GitLab's move to formalize AI transformation as an embedded,
  product-managed function (the AI Transformation Owner plus Enterprise AI pairing), not a
  generic "GitLab is a great remote culture" answer. Tie it to the same pattern Santiago already
  runs at Pragma AI: an owned MCP brain service, a partnered technical build layer, and a
  Champion-style adoption process.
- **Why you fit**: Anchor on the Pragma AI MCP brain and the Kairos/Plutus MCP work as proof he
  has already done the specific job (own an internal AI system end to end, partner with technical
  delivery, drive adoption), not a general AI-engineer pitch. Mention the founder-era stakeholder
  and 0-to-1 experience only as secondary support.
- **Anything else**: If asked, raise the location question directly and factually (confirm
  whether the role is open to Mexico-based candidates given the Canada/UK/US tag on the posting)
  rather than letting a location mismatch surface later in the process.
