## 2. Core narrative
Caylent built its business as an all-in AWS consulting shop and is now racing to become the
default delivery partner for enterprises trying to put Claude and other foundation models into
production, not just experiment with them. It holds "charter member" status in Anthropic's Claude
Partner Network and launched a dedicated business unit, Anthropic Consulting & Engineering (ACE),
in April 2026, explicitly split into an "Agentic SDLC" track (embedding Claude Code across
engineering teams) and an "Applied AI" track (forward-deployed engineers building Claude-powered
products for clients). The same thesis, that AI agents should do real delivery work under expert
oversight rather than just assist individuals, shows up in its internal tooling too: "Caylent
Accelerate," built on Bedrock, AWS Transform, and a proprietary agentic orchestration framework,
runs coordinated "agentic fleets" against modernization and CloudOps work, and the company claims
it expedites 70 percent of remediation work and cuts MTTR by 40 percent. Backed by private equity
since 2022 and actively rolling up complementary AWS shops (Trek10 for managed services, Pronetx
for AWS-native contact center/CX), Caylent is deliberately building toward being the single,
full-lifecycle AWS-plus-AI partner rather than staying a point solution, and a June 2026
multi-year AWS Strategic Collaboration Agreement doubled down on that bet with direct AWS
investment behind agentic AI, Amazon Connect CX, and next-gen managed services.

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago (AI/ML Engineering Manager, Greenhouse 5985809004)
**Best hook to lead with:** the role is explicitly dual-scope, senior technical authority on
client AI/ML engagements plus real people management (hiring, 1:1s, performance calibration,
staffing), inside a consulting delivery model. Santiago has run both halves at small scale
already: as co-founder of Pragma AI he was the senior technical voice on client AI engagements
(RAG systems, agentic tooling, deployment) while also leading and staffing a team, and at Online
Basics he led a 7-person team end to end (hiring, workflows, incident response) while owning the
technical architecture of the company's internal platform. The job posting's own language,
"comprehensive ML assessments across infrastructure, data pipelines, and organizational
readiness" and "translate complexity into executable standards," is close to what Santiago has
already done client-side as a consultant, not just as an internal engineer.
[resume/master_resume.yaml], [Job posting (Greenhouse 5985809004)](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/caylent/jobs/5985809004)

- **Forward-deployed, client-facing AI delivery**: Pragma AI is a B2B AI consultancy where
  Santiago built and delivered tailor-made AI solutions (RAG "company brain," conversational
  agents, process automation) directly for client data and workflows, the same forward-deployed
  posture ACE's Applied AI track and this role's "lead complex customer engagements" mandate
  describe. [resume/master_resume.yaml]
- **Agentic systems and MCP, not just LLM calls**: Santiago built a hosted, authenticated MCP
  "brain" service (per-operator tokens, rate limits) exposing 15 agent definitions and 10 reusable
  routines at Pragma AI, and a standalone MCP control plane plus an LLM research agent in Plutus.
  The JD explicitly lists "agentic systems" and RAG as core foundation-model competencies, and
  Caylent's own AI practice (ACE, Caylent Accelerate) is built around agent orchestration, so this
  is a direct architectural match, not just keyword overlap. [resume/master_resume.yaml]
- **AWS-native background**: Online Basics' internal platform and Plutus both run on AWS-adjacent
  infrastructure and cloud-hosted services; this is real but should not be oversold as deep AWS ML
  specialty depth (see gap below), so frame it as a credible starting point rather than mastery.
  [resume/master_resume.yaml]
- **People leadership inside a technical role, not despite it**: led a 7-person team at Online
  Basics with git-based workflows, code review, and incident response; the JD wants a manager who
  is "both deeply technical and accountable for the growth and performance of a team," which maps
  to how Santiago already frames his founder background (Family B in his target profile: AI
  deployment/enablement roles at director/manager scope). [resume/master_resume.yaml],
  [config/profile.yaml]

**Honest concerns / watch-outs:**
- **The JD's bar is 10+ years in ML/AI with client-facing consulting seniority, and deep expertise
  in 2 to 3 specific ML domains (classical ML, computer vision, NLP, time series)**. Santiago's
  strength is applied LLM/agentic engineering and full-stack product delivery, not classical ML,
  CV, or time series depth; he should be honest in interviews that his ML domain depth is
  concentrated in generative/agentic AI rather than broad across the traditional ML landscape.
- **No formal AWS ML Specialty or Solutions Architect certification**, which the posting calls out
  as a "strong differentiator." This is a real, nameable gap, not disqualifying since it is listed
  as a plus rather than a requirement.
- **His people-management scale (7 direct reports at Online Basics, small co-founder team at
  Pragma AI) is smaller than what a Director-of-AI/ML-reporting engineering manager at a
  multi-hundred-person consultancy likely expects**; worth naming as a genuine step up in scope
  rather than implying equivalent scale.
- **No named track record of formal pre-sales / proposal-scoping work inside a large consulting
  organization**; his client and stakeholder work has been founder-led and informal by comparison,
  which is a legitimate process gap to acknowledge.

## 8. Application-question angles
- **Why this company**: Lead with the Core narrative, Caylent is not just an AWS shop that added
  an AI practice, it built a dedicated business unit (ACE) with its own forward-deployed engineer
  model specifically for Claude, backed by charter-member status in Anthropic's Claude Partner
  Network and its own internally-built agentic delivery engine (Caylent Accelerate). Frame
  Santiago's interest as wanting to bring hands-on RAG and agentic-systems experience into a firm
  that is actively betting its next phase of growth on exactly that kind of production AI
  delivery, not a generic "I like AWS" answer.
- **Why you fit**: Anchor on the dual technical-plus-people scope of the role. Santiago has
  already been the senior technical voice on client AI engagements (Pragma AI's RAG brain and MCP
  agent infrastructure) while leading a team (7-person team at Online Basics, co-founder team at
  Pragma AI), which mirrors this role's split between hands-on architecture and people management
  inside client-facing delivery. Be direct that his ML domain depth is concentrated in generative
  and agentic AI, and name that as the specific value he adds to a team, not a universal ML
  generalist claim.
- **Anything else**: Use this slot for the honest gaps rather than more selling: no AWS ML
  Specialty or Solutions Architect certification yet, and people-management experience at a
  smaller scale than a multi-hundred-person consultancy's engagements. Point to a track record of
  closing unfamiliar-domain gaps fast (picking up Swift and Java at Nextia.mx, standing up AWS
  infrastructure and 10+ third-party API integrations at Online Basics, building the MCP agent
  layer at Pragma AI from scratch) as evidence he would close these gaps quickly on the job.
