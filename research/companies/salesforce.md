# Salesforce, AI Engineer / Forward Deployed Engineer (Mexico City)

Target job: [AI Engineer - Forward Deployed Engineer (Multiple Levels), Mexico City](https://careers.salesforce.com/en/jobs/jr346848/ai-engineer-forward-deployed-engineer-multiple-levels/)
(jobhunt id 8027, posted 2026-06-18, source Adzuna)

## Core narrative

Salesforce is converting itself from a CRM vendor into an "Agentic Enterprise" platform: Agentforce
(autonomous AI agents), Data 360 (formerly Data Cloud, the unified real-time data layer), and
Einstein now sit at the center of the company's pitch, and Agentforce hit 1.2 billion USD in ARR
in Q1 FY27, up 205 percent year over year (source: Salesforce Q4 FY26 earnings coverage,
see Sources). To actually get enterprise customers from pilot to production, Salesforce created a
brand-new internal role, the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE): a hands-on coder embedded with one
client at a time (often on-site) who builds and ships a working agent rather than configuring a
generic product. FDE job postings grew over 800 percent between January and September 2025 and
Salesforce has publicly committed to building a team of 1,000 FDEs, while regular software
engineering hiring has been effectively frozen (CEO Marc Benioff said in 2025 that the company
does not plan to hire more engineers, crediting AI-driven productivity gains, with headcount
holding near 15,000 engineers). This specific CDMX opening sits at the intersection of both
storylines: Salesforce is throwing headcount at the one engineering function it still wants to grow
(FDE), and it just built a 2,000-person, 192,000-square-foot flagship LatAm office and Global
Delivery Center in Polanco, Mexico City (opened November 2025) as part of a 1 billion USD,
five-year Mexico investment plan.

## Product

- **Agentforce**: autonomous AI agents that act across the CRM lifecycle (lead qualification, case
  routing, opportunity management, customer service, SDR outreach) without constant human
  intervention; closed 5,000 deals in its first 90 days, 3,000+ paid, the fastest ramp of any
  Salesforce product to date.
- **Data 360** (renamed from Data Cloud as part of "Agentforce 360"): the real-time data engine
  that unifies customer and external data (including zero-copy links to Snowflake and Databricks)
  into a single profile that gives agents live context.
- **Einstein**: the underlying predictive/generative AI layer.
- Salesforce just signed a definitive agreement (June 15, 2026) to acquire Fin (formerly Intercom)
  for roughly 3.6 billion USD to add packaged customer-agent offerings alongside Agentforce's
  customizable platform, and closed the 8 billion USD acquisition of Informatica in November 2025
  to add data catalog, governance, and MDM as the "data foundation for agentic AI."
- Business model: enterprise SaaS/subscription CRM plus a fast-growing agent/data consumption
  layer (Data Cloud and AI ARR reached 900 million USD in FY25, up 120 percent year over year).

## Scale

- FY2026 (ended Jan 31, 2026): 41.5 billion USD revenue (+10 percent YoY), 83,334 employees
  (+9 percent YoY), ~20 percent operating margin, 14.4 billion USD free cash flow.
- Engineering headcount has been roughly flat near 15,000 for about two years by Benioff's own
  account, a deliberate AI-substitution strategy, while sales headcount is growing (1,000 to 2,000
  new sales hires) and FDE is the named exception on the technical side.
- Mexico City: operating since 2006; committed to growing the Mexico City workforce 50 percent by
  January 2025 and hit that ahead of schedule; new Polanco office (opened Nov 2025) is Salesforce's
  largest LatAm office, capacity for up to 2,000 employees, and houses the company's second Global
  Delivery Center (the first GDC opened in Mexico City in 2024).

## Tech stack (from the job posting itself)

Required: Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, Java, or Apex (Apex is one option among several, not a
hard requirement); hands-on LLM and prompt-engineering experience; strong data modeling, REST
APIs, and integration-pattern experience; 3+ years of software engineering or technical-delivery
experience with production systems; prior customer-facing technical delivery (consulting,
professional services, or similar); a CS degree or related field; about 25 percent travel.

AI/dev tooling named in the posting: **Cursor, Claude, LangChain, LlamaIndex**. Data platforms:
Salesforce Data 360, Snowflake, Databricks, BigQuery. Nice-to-have: Salesforce certifications
(Administrator, Platform Developer I, Agentforce Specialist), agent-framework and cloud
data-platform experience.

Platform-wide, Salesforce shipped a native MCP (Model Context Protocol) client in Agentforce
starting with the July 2025 release, plus an enterprise MCP server registry and MCP support inside
AgentExchange, so agents can call external tools/servers through the same open protocol rather than
bespoke integrations.

## Culture

- Salesforce's own framing of the FDE role: "master builders or master fixers" who sit side by
  side with a customer, often embedded full time on one account for about three months, coding
  live, translating agentic AI into a working solution, and reporting field/platform gaps back to
  product teams. Problem-solving and customer communication are called out as the top two traits,
  ahead of any single tech stack.
- Public FDE Glassdoor-style signal from the company itself: framed as a hybrid of "tech guru,
  business consultant, and hand-holder," explicitly positioned as sitting between the product and
  professional-services orgs rather than inside a traditional back-office engineering ladder.
- Mexico City employees rate the company 4.5 out of 5 on Glassdoor (65 reviews) with compensation
  and benefits rated 4.8 out of 5.
- Company-wide, Salesforce's long-running public identity is built around Trailblazer branding and
  an "Ohana" (family) culture language; this dossier does not further verify those claims beyond
  what is directly relevant to the FDE role, per the instruction to stay focused rather than
  recount generic corporate history.
