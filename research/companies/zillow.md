# Zillow

## 1. Snapshot
Zillow Group is a public (Nasdaq: Z / ZG), Seattle-headquartered real estate marketplace founded in 2006 by Rich Barton and Lloyd Frink, the most-visited real estate platform in the U.S. (Wikipedia; Fortune). It runs Premier Agent advertising, Zillow Showcase, rentals, and a mortgage arm. Q2 2026 revenue was up 18 percent year over year to USD 772 million, above guidance, with adjusted EBITDA of USD 176 million (23 percent margin) and adjusted net income of USD 118 million (Zillow Q2 2026 earnings coverage, Yahoo Finance). The company cut about 200 roles in January 2026 and then a further 500+ roles (about 7 percent of staff) in August 2026 as part of a stated cost-discipline push against a flat housing market (GeekWire; Fast Company). It has run a Mexico City engineering hub since 2023 (Milenio; Zillow careers site); as of September 2026 the hub still has around 10 live engineering reqs open on Zillow's Workday board, including this Senior AI-Native Product Engineer, Full Stack role (Zillow_Group_External Workday board, direct check 2026-09-01).

## 2. Core narrative
Zillow is a profitable, growing public "housing super app" (Fortune) that is mid-way through two things at once: pushing AI into daily engineering work company-wide (CTO David Beitel describes engineers using Cursor and Claude for coding for over two years, and 4,600+ internally-built AI agents on an enterprise Glean/ChatGPT stack, Fortune, Feb 2026) and running a real, ongoing engineering center of gravity in Mexico City led by Julio Saenz (who previously built Lyft's Mexico City office) as Senior Director of Engineering and Mexico Country Lead (TheOrg; Karat). This specific role sits on the Touring Buyer Experience (TBEX) team, a cross-platform product engineering team that owns the end-to-end home-touring flow across web, iOS, and Android and describes itself as "deeply AI-forward in how we build with standard AI-assisted coding across the team" (Zillow Workday posting, req P750225). The pitch is joining a team that has already normalized AI-assisted, cross-stack engineering as the day-to-day default, inside a Mexico hub that is a real, growing site rather than a token satellite, at a company that just posted double-digit growth even while trimming headcount elsewhere.

## 3. Mission & product
- Real estate marketplace connecting buyers, sellers, renters, agents, and lenders; "reimagining how people move through the real estate market" (Fortune, July 2025; Wikipedia).
- Revenue drivers by Q2 2026: For Sale revenue up 14 percent YoY to USD 549 million, Residential up 7 percent to USD 465 million, Mortgages up 75 percent to USD 84 million (95 percent growth in purchase loan origination volume, USD 2.2 billion), and Rentals up 31 percent to USD 209 million, with multifamily rentals growing 42 percent YoY (Yahoo Finance, Zillow Q2 2026 earnings coverage).
- February 2025: paid Redfin USD 100 million to become the exclusive multifamily (25+ unit) rental-listings provider on Redfin, Rent.com, and ApartmentGuide.com, expanding the Zillow Rentals Network; this triggered roughly 450 Redfin role cuts and drew a September 2025 FTC antitrust suit plus a coordinated multi-state lawsuit in October 2025 (GeekWire; PRNewswire/Zillow investor release; Wikipedia).
- Also facing a June 2025 Compass antitrust suit over Zillow's 24-hour listing-exclusivity policy and a September 2025 class action alleging steering toward affiliated agents (Wikipedia). No source found reporting resolution of any of these suits as of September 2026; treat them as still active/unresolved unless confirmed otherwise.
- Discontinued its climate-risk scoring feature in November 2025 after agent complaints about unsellable flagged listings (Wikipedia); an example of Zillow pulling back a feature under commercial pressure.

## 4. Mexico City engineering presence and the TBEX role (key facts)
- Zillow opened its Mexico engineering center in 2023, explicitly to attract Mexican technical talent without requiring relocation; company messaging frames it as a cloud-based, distributed org where "all collaborators have the freedom to work where they are most productive: from home, from an office, or a combination of both" (Milenio, "Zillow abre centro en México para nuevos talentos").
- Led by Julio Saenz, Senior Director of Engineering and Mexico Country Lead, who previously built and led Lyft's Mexico City site before joining Zillow (TheOrg; Karat, "Hiring Engineers in Latin America: Insights From Etsy, PayPal, Pinterest, and Zillow"; Shorestories podcast episode referencing him as "the man who started the Mexico City offices of Lyft and Zillow").
- As of a direct check of Zillow's Workday external board on 2026-09-01, roughly 10 Mexico City engineering reqs are live, including this Senior AI-Native Product Engineer, Full Stack role (req P750225, posted 2026-08-29) alongside a Remote-USA twin of the same title (req P750295), confirming Zillow splits the same role into a Mexico-hired req and a US-hired req rather than treating Mexico as an afterthought.
- This role's team: the Touring Buyer Experience (TBEX) team owns the end-to-end touring experience across web, iOS, and Android as a cross-platform product engineering team with shared ownership of the customer experience, helping buyers move from intent to booking and completing a home tour (Zillow Workday posting, req P750225).
- Notable inconsistency in the posting's own text: despite the "Full Stack" title, the qualifications section of req P750225 is written for a mobile product builder ("significant experience building and shipping production mobile applications on iOS and/or Android," "depth in one mobile platform and working familiarity with the other"), matching almost verbatim the separate "Mobile" variant of this same job family (Builtin listing for the Mobile role; Zillow Workday posting, req P750225). Worth a direct question to the recruiter about whether this specific CDMX req is actually mobile-leaning or whether the JD was templated incorrectly.
- Role is a teleworker position: employees may live anywhere in Mexico, with availability to travel to Mexico City for occasional recommended office events (Zillow Workday posting, req P750225), matching the arrangement already confirmed for the Senior Full Stack role (job 7769) Santiago applied to in July 2026.
- No compensation figures are listed on the Mexico posting itself (unlike the US Remote twin, which lists USD 152,900 to 257,100 depending on state cost tier); Zillow Mexico pay is set separately (Zillow Workday posting, req P750225; Builtin listing for the US Full Stack req).
- Comp benchmark for the level this role likely maps to: Levels.fyi reports Zillow P4 (senior) software engineers in Mexico at a median total compensation around MXN 1.6 million per year (base around MXN 1.39 million, plus RSU stock and bonus), versus roughly MXN 1.33 million total for P3 (Levels.fyi, Zillow Software Engineer salaries, Mexico, accessed 2026-09-01). Treat as a directional benchmark, not a guaranteed number for this specific req.
- Interview process signal from a Mexico City Fullstack Engineer candidate (April 2025): two CodeSignal-based online assessments, one backend (database query/filtering task) and one extended frontend React exercise (building a form-driven app against integrated tests); the candidate rated it a positive experience overall and specifically called out "good salary," "good benefits," "100 percent remote," and "great teammates," while also noting weaker follow-up and "useless 1:1 reviews" as concerns (JoinTaro, Zillow Fullstack Engineer interview experience, Ciudad de Mexico, April 1 2025).
- Separate employee-review signal (anecdotal, one data point): a former Senior Software Engineer III reported that "cultural equity doesn't feel consistent for teams in Mexico (less voice/visibility in decisions)" and cited micromanagement from some managers (JoinTaro, Zillow Mobile Engineer interview experience, Ciudad de Mexico, January 10 2025).
- No source found reporting Mexico-office-specific layoff or hiring-freeze impact from either the January 2026 or August 2026 cuts; the August round's only location detail found is that 91 of the 500+ cuts were in Seattle (GeekWire, Aug 2026 coverage). This role posted fresh (2026-08-29), five days after the August 4 layoff announcement, which is a positive signal that Mexico hiring did not pause alongside the U.S. cuts.

## 7. Engineering & AI direction
- Core web/mobile-web stack historically documented for the Mexico org: ES6, React, TypeScript, GraphQL for web/mobile-web; Python, Java, Node.js, and Go for backend/services (job posting text, job 7769, Zillow's own prior description of the stack). This specific TBEX posting (req P750225) does not restate a stack list, focusing instead on product-engineering behaviors and AI-assisted development; treat the GraphQL/Go detail as still representative of the Mexico org generally but not confirmed for this specific team.
- Company-wide AI-native push, per CTO David Beitel (Fortune, Feb 2026): Cursor and Claude used for coding for "over two years," enterprise ChatGPT access for all employees via a Glean-governed environment, 4,600+ employee-built internal AI agents, and a stated push to use AI "upfront" in task design rather than as an afterthought.
- This role's team explicitly describes itself as "deeply AI-forward... with standard AI-assisted coding across the team, shipping across all parts of the stack with on-call and cross-stack ownership," and lists "practical experience using AI-assisted development tools... in day-to-day work while maintaining accountability for quality, correctness, and customer experience" as an explicit qualification (Zillow Workday posting, req P750225). This "AI-Native Product Engineer" title now spans at least three variants found in research (TBEX/Mobile, TBEX/Full Stack in Mexico City, and a Metro-team Full Stack variant in the US), suggesting it is a deliberate, company-wide engineering title change rather than a one-off posting.
- Customer-facing AI bets described in press: computer-vision models generating 3D walkthroughs and AI-enabled virtual staging for listings, machine-learning-generated "super listings" from photos/panoramas, and AI tools aimed at real-estate agents, part of a stated "housing super app" strategy to touch every step of a transaction (Techbrew, Aug 2025; Fortune, July 2025).
- The Zestimate (Zillow's original AI/ML product) reported a median error rate of 1.8 percent for on-market homes and 7.2 percent for off-market homes in 2025 (Techbrew/Fortune coverage, via search summary).
- Public engineering blog ("Zillow Tech Hub" on Medium; zillow.com/tech) covers software engineering, AI/ML and research, data and analytics, and engineering culture, indicating an active, external-facing engineering communications practice (medium.com/zillow-tech-hub).
