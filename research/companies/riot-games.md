# Riot Games

Draft dossier for Santiago's review. Researched: 2026-09-02.

## Core narrative

Riot is a "player first" studio built and sustained by League of Legends, VALORANT, and Teamfight Tactics, now trimming a decade of side bets (2XKO team cut roughly in half, publishing division cut again, Riot Forge shut down) to refocus on those core live services (gamedeveloper.com, sheepesports.com, both accessed 2026-09-02). Inside that leaner company, AI is one of the few areas actively growing: a new Head of Experiential AI role, a Central Product AI/ML product line, and this Principal Software Engineer AI Enablement seat, all created in 2026 to push generative AI and LLM adoption into live game teams (pcgamesn.com; riotgames.com job posting).

## Mission & product

Riot Games, founded 2006, headquartered in Los Angeles, organizes around a "Player First" mission and operates seven live titles: League of Legends (MOBA), VALORANT (FPS), Teamfight Tactics (auto battler), League of Legends: Wild Rift (mobile), Legends of Runeterra (card game), 2XKO (fighting game), and Riftbound (physical trading card game) (riotgames.com/en/who-we-are). The company has over 4,500 employees across 20+ offices worldwide (riotgames.com/en/who-we-are). Revenue is driven by free to play live service games monetized through cosmetics and esports, not by unit sales; R&D is run as a unified department where engineers move between projects rather than being laid off when a title is cancelled, and a "core tech team" maintains shared technology used across every project stage (riotgames.com/en/r-and-d-office/engineering-in-riot-r-d).

## Recent news (last ~12 months)

- 2026-02-09: Riot laid off roughly 80 people, about half the global 2XKO team, after the fighting game's post-launch momentum did not reach the level needed to sustain a team that size; Riot said it would keep supporting the title with a smaller, focused team (gamedeveloper.com/gameinformer, via sheepesports.com and gamedeveloper.com).
- 2026-02-25/26: A second, smaller round cut about 12 roles across three publishing teams in multiple offices, on top of "team by team" changes elsewhere (gamedeveloper.com).
- Since 2023, Riot has cut over 600 roles total, shut down Riot Forge (its third party publishing arm) and the Riot Esports Network, and is narrowing focus to League of Legends, VALORANT, and Teamfight Tactics as its main earners (blog.udonis.co, cross-checked against the gamedeveloper.com reporting above).
- 2026-06-09: Brendan Mulligan, a former Director of Corporate Development, returned to Riot as Head of Experiential AI to build "wild and weird" generative AI player experiences (an AI dungeon master concept was named as an example) across League and VALORANT; he said the goal is new experiences "that wouldn't have been possible pre-AI" without disrupting Riot's creative culture, and that he would "fire himself" if AI initiatives damaged the core games (pcgamesn.com/ai/riot-games-player-experiences).
- 2026 (undated, live posting as of 2026-09-02): Central Product is actively hiring multiple AI roles at once, including this Principal Software Engineer, AI Enablement seat and a separate Principal Technical Product Manager, AI - Central Product role, both reporting into AI leadership rather than a single game team (riotgames.com job board).

## Culture & hiring signals

- In office approach: Riot calls itself "an in-person company that believes in the value of hybrid flexibility." There is no single company-wide office mandate; individual leaders set the rhythm for their team, with the general guidance being in office "at least a few times a month" and senior leaders held to a higher bar (riotgames.com/en/work-with-us/in-office-approach). For an LA-based Principal role this likely means a real, if not daily, in-office cadence.
- Relocation: the same page states relocation support "may be available depending on the role and hiring circumstances," to be confirmed by the recruiter for the specific situation. It does not promise relocation for every hire (riotgames.com/en/work-with-us/in-office-approach).
- Location residency rule: Riot states employees must live in the same state (or country, for non-US offices) as their home office for tax purposes, though travel into the office when needed is expected. This confirms the role is genuinely on-site in Los Angeles, not remote-from-Mexico (riotgames.com/en/work-with-us/in-office-approach).
- Visa sponsorship: the in-office-approach page does not mention visa sponsorship at all. Third-party aggregator data (not a primary Riot source, so treated as directional only) reports Riot sponsored 83 visas in a recent year across H-1B, E-3, and EB-2/EB-3 categories, and separate Riot job postings for Sydney explicitly mention visa sponsorship for that office (migratemate.co; myvisajobs.com; both unverified against a primary Riot statement). Whether Riot files TN sponsorship for a Mexico-based hire specifically should be confirmed directly with the recruiter; nothing in the primary source material rules it out or in for this role.
- Values: five stated values are player focus, seriousness about games, delivering on ambitions, long-term commitment, and organizational unity, publicly framed around "Player First" (riotgames.com/en/who-we-are).

## Target role: Principal Software Engineer, AI Enablement, Central Product (Los Angeles)

Source: JD pulled via `src.query job 40698`, original posting at riotgames.com/en/work-with-us/job/7872902.

- Team shape: an "elite, highly mobile strike team" inside Central Product that embeds with any Riot team to accelerate adoption of generative AI, LLMs, and reinforcement learning, then hands off and moves to the next team. The role reports to Riot's Head of AI Product (title as stated in the JD; not independently confirmed to be the same person as the Head of Experiential AI named in the June 2026 press coverage above).
- Core responsibilities: lead technical integration of modern AI practices into existing engines, tools, and pipelines; embed with partner teams to set technical direction and ship scalable solutions; mentor and pair-program embedded engineers through to full code-ownership handoff; evangelize AI engineering best practices and security/performance guardrails across Riot; build new middleware, APIs, or foundations where off-the-shelf tools fall short; manage high ambiguity and build consensus across engineering and product leadership.
- Required qualifications: BS in Computer Science or equivalent experience, 8 to 12 years of software engineering experience; deep C++ plus at least one other language (Python or C# named as examples); systems integration and technical leadership experience; shipped at least one AAA game or a large-scale, high-concurrency live service product; a track record of setting technical strategy and leading multi-month or multi-year initiatives under high ambiguity; practical experience using, integrating, or building AI tools, APIs, or infrastructure.
- Desired qualifications: Unreal Engine (C++) extension or development experience; familiarity with ML frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow) or LLM orchestration; background on shared engine, central tools, or platform engineering teams; familiarity with the Runeterra or VALORANT IPs.
- Compensation and logistics: Los Angeles base salary range $229,200 to $319,500 plus incentive compensation, equity, 401k match, medical/dental/vision/life insurance, and open PTO. Posted 2026-08-27, on-site in Los Angeles (not remote).
