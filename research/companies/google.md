## 2. Core narrative
Google Cloud is converting frontier AI research into revenue by embedding engineers directly
inside enterprise customers rather than only selling APIs and advisory hours. The GenAI Forward
Deployed Engineer role exists specifically to close the last mile between Google's AI products and
production reality: the posting describes the job as moving "beyond high-level architecture to
code, debug, and jointly ship bespoke agentic solutions directly within the customer's
environment," managing the integration complexity, data readiness, and state-management work that
keeps AI pilots from reaching enterprise-grade maturity, and feeding field insights back into
Google's product roadmap. At Google Cloud Next 2026, Google rebranded and expanded Vertex AI into
the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform and announced it is now extending forward-deployed engineering
talent to strategic partners (Accenture, Deloitte, McKinsey) for joint customer implementations,
evidence this embedded-builder model is being scaled, not treated as a side motion. That scale-up
is landing in Mexico specifically: Google has stood up an Engineering Center of Excellence in
Mexico City explicitly because, in its own Director of Engineering's words, it has struggled to
find enough talent in places like California and is turning to Mexican engineers, opened a cloud
region in Queretaro, and launched an AI-skilling push (Capacita+ IA) aiming to train up to a
million Mexicans. This bilingual, Mexico-City, citizen-only FDE III role sits exactly at that
intersection: hands-on agentic-AI delivery for LATAM enterprise accounts inside a company that is
visibly building out its regional AI and engineering footprint, not just fulfilling a single
headcount request.

## 3. Mission & product
- Google Cloud's enterprise AI platform was rebranded at Next 2026 from Vertex AI into the
  **Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform**: build tools (Agent Development Kit, Agent Studio, Agent
  Runtime, Agent Sandbox), memory/session management, governance and security (Agent Identity,
  Agent Registry, Agent Gateway, Agent Security Dashboard), and evaluation/optimization tooling
  (Agent Simulation, Agent Evaluation, Agent Optimizer). Google also announced an **Agentic Data
  Cloud** (Knowledge Catalog, zero-copy federation with Palantir/Salesforce/SAP/ServiceNow/
  Workday, a Cross-Cloud Lakehouse built on the former BigLake) and 8th-generation TPUs (TPU 8t
  for training, TPU 8i for inference/agentic workloads).
  [Google Cloud Next 2026 wrap-up](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/google-cloud-next/google-cloud-next-2026-wrap-up)
- Named enterprise agent deployments cited by Google: GE Appliances (800+ agents across
  manufacturing/logistics), Tata Steel (300+ agents, 9-month rollout), Unilever (multi-agent
  procurement), Deutsche Telekom (MINDR, cited 95% reduction in event-management time), Home
  Depot (Magic Apron plus an AI phone agent), Macy's ("Ask Macy's" concierge), Citi Wealth
  (multilingual Citi Sky agent). [Google Cloud Next 2026 wrap-up](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/google-cloud-next/google-cloud-next-2026-wrap-up)
- Relevant regional customer signal: BBVA deepened its Google Cloud partnership, rolling out
  Google Workspace with Gemini to 100,000 employees globally (BBVA Bancomer is BBVA's Mexican
  banking unit), and Google Cloud separately launched Gemini Enterprise for Financial Services in
  August 2026 aimed at capital-markets and corporate-banking teams.
  [BBVA deepens partnership with Google Cloud](https://www.bbva.com/en/innovation/bbva-deepens-partnership-with-google-cloud-to-innovate-with-ai/),
  [Google Cloud launches Gemini Enterprise for Financial Services](https://www.googlecloudpresscorner.com/2026-08-25-Google-Cloud-Launches-Gemini-Enterprise-for-Financial-Services)
- Business model for this specific role: Google Cloud Consulting / Applied AI runs a
  services-plus-platform motion, forward-deploying engineers into named strategic accounts
  (from "early stage startups to prominent, established companies" per the FDE job family) to
  ship production agentic systems, not just sell licenses.
  [Job posting: FDE III, GenAI, Google Cloud, Mexico City](https://careers.google.com/jobs/results/74283803417158342-forward-deployed-engineer-iii/)

## 4. Culture & voice
- Google's hiring rubric is built around four signals: General Cognitive Ability, Role-Related
  Knowledge, Leadership, and "Googleyness," Google's internal term for a set of behavioral traits
  assessed in every loop. Google's own employees and interns describe it informally as being
  "collaborative, ingenious, passionate, friendly, genuine," and helpful to colleagues regardless
  of how busy they are. [Google's own blog on intern Googleyness](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/life-at-google/real-internship/)
- Third-party interview-prep sources (not Google's own copy, but widely cited and consistent with
  the official four-signal framework) describe Googleyness as grouping into: thriving in
  ambiguity, valuing feedback, challenging the status quo, putting the user first, doing the right
  thing, and caring about the team; useful as directional prep, not verbatim Google language.
  [igotanoffer: Googleyness interview guide](https://igotanoffer.com/blogs/tech/googleyness-leadership-interview-questions)
- Reported 2026 interview loop for this specific FDE family (per interview-prep aggregation, not
  an official Google source): recruiter screen, a coding/algorithms round, a "vibe coding" round
  (collaborative, ambiguous production-style requirements), an agentic/ML system-design round
  covering RAG architectures, vector databases, and deployment trade-offs, and a Googleyness
  behavioral round; some reports describe the loop compressed into as few as two days.
  [Exponent: Google FDE interview guide](https://www.tryexponent.com/guides/google-forward-deployed-engineer-interview)
- Google states it is "committed to building a workforce that is representative of the users we
  serve, creating a culture of belonging" on its careers site.
  [Google Careers](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/)
- Local signal: 89% of Mexican respondents in an IPSOS study commissioned around Google's 20-year
  Mexico anniversary agreed "Google Mexico's presence has a positive impact on my life," and
  Google is opening new office space in Polanco, Mexico City.
  [Celebrating 20 years of Google in Mexico](https://blog.google/company-news/inside-google/around-the-globe/google-latin-america/google-mexico-20-years/)

## 6. Engineering & AI direction
- The FDE III job description (verified via Google's own search snippet for this exact posting)
  states minimum qualifications of a bachelor's degree or equivalent, 5 years building software
  with Python or a similar language, experience architecting AI systems on cloud platforms, and
  experience building pipelines for structured and unstructured data using vector databases and
  RAG-like architectures; preferred qualifications include a master's or PhD in AI/CS, experience
  implementing multi-agent systems with frameworks like LangGraph, CrewAI, or Google's own Agent
  Development Kit (ADK), and knowledge of LLM-native evaluation metrics, state management, and
  granular tracing. Resumes must be submitted in English.
  [Job posting: FDE III, GenAI, Google Cloud, Mexico City](https://careers.google.com/jobs/results/74283803417158342-forward-deployed-engineer-iii/)
- The team, per the job family description, is the "AAI Solutions Consulting Team," described as
  the technical engine "defining the best practices and deployment frameworks" for building and
  deploying enterprise AI agents at scale, synchronizing Solution Architecture, Engineering, and
  Product to move agents "from discovery to high-scale production."
  [Google FDE role description (search excerpt)](https://careers.google.com/jobs/results/123340188553224902-senior-forward-deployed-engineer/)
- Core responsibilities per the FDE job family: serve as lead developer for customer AI
  applications transitioning prototypes into production-grade agentic workflows; architect and
  code connections between Google's AI products and a customer's live infrastructure (APIs,
  legacy data silos, security perimeters); build evaluation pipelines and observability for
  agentic systems against accuracy, safety, and latency requirements.
  [Google FDE role description (search excerpt)](https://careers.google.com/jobs/results/123340188553224902-senior-forward-deployed-engineer/)
- Infrastructure direction: 8th-gen TPUs (TPU 8t training, TPU 8i inference/agentic workloads,
  reported 80% better performance-per-dollar for agentic workloads than the prior generation),
  native PyTorch support (TorchTPU), and NVIDIA Blackwell GPU availability via Google Distributed
  Cloud, alongside the new Agentic Data Cloud (Iceberg-based Cross-Cloud Lakehouse, zero-copy
  federation to third-party enterprise systems). [Google Cloud Next 2026 wrap-up](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/google-cloud-next/google-cloud-next-2026-wrap-up)
- Reported (third-party, not Google's own page) interview signal for this exact role family: the
  loop weights a "vibe coding" round (ambiguous, production-style requirements solved live) and a
  client-simulation/customer-facing round as heavily as, or more heavily than, the algorithmic
  coding round, consistent with the job's emphasis on shipping inside live customer environments
  rather than pure LeetCode-style screening.

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago (FDE III, Generative AI, Google Cloud, Mexico City)
**Best hook to lead with:** Santiago has already done, at founder scale, the exact motion this
role is titled for: embedding directly with a client to turn a frontier AI capability into a
production system the client's own non-technical people run. At Pragma AI he co-built and shipped
a RAG "company brain" and internal agent tooling directly inside client and internal operations
(quoting, diagnostics, onboarding), not as a slide deck but as running code with evaluation
harnesses and access controls. That is a smaller, self-directed version of what Google is now
scaling into a formal org (AAI Solutions Consulting) and extending outward to Accenture, Deloitte,
and McKinsey, evidence that "forward-deployed, embedded AI builder" is becoming a real category
Google is investing in, not a one-off requisition.

- **RAG and vector-database production experience, the JD's literal minimum qualification**:
  Pragma AI's "Pragma Brain" ships scoped retrieval with deterministic no-answer/citation gating,
  multilingual ES/EN embeddings, and an automated eval harness; Plutus adds a separate LLM
  research agent and MCP control plane over a Postgres/TimescaleDB backend. This maps directly to
  the JD's "building pipelines for structured and unstructured data using vector databases and
  RAG-like architectures." [resume/master_resume.yaml]
- **Multi-agent and tool-use frameworks, a named preferred qualification**: Kairos exposes 52
  tools through a single typed layer to both an in-app Claude Agent SDK agent and a standalone MCP
  server; Pragma's internal platform runs 15 agent definitions and 10 reusable routines over a
  hosted MCP service. Not LangGraph/CrewAI/ADK by name, but the same underlying skill (multi-agent
  orchestration, tool-definition layers, session state) the JD asks for.
  [resume/master_resume.yaml]
- **Bilingual, Mexico-City, client-facing, and eligible under the posting's citizenship
  requirement**: the role is explicitly English/Spanish and restricted to Mexican citizens under
  Article 7; Santiago is a Mexican citizen based in Mexico City, fluent in both languages, and has
  run direct client deployments (Eugenia.ai on-site HOA onboarding, Pragma AI client diagnostics),
  a real, not theoretical, match to "embed with strategic accounts."
  [Job posting](https://careers.google.com/jobs/results/74283803417158342-forward-deployed-engineer-iii/), [resume/master_resume.yaml]
- **Founder-level ownership of ambiguous, 0-to-1 builds**: the "vibe coding" and Googleyness
  rounds reportedly weight comfort with ambiguity and bias to action; five years running Online
  Basics end to end and taking Eugenia.ai from concept to acquisition in 18 months are direct,
  provable evidence of that trait, not just a claimed soft skill.

**Honest concerns / watch-outs:**
- **Years of experience is a real gap against the posted minimum**: the FDE III minimum
  qualification is 5 years of software development experience with Python or a similar language.
  Santiago's hands-on coding history spans Nextia.mx (2017-2018), then a multi-year founder gap
  where engineering was one of several hats, then a return to heavy hands-on building in 2026
  (Plutus, Kairos, Pragma AI). He should be ready to make the case for equivalent depth (production
  systems shipped solo, real users) rather than assume the years line item reads cleanly.
- **No named LangGraph, CrewAI, or Google ADK experience**: his agent-framework experience is real
  but built on the Claude Agent SDK, MCP, and a custom Hermes-based agent, not the specific
  frameworks the JD calls out as preferred. Worth a direct, honest "different stack, same
  underlying pattern" answer rather than implying direct experience.
- **No enterprise-scale deployment experience**: his production systems (Pragma Brain, Kairos,
  Plutus, Online Basics' internal platform) serve small teams and SMB clients, not the
  Fortune-100-scale accounts (GE Appliances, Tata Steel, Citi) Google's FDE org is shown working
  with. This is a genuine scale gap to name, not talk around.
- **Big-company, on-site, formal-interview-loop environment is a real change from founder life**:
  five-plus years running his own company is a strong ownership signal, but he has not worked
  inside a company at Google's scale and process maturity; worth a candid, prepared answer for why
  that trade (structure, scale, an internal platform investment few companies can match) appeals
  to him now.
- **Layoff/hiring-freeze backdrop**: 2026 reporting describes rolling, unannounced cuts touching
  parts of Google Cloud even during record revenue growth; worth acknowledging as a real,
  if generic-to-big-tech, risk rather than treating Google as immune to it.

## 8. Application-question angles
- **Why this company**: Lead with the Core narrative's double bet: Google Cloud is scaling
  forward-deployed, embedded AI engineering from a niche motion into a real organization (the AAI
  Solutions Consulting team, now extended to Accenture/Deloitte/McKinsey), and it is doing that
  scale-up with real, dated investment in Mexico specifically (Queretaro region, the Mexico City
  Engineering Center of Excellence, Capacita+ IA's push to train a million Mexicans in AI). Frame
  interest as wanting to be inside that build-out in Mexico City rather than a generic "I love
  Google" answer.
- **Why you fit**: Anchor on the Pragma AI parallel: Santiago has already embedded with clients to
  ship a working RAG system and agent tooling into production, with real evaluation and access
  controls, in Spanish and English, for non-technical Mexican SMB operators. Name that directly as
  a smaller version of the exact job (forward-deployed, code-and-ship, not just advise), then be
  candid that the years-of-experience line and enterprise scale are areas he would be stepping up
  into, not repeating.
- **Anything else**: Use this slot for the honest gaps: no direct LangGraph/CrewAI/ADK production
  experience and no Fortune-100-scale deployment track record, paired with concrete evidence of
  fast ramp on unfamiliar stacks (Swift and Java at Nextia.mx, a Claude Agent SDK plus MCP stack
  built from scratch at Pragma AI and Kairos in 2026) as the basis for closing that gap quickly on
  the job. Optionally note eligibility clearly: Mexican citizen, Mexico City based, bilingual,
  directly meeting the posting's Article 7 and language requirements.
