# Vimcal

## 1. Snapshot
Consumer/prosumer calendar and scheduling app ("the world's fastest calendar") with an AI scheduling layer (Vimcal AI) and a dedicated product for executive assistants (Vimcal EA). Y Combinator S18, founded 2018, Seed stage: confirmed total funding is $6.85-6.97M (mostly a $4.5M seed led by Altos Ventures in Nov 2023); some third-party trackers list total funding as high as $8.9M with additional investors (Asymmetric Capital Partners, Function, Gaingels, Unpopular Ventures) but I could not confirm the amount or date from a primary source, treat as unverified. HQ New York City; small, largely remote-friendly team, reported between 9 (YC profile) and 11-50 (Asymmetric Capital Partners portfolio page), with LinkedIn showing about 16. [ycombinator.com/companies/vimcal](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vimcal), [TechCrunch, Nov 28 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/11/28/vimcal-raises-4-5-million-to-expand-its-team-offerings/), [PR Newswire, Nov 28 2023](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/vimcal-secures-4-5-million-in-seed-funding-led-by-altos-ventures-301998576.html), [jobs.acp.vc/companies/vimcal-2](https://jobs.acp.vc/companies/vimcal-2)

## 2. Core narrative
Founders John Li and Mike Zhao met at Berkeley and have built three companies together; Vimcal, launched in January 2020, was the first one they built purely to scratch their own itch, after Li found himself scheduling 30+ investor meetings a week during a prior fundraise and hating every calendar tool available. The founding obsession has stayed the same since: strip every unnecessary step out of the calendar (their own benchmark is "half the number of steps or less" versus any competitor, reached by literally counting keystrokes and mouse movements for the top-10 things people do in a calendar), and now extend that same speed-and-craft obsession into AI, an "agentic scheduling experience" built on Claude that is the explicit focus of this open role. It is a small (roughly 10-16 person), Seed-stage, founder-led team, not a large, process-heavy org. [f2f.substack.com, Startup Spotlight #52](https://f2f.substack.com/p/startup-spotlight-52-vimcal), [TechCrunch, Nov 15 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/15/vimcal-wants-be-the-most-nifty-calendar-app-on-the-block/), [findwork.dev job posting](https://findwork.dev/n98bOBX/ai-product-engineer-full-stack-at-vimcal)

## 3. Mission & product
- Core calendar app: web, Mac/Windows desktop, iOS, and Chrome extension; unifies Google Workspace and Outlook accounts; keyboard-shortcut-driven, sub-100ms response times, natural-language command bar ("Lunch meeting with Lisa at 1pm tomorrow"), "Time Travel" cross-timezone view, Slots/Personal Links for availability sharing. [vimcal.com](https://www.vimcal.com/), [TechCrunch, Nov 15 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/15/vimcal-wants-be-the-most-nifty-calendar-app-on-the-block/)
- Vimcal AI: an AI scheduling assistant that reads pasted availability lists or Calendly links and surfaces mutual free time for confirmation, plus AI-generated attendee "dossiers" (originally described as "GPT-powered") summarizing guests directly in the invite. [vimcal.com/vimcal-ai](https://www.vimcal.com/vimcal-ai)
- Vimcal EA (formerly "Maestro"): a separate product built specifically for executive assistants managing multiple executives' calendars; features include auto-deleting calendar holds (cut from 77 clicks to 8), reschedule/cancellation counters, and multi-executive profile management. Positioned as "the first calendar built for Executive Assistants." [vimcal.com/ea](https://www.vimcal.com/ea)
- Business model: consumer/prosumer subscription, individual plans from $20/month or $200/year after a free trial, team pricing for 5+ seats; EA/enterprise pricing sold separately. [vimcal.com/pricing](https://www.vimcal.com/pricing) (referenced via [vimcal.com](https://www.vimcal.com/))
- Named customers/logos: OpenAI, Zapier, Menlo Ventures, Harry's, and Mercury (named on the main site); Netflix, Kaiser Permanente, Airtable, the NBA, and Square appear as logos on the Vimcal AI page (shown, not explicitly named in body copy). Marketing copy claims "2,000+ companies" use Vimcal AI. [vimcal.com](https://www.vimcal.com/), [vimcal.com/vimcal-ai](https://www.vimcal.com/vimcal-ai)

## 4. Culture & voice
- The job posting itself is the clearest culture signal: Vimcal calls itself "product-obsessed," states its mission as helping people "give people back their time," and lists four hiring criteria ahead of credentials: "Warmth, Clear thinking, Bias towards over-communication, Excessive attention to detail." It explicitly says "We don't care where you got your degree or if you even have one," and welcomes candidates from economics, law, and bootcamp backgrounds. [findwork.dev job posting](https://findwork.dev/n98bOBX/ai-product-engineer-full-stack-at-vimcal)
- Founder voice matches: "Fast, not rushed. It's easy to conflate the two... You can move at a fast tempo by saying no," and "I'm very proud of the product we built. We've put so much effort into crafting a beautiful, fast experience and solving problems our users didn't even know they had." [f2f.substack.com, Startup Spotlight #52](https://f2f.substack.com/p/startup-spotlight-52-vimcal)
- Public-facing voice toward the EA persona is community-driven rather than corporate: Vimcal runs a "CCing My EA" newsletter ("Thursday Things") and has hosted in-person EA meetups (e.g., in Chicago and, planned as of July 2025, an NYC event with Offsite). [ccingmyea.vimcal.com, Jul 31 2025](https://ccingmyea.vimcal.com/p/thursday-things-jul-31st-2025)
- No dedicated public engineering blog was found; almost all technical/AI detail available externally comes from product marketing pages and the job posting itself, not technical writing, which is itself a data point about a very small, product-first (not blog-first) engineering culture.

## 6. Engineering & AI direction
- Confirmed stack, from the job posting: Ruby on Rails backend, React and TypeScript frontend, and Claude (Anthropic) as the model powering current AI feature work. Earlier product marketing (Vimcal AI page) referenced "GPT-powered dossiers," so the team has used both OpenAI and Anthropic models, with the newest engineering role explicitly built around Claude. [findwork.dev job posting](https://findwork.dev/n98bOBX/ai-product-engineer-full-stack-at-vimcal), [vimcal.com/vimcal-ai](https://www.vimcal.com/vimcal-ai)
- The AI Product Engineer (Full-Stack) role is scoped to design and ship AI-powered calendar features end-to-end, explicitly including "agentic scheduling experiences" and smart suggestions, spanning React frontend work, Rails backend work, and LLM integration, while keeping the product reliable for users who open their calendar 30-40 times a day (a very low tolerance for latency or breakage, consistent with the brand's speed obsession). [findwork.dev job posting](https://findwork.dev/n98bOBX/ai-product-engineer-full-stack-at-vimcal)
- Required: 3+ years of software engineering experience with a proven track record shipping LLM/AI features in production, proficiency in React and TypeScript, strong product instincts, and the ability to reason about AI system limitations and make production-ready tradeoffs. Nice-to-have: experience with agentic patterns, RAG, function calling, and multi-step LLM workflows. [findwork.dev job posting](https://findwork.dev/n98bOBX/ai-product-engineer-full-stack-at-vimcal)
- Listed compensation on Y Combinator's own board: $150,000-$200,000, location shown as "New York, NY, US / Remote (US)" (see watch-out in section 7 on what this implies for remote-from-Mexico eligibility). [ycombinator.com/companies/vimcal/jobs](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vimcal/jobs)
- Given the team's small size (roughly 10-16 people), whoever fills this role will likely own a meaningful share of the AI product surface directly rather than a narrow slice of a larger platform team.

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago
**Best hook to lead with**: Santiago ships production LLM/agent systems using Claude every day, both as his default coding tool (Claude Code) and as the model behind real shipped products: Pragma AI's RAG "company brain" (Pragma Brain), a Hermes-framework agent with tool-use/function calling for personal finance, and an MCP-exposed research agent in Plutus. That is a near-exact match for what this specific posting asks for: proven production LLM/AI feature delivery, on Claude, with agentic patterns, RAG, and function calling named as the desired nice-to-haves. This is not generic "AI interest," it is the same tools and patterns on the JD, already shipped.

Supporting angles:
- Full-stack, end-to-end ownership as a 3x founder: at Eugenia.ai he co-built the full product (React frontend, Node.js backend on AWS) and owned it from concept through client deployment and eventual acquisition, the same "design and ship end-to-end" scope this role asks for, not a narrow contributor role.
- Comfort operating in a small, high-ownership team: Online Basics scaled to $8M in revenue with a lean 9-person team and over $500K revenue-per-employee; Vimcal is a similarly small (roughly 10-16 person), founder-led team where this role is effectively the AI product owner, not one of many engineers on a feature.
- The hiring philosophy (non-credentialist, "warmth, clear thinking, over-communication, attention to detail") plausibly favors his path: a Chemical Engineering degree rather than a CS degree, a founder-built engineering background, and direct, hands-on client and stakeholder management (Eugenia.ai's on-site HOA/property-manager deployments, leading a 9-person team) all speak to those specific traits rather than pedigree.

**Honest concerns / watch-outs**:
- Remote-from-Mexico eligibility is genuinely unclear. Y Combinator's own jobs board lists the role as "New York, NY, US / Remote (US)," which reads as US-remote only, while the findwork.dev listing does not state a geographic restriction either way. This needs to be confirmed directly with Vimcal before or early in the process; TN visa eligibility covers physical work authorization inside the US, it does not by itself resolve a "must be US-based/remote-in-US" policy. [ycombinator.com/companies/vimcal/jobs](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/vimcal/jobs), [findwork.dev job posting](https://findwork.dev/n98bOBX/ai-product-engineer-full-stack-at-vimcal)
- Ruby on Rails is the backend language in the stack and is not on Santiago's resume (his backend experience is Python/FastAPI and Node.js). Worth naming honestly rather than glossing over, alongside the fact that he has picked up new stacks quickly before (Swift and Java at Nextia, FastAPI/TimescaleDB for Plutus).
- Company scale and funding are genuinely small: confirmed total funding is under $7M, last raised in November 2023, and headcount is roughly 10-16 people. This is real startup risk (runway, stability) worth a candid gut check, even though the small-team, high-ownership shape closely matches his own founder background.

## 8. Application-question angles
- **Why this company**: Lead with the Claude-based "agentic scheduling experience" hook, not a generic "I love productivity tools" line. Vimcal's entire identity is an obsession with removing friction ("fast, not rushed," "half the number of steps") and it is now extending that obsession into agentic AI on Claude, which is exactly the kind of production LLM/agent work Santiago does daily and has already shipped (Claude Code as his default tool, Pragma Brain, the Hermes-based agent). Anchor on that specific overlap rather than restating the job description back to them.
- **Why you fit**: Center on the two most concrete, verifiable proof points: shipped agentic/RAG systems with function calling (Pragma Brain, the Hermes personal-finance agent, Plutus's MCP research agent) against the JD's explicit "ship LLM/AI features in production" and "agentic patterns, RAG, function calling" asks, plus full-stack, 0-to-1 product ownership (Eugenia.ai, Online Basics) against the JD's "design and ship end-to-end" scope. Do not checklist mission, culture, news, and engineering in one answer, pick this one narrative thread.
- **Anything else**: Use this slot for the two honest, concrete items rather than more enthusiasm: (1) confirm remote-from-Mexico eligibility given the "Remote (US)" framing on the YC listing, and (2) name the Rails gap directly, framed against his track record of picking up new backend stacks quickly.
