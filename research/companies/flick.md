# Flick (flick.art)

Draft dossier for Santiago Coronado, prepared for job id 9163 (Senior Full-stack Engineer,
remote). Review before use, this is a first pass, not a final source of truth.

## 1. Snapshot

Flick (flick.art) is an AI-native filmmaking platform, "Figma plus Cursor, for AI films": a
single workspace that takes a filmmaker from script to storyboard to shots to finished cut with
cinematic control over character, style, and camera language, rather than a one-click video
generator. Y Combinator Fall 2025 batch. Seed stage: raised $6M in May 2026 from True Ventures,
GV (Google Ventures), Lightspeed, Y Combinator, Formosa Capital, Pioneer Fund, Olive Tree
Capital, N1, and angels. Two co-founders as of the raise, actively hiring a founding engineering
team. Based in the San Francisco Bay Area (Sunnyvale, CA listed on the job posting), remote
flexibility offered, visa sponsorship available.
[YC company page](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flick),
[Businesswire seed announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260514408256/en/Flick-Raises-$6M-Seed-Round-to-Redefine-How-AI-Films-Are-Created),
[YC job posting](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flick/jobs/mqWOs5b-senior-full-stack-engineer).

Important disambiguation: this is NOT Flick.social/Flick.tech, the AI Instagram/hashtag
marketing tool. This Flick (flick.art) is the AI filmmaking studio, confirmed by the job
description text ("Founded by Engineer who built Instagram Stories + Award winning filmmaker")
matching Ray Wang and Zoey Zhang.

## 2. Core narrative

Flick pairs a founding Instagram engineer with an award-winning AI filmmaker to build the
professional-grade creative tool that one-click AI video generators are not: a filmmaker's
instrument, not a slot machine. Ray Wang wrote most of Instagram Stories' backend during its
first year and helped scale it to 400M+ daily active users; Zoey Zhang went from traditional
filmmaking to winning Best Visual at MIT's AI Film Hack in February 2025 and has since picked up
nominations or wins at 20 to 30+ international festivals. The pitch to investors and candidates
alike is "we handle AI, you direct films": the company explicitly frames itself as building tools
so filmmakers can stay in the director's chair, not be replaced by the model.
[True Ventures investment post](https://www.trueventures.com/blog/why-we-invested-in-ai-filmmaking-platform-flick),
[pulse2 funding recap](https://pulse2.com/flick-6-million-raised-for-ai-native-filmmaking-platform/).

## 3. Mission & product

- Product: a non-linear creative canvas where scripts, characters, scenes, and shots are
  connected nodes, with cinematic controls for visual consistency, camera language, shot angles,
  and emotional arc, plus an abstracted AI model layer so the filmmaker's attention stays on
  story rather than prompt-wrangling.
  [True Ventures](https://www.trueventures.com/blog/why-we-invested-in-ai-filmmaking-platform-flick).
- Flick TV: launched late June/early July 2026, a companion distribution and streaming
  destination for original AI films and series made with Flick, positioned as the studio's own
  "home" for AI-native storytelling and creator-led IP, not just a generation tool.
  [Launch YC: Flick TV](https://www.ycombinator.com/launches/RCR-flick-tv-the-future-home-of-original-ai-films).
- Flick Filmmaker Residency: a cohort program (10+ emerging filmmakers so far) producing short
  films that have screened at Cinequest, MIT, and the Omni AI Film Festival, each cohort led by
  co-founder Zhang, used both as quality proof and as a pipeline for content on Flick TV.
  [pulse2](https://pulse2.com/flick-6-million-raised-for-ai-native-filmmaking-platform/),
  [Businesswire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260514408256/en/Flick-Raises-$6M-Seed-Round-to-Redefine-How-AI-Films-Are-Created).
- Customers/audience: professional and emerging filmmakers, not casual social-video creators.
  Investor language explicitly contrasts Flick with "generic AI video generation" aimed at
  volume/social content.
  [True Ventures](https://www.trueventures.com/blog/why-we-invested-in-ai-filmmaking-platform-flick).
- Business model: not publicly detailed in available sources (unverified); the YC listing
  describes it as a software platform, pricing/monetization not disclosed in primary sources
  found.

## 6. Engineering & AI direction

- Stack (from the job postings, primary source): TypeScript, React, and WebGL on the frontend
  for the canvas/timeline/node-graph editor; Python for a backend "Agent framework" built
  in-house; Node for services; the Senior Full-stack posting also asks for "modern web
  frameworks" and general polyglot backend/API skill.
  [YC Senior Full-stack posting](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flick/jobs/mqWOs5b-senior-full-stack-engineer).
- The role is explicitly "founding full stack engineer": own backend systems for media
  generation, storage, and delivery; architect APIs for real-time, stateful creative workflows;
  build and maintain integrations with AI model providers and compute platforms; and stand up
  testing, observability, and CI/CD from a comparatively early baseline.
  [Job posting via findwork](https://findwork.dev/XE5rgjX/senior-full-stack-engineer-at-flick).
- The companion Senior/Staff Frontend role is scoped around the editor itself (canvas, timeline,
  node graph, playback) and large client-side state, confirming the product is a true creative
  tool/IDE-style interface, not a simple form-and-generate wrapper.
  [YC Frontend posting](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flick/jobs/Tdu6FH6-senior-staff-frontend-engineer).
- AI bet: the platform is built around an "abstracted model layer" so the product is not locked
  to one generation model or vendor, plus a proprietary Python agent framework for orchestrating
  generation, storage, and creative workflow state.
  [True Ventures](https://www.trueventures.com/blog/why-we-invested-in-ai-filmmaking-platform-flick),
  [YC Senior Full-stack posting](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flick/jobs/mqWOs5b-senior-full-stack-engineer).
- Compensation on the listing: $100K to $200K base plus 0.10% to 1.00% equity, 3+ years
  experience expected, visa sponsorship offered.
  [YC Senior Full-stack posting](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flick/jobs/mqWOs5b-senior-full-stack-engineer).

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago

Lead hook: **founder-engineer who ships 0-to-1 full-stack AI products end to end.** Flick is a
2-person founding team hiring its first engineers to build production infrastructure around
AI model orchestration, real-time stateful workflows, and media generation/delivery. Santiago has
twice taken a product from zero to a real, paying user base as founder and lead engineer
(Eugenia.ai: React/Node full-stack SaaS to 20+ B2B buildings and 10,000+ users in 18 months and an
acquisition; Online Basics: LLM-workflow-driven operations to $8M revenue on a lean team), and his
current build (Plutus) is a FastAPI/Python + React/TypeScript system with Celery/Redis async
workers and an LLM research agent, which maps directly onto Flick's "async-heavy, real-time,
stateful" backend and Python agent-framework needs.

Second hook: **production LLM/AI-product experience, not just prototype experience.** He designed
and shipped an LLM workflow on the Anthropic and OpenAI APIs with custom prompt engineering and a
multi-stage validation pipeline running in production (Online Basics), and built a RAG "company
brain" and tool-use/function-calling agents at Pragma AI. This is directly relevant to Flick's
"build and maintain integrations with AI model providers" requirement and its own in-house agent
framework.

Honest gaps and watch-outs:
- No filmmaking, animation, video editing, or game-dev background (a listed nice-to-have, not a
  requirement). If genuinely interested in the craft side, this needs an honest, non-fabricated
  answer, lean on general creative-tool/UX instincts (Eugenia.ai's mobile/web product design work)
  rather than claiming film experience he doesn't have.
- No direct experience with WebGL, canvas/timeline/node-graph editors, or real-time media
  pipelines at the fidelity this product needs; his frontend depth is React/TypeScript web apps,
  not creative-tooling-grade rendering. Worth naming directly and pairing with fast-learning
  evidence (multiple full-stack systems shipped solo or with lean teams).
- Compensation: listing is $100K to $200K base plus 0.10% to 1.00% equity, which clears his
  65k USD floor but is meaningfully below his prior revenue-per-employee outcomes; worth going in
  clear-eyed that this is an early-stage equity bet, not a comp play.
- Location: role lists Sunnyvale, CA with "remote flexibility," not explicitly worldwide-remote.
  Should confirm during process whether remote-from-Mexico-City is actually workable given a
  2-person team likely wanting some in-person overlap, and that TN-visa/sponsorship logistics are
  compatible (the listing does say visa sponsorship is available).
  [YC Senior Full-stack posting](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/flick/jobs/mqWOs5b-senior-full-stack-engineer).

## 8. Application-question angles

- **Why this company**: lean into the founder pairing, not a generic "I like AI video." Flick is
  explicitly building the tool for filmmakers who want control, not another one-click generator,
  led by someone who scaled Instagram Stories' backend and someone who's already won festival
  awards using early versions of the product. That combination of deep infra chops and real
  creative-domain proof is the specific bet worth naming.
- **Why you fit**: anchor on being a founder-engineer who has shipped full-stack AI products from
  zero, alone or with a lean team, twice, and who has already built the two things this role
  needs most: async-heavy backend systems (Plutus's Celery/Redis pipeline) and production LLM
  integrations with real validation logic (Online Basics' generation pipeline, Pragma's RAG
  agent). Frame it as "I've done the founding-engineer job before, including the parts that don't
  show up in a job description," not a feature-by-feature skills match.
- **Anything else**: be direct that he does not come from film/video, and reframe that as an
  asset rather than hide it: he is the backend/infra builder who lets Zhang's creative vision and
  Wang's product instincts run on solid rails, and he is used to being the person on a 2 to 9
  person team who owns reliability, testing, and shipping speed at once.
