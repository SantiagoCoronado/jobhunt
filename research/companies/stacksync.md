# Stacksync

## 1. Snapshot
Stacksync is a real-time, two-way (bidirectional) data sync and enterprise integration platform, connecting CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot, SAP), databases (Postgres, BigQuery), ERPs, and 1,000+ other business apps, including legacy systems without APIs via browser automation [ycombinator.com/companies/stacksync](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/stacksync), [stacksync.com](https://www.stacksync.com/). Y Combinator W24 batch, founded 2022 by Ruben Burdin (CEO) and Alexis Favre (CTO) [ycombinator.com/companies/stacksync](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/stacksync). Seed stage: about $500K (YC plus a small group of angel/seed funds; PitchBook lists Kima Ventures, Lightbird Ventures, Moonfire, Motier Ventures, Oasis Capital as investors) [tracxn.com](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/stacksync/__etLit3vobBA6-l47qo7O54HZ2EIfIm-giE7HGj8PADc/funding-and-investors). HQ San Francisco with a Lausanne, Switzerland presence; remote-friendly across several countries [ycombinator.com/companies/stacksync/jobs](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/stacksync/jobs). Team size is reported inconsistently across sources (YC's own page says 34; other aggregators say 9 to 17), so treat headcount as approximate, not a hard fact.

## 2. Core narrative
Stacksync's pitch is that "most AI products help individuals save minutes; Stacksync helps companies run their operations" (from the role's own posting) [job description, findwork listing](https://findwork.dev/nKJw2qX/senior-frontend-engineer-ai-product-ux-at-stacksync). The company built years of unglamorous plumbing (bidirectional sync, connectors, workflow orchestration, retries, conflict resolution, legacy-system integration via browser automation) and is now repositioning that plumbing as the substrate for enterprise AI agents: "Your last integration took months. Your next one takes a prompt." [stacksync.com](https://www.stacksync.com/). Product Hunt copy explicitly brands it "the first AI-native Enterprise Integration Platform" (launch dated roughly January 2026) [producthunt.com/products/stacksync](https://www.producthunt.com/products/stacksync). The distinctive story: a deep, boring, hard integration layer (EDI, legacy IBM AS/400, no-API systems) is the moat, and the current bet is wrapping AI agents and a cleaner frontend UX around that moat rather than starting from a UI-first product.

## 3. Mission & product
- Two-way sync keeps a CRM/ERP and a database (or another app) continuously and bidirectionally consistent, so teams can read and write CRM data via SQL instead of building custom API integrations [docs.stacksync.com/two-way-sync](https://docs.stacksync.com/two-way-sync).
- 200+ pre-built, no-code connectors (Salesforce, HubSpot, NetSuite, Zendesk, Shopify, Stripe, SAP, Workday, Snowflake, Postgres, BigQuery, Slack, Teams, AWS, and legacy systems like IBM AS/400) [stacksync.com](https://www.stacksync.com/), [stacksync.com/connectors/ibm-as400](https://www.stacksync.com/connectors/ibm-as400).
- Target buyers: engineering, RevOps, data, finance, and customer-support teams at mid-to-large enterprises in e-commerce/retail, logistics, manufacturing, energy, healthcare, insurance, public sector, and SaaS [stacksync.com](https://www.stacksync.com/).
- Named customers/logos: Gladia, IDEXX, Vimeo, Codility, ACERTUS, Rinsed, Eko, Ubicloud; several case studies describe migrations off MuleSoft, Celigo, Fivetran, Heroku Connect, and Matillion [stacksync.com](https://www.stacksync.com/).
- Business model: freemium with a 14-day trial, paid tiers, plus "white-glove deployment" professional services for enterprise rollouts [stacksync.com](https://www.stacksync.com/), [stacksync.com/pricing](https://www.stacksync.com/pricing).
- Newer framing: an "AI Copilot" that lets a user describe an integration and have an agent "plan it, write the code, and ship it to production," and AI agents that read a company's knowledge base and call its enterprise apps [stacksync.com/team/engineering](https://www.stacksync.com/team/engineering).

## 6. Engineering & AI direction
- This specific role's stated stack: React with IBM Carbon Design System components for the frontend [YC job posting](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/stacksync/jobs/cyy7Ahf-senior-frontend-engineer-ai-product-ux), [findwork.dev listing](https://findwork.dev/nKJw2qX/senior-frontend-engineer-ai-product-ux-at-stacksync).
- Backend/infra handles asynchronous jobs, data mappings, approvals, error handling, retries, and multi-system orchestration; the frontend role's core mandate is to translate that complexity into simple, predictable enterprise UX [findwork.dev listing](https://findwork.dev/nKJw2qX/senior-frontend-engineer-ai-product-ux-at-stacksync).
- Engineering-facing marketing describes a TypeScript SDK plus a visual workflow builder, CI/CD-adjacent integration via webhooks (Sentry, Datadog, PagerDuty, Snyk, GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket, Linear/Jira/Asana), and an "AI Copilot" for building integrations from natural language [stacksync.com/team/engineering](https://www.stacksync.com/team/engineering).
- Product direction is explicitly toward enterprise AI agents that read a company's data/knowledge base and take actions in connected systems (CRM/ERP), i.e. sync infrastructure becoming the tool-calling backbone for agentic workflows [stacksync.com](https://www.stacksync.com/), [stacksync.com/team/engineering](https://www.stacksync.com/team/engineering).
- No public engineering blog with deep technical posts (architecture, incident postmortems, etc.) was found; the blog is largely SEO/product-marketing content (integration guides, comparison posts) rather than an engineering-culture blog [stacksync.com/blog](https://www.stacksync.com/blog).
