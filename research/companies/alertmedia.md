# AlertMedia

researched: 2026-08-24
status: draft for Santiago to review

## 1. Snapshot

AlertMedia builds a cloud-based risk intelligence and response (emergency mass communication
and threat monitoring) platform, serving over 4,000 clients including Walmart, JetBlue, DHL,
Coca-Cola Bottling, and Thomson Reuters ([about page copy quoted on job posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/alertmedia/jobs/8735080002),
[Feb 2026 sale-exploration coverage](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/vista-equity-explores-1-billion-150250511.html)).
Founded 2013, Austin, Texas HQ ([Built In Austin funding article](https://www.builtinaustin.com/articles/alertmedia-raises-15m)).
High-growth, PE-backed SaaS: Vista Equity Partners took a majority (strategic growth) stake in
March 2021 through its Foundation Fund; prior investors JMI Equity, Next Coast Ventures,
Silverton Partners, and founder/CEO Brian Cruver retained stakes
([Fortune, Mar 2021](https://fortune.com/2021/03/10/vista-equity-buys-controlling-stake-in-alertmedia)).
As of June 2026, roughly 505 employees ([WebSearch summary of company-size data point, unverified primary source]).
Revenue is over $100M annually and Vista was exploring a sale of AlertMedia valued at $1B+ as of
February 2026, with JPMorgan running the process
([Yahoo Finance, Feb 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/vista-equity-explores-1-billion-150250511.html)).
Has a Mexico City office in the Reforma area alongside its Austin HQ and a UK presence
([Built In Mexico City job listings search](https://builtin.com/company/alertmedia/jobs)).

## 2. Core narrative

AlertMedia sells the tooling that keeps people safe when something goes wrong (severe weather,
violence, travel risk, brand-reputation threats) for enterprise customers, and it has spent the
last year turning that same "reduce time to signal" mission inward: it just named its first
Chief AI Officer (May 2026) and is running "an AI-first development model that's already well
underway," explicitly hiring engineers to build the internal AI agents and skills that take on
engineering work itself, not just to consume AI tools someone else built
([Sr. Software Engineer (AI Tools) posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/alertmedia/jobs/8735080002),
[Chief AI Officer appointment, Built In Austin, May 2026](https://www.builtinaustin.com/articles/alertmedia-appoints-president-chief-ai-officer-20260521)).
That is the distinctive hook: a profitable, PE-backed, mission-driven (life-safety) company that
is treating its own engineering org as an AI-adoption product, with dedicated headcount for it,
at a moment when it is also fielding a $1B+ sale process.

## 3. Mission & product

- Mission, in the company's own words: "help organizations protect their people, operations, and
  brand" through a "Risk Intelligence and Response platform" that unifies "critical information
  and workflows" so teams can "detect emerging threats, assess impact, and respond with
  confidence" ([job posting About/mission copy](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/alertmedia/jobs/8735080002)).
- Product line includes mass notification/communication, threat monitoring, and (newer) AI-driven
  social/OSINT intelligence: in July 2026 AlertMedia expanded "Social Intelligence," an AI-powered
  monitoring product now covering 65+ social platforms, fringe communities, and dark-web sources,
  analyzing about 15 million records a day, aimed at corporate security teams tracking threats to
  executives, employees, and brand
  ([Morningstar/BusinessWire release, Jul 14 2026](https://www.morningstar.com/news/business-wire/20260714127882/alertmedia-expands-ai-powered-social-intelligence-to-help-security-teams-detect-and-mitigate-emerging-risks-faster)).
- Customers span small businesses to Fortune 500s in 130+ countries; named logos include Walmart,
  JetBlue, DHL, Coca-Cola Bottling, Greyhound, Healthgrades, and Thomson Reuters
  ([Yahoo Finance, Feb 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/vista-equity-explores-1-billion-150250511.html)).
- Business model: enterprise SaaS subscription (implied by "PE-backed SaaS," "clients," and the
  revenue multiple language in the sale-exploration reporting); no public per-seat pricing found.

## 6. Engineering & AI direction

- The open Sr. Software Engineer (AI Tools) role is the clearest primary-source signal: AlertMedia
  is running "an AI-first development model," and this hire is meant to "design and extend the
  agents, skills, and automation that take on engineering work," widening what those agents own
  "from planning and implementation through code review and CI," while also fixing the friction
  that caps hand-off (slow local environments, long feedback loops, manual steps)
  ([job posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/alertmedia/jobs/8735080002)).
- Tooling named explicitly: the team uses agentic coding tools "predominantly" Codex and Claude,
  daily, and wants someone who extends these tools (custom agents, skills, prompts) rather than
  only consuming them. Required stack: Python or JavaScript/TypeScript, AWS, PostgreSQL, Docker,
  local dev environments, CI systems, automated testing
  ([job posting, raw fetch of Greenhouse page](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/alertmedia/jobs/8735080002)).
  A separate Built In listing for the AlertMedia Software Engineer in Test II role also mentions
  the Reforma-area Mexico City office as hybrid 1 to 2 days a week for that specific role
  ([Built In listing](https://builtin.com/job/software-engineer-test-ii/7454961)), suggesting
  hybrid cadence varies by role rather than being one company-wide policy.
- Product-side AI: the July 2026 Social Intelligence release describes "continually trained AI" to
  surface concerning OSINT posts at scale (65+ platforms, ~15M records/day analyzed), and the May
  2026 leadership release references "agentic capabilities" shipped into the product in the prior
  year ([Morningstar/BusinessWire](https://www.morningstar.com/news/business-wire/20260714127882/alertmedia-expands-ai-powered-social-intelligence-to-help-security-teams-detect-and-mitigate-emerging-risks-faster),
  [Built In Austin](https://www.builtinaustin.com/articles/alertmedia-appoints-president-chief-ai-officer-20260521)).
- Newly created Chief AI Officer role (May 2026) is charged with setting "architectural standards
  and security governance for AI systems" and driving adoption company-wide
  ([Built In Austin](https://www.builtinaustin.com/articles/alertmedia-appoints-president-chief-ai-officer-20260521)),
  meaning the AI Tools engineering hire sits underneath a fresh, executive-level AI mandate rather
  than a grassroots-only effort.
- No public engineering blog was located during this research pass (searches for an AlertMedia
  eng blog returned no company-authored results); this is a gap, not a claim that one does not
  exist.

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago

**Best hook to lead with**: this role is explicitly about building internal AI tooling and agents
that other engineers use daily, which is Santiago's strongest lived pattern. At Online Basics he
built the company's internal operations platform (React front end, SQL, Python ETL) used daily by
operators to run an $8M business; at Pragma AI he co-built an internal MCP-based operating
platform (agent definitions, reusable routines, an internal CRM) that lets operators run the
business as tools inside Claude Code; Kairos is a personal command center that exposes 52 tools
through both an in-app agent and a standalone MCP server. That is close to a direct match for "AI
agents and skills used in the development process" and "internal tools that remove friction,"
which is exactly this posting's charter
([job posting](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/alertmedia/jobs/8735080002),
`resume/master_resume.yaml` Online Basics, Pragma AI, and Kairos entries).

Secondary hook: he already builds daily inside Claude Code (Claude Agent SDK, MCP tool layers,
Claude Code as a work surface at Pragma AI), matching the posting's explicit "we use Codex and
Claude predominantly" and its ask for someone who extends agentic tools rather than only consumes
them.

**Honest gaps and watch-outs**:
- The posting asks for 6 to 8 years of hands-on software engineering experience. Santiago's
  background is real and technically deep but runs through founder/CEO roles and consulting
  rather than a traditional single-track SWE career; this is worth naming plainly rather than
  finessing, since the resume should let the work speak for itself.
- The role wants daily production-CI, test-generation, and local-dev-environment optimization
  work; Santiago's CI/testing depth (Vitest, Playwright suites in Kairos and the Hydrel project)
  is real but smaller-scale than an enterprise CI pipeline serving 500 engineers.
- No public engineering blog was found to further verify day-to-day practices; the assessment of
  "AI-first development model" rests on this one job posting plus the Chief AI Officer press
  coverage, not on a broader engineering-culture archive.
- Location: confirmed fine. Santiago is Mexico City based and the live Greenhouse posting states
  the role is "remote based in Mexico," with the Reforma office available but explicitly "not
  required, but optional" (see Watch-outs below for a discrepancy versus assumed hybrid framing).

## 8. Application-question angles

- **Why this company**: lead with the AI-first development model and the new Chief AI Officer
  mandate, not the general "risk intelligence" mission. The angle: AlertMedia is treating its own
  engineering org as an AI-adoption problem worth dedicated headcount and executive sponsorship,
  which is the exact kind of 0-to-1 internal-tooling work Santiago has already been doing (Pragma
  AI's internal MCP platform, Online Basics' internal ops platform). Keep the life-safety mission
  as a one-line note, not the centerpiece; the tooling mandate is the more specific, resonant hook.
- **Why you fit**: lean on the pattern, not a list of tools. He has repeatedly built the internal
  system that lets a small team run a real business (Online Basics' pricing/inventory platform,
  Pragma's MCP brain and internal CRM, Kairos' 52-tool agent layer), and he already lives inside
  Claude Code daily as both a builder and a user. Name one concrete artifact (for example, the
  Pragma internal MCP platform) rather than summarizing the whole resume.
- **Anything else**: worth a short, honest note on the experience-shape gap (founder/consulting
  track rather than traditional single-employer SWE tenure) framed as why the internal-tooling
  angle fits him better than a generic feature-team role, plus a plain confirmation of Mexico
  City residency and work authorization since the posting states AlertMedia does not sponsor.

## 9. Watch-outs

- **Location/hybrid discrepancy**: the task brief describes this posting as "hybrid 2 days/week
  in the Reforma area." The live Greenhouse posting, fetched directly on 2026-08-24, actually
  reads: "This position is remote based in Mexico. Must reside in Mexico and we have an office in
  Mexico City (Reforma area), if you're interested in going into the office. Not required, but
  optional." ([job posting, raw fetch](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/alertmedia/jobs/8735080002)).
  A separate AlertMedia Mexico City role (Software Engineer in Test II) is listed elsewhere as
  hybrid 1 to 2 days/week ([Built In](https://builtin.com/job/software-engineer-test-ii/7454961)),
  so hybrid cadence appears to be role-specific, not a blanket policy; do not assume this AI Tools
  role requires in-office days without reconfirming at application/interview time.
- **No sponsorship**: the posting states AlertMedia does not sponsor and requires existing legal
  authorization to work in Mexico indefinitely; not a concern for Santiago (Mexican citizen) but
  worth confirming residency status explicitly in the application.
- **Active sale process**: Vista Equity was exploring a $1B+ sale of AlertMedia as of February
  2026 ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/vista-equity-explores-1-billion-150250511.html)).
  As of this research date no outcome was found; a pending ownership change during onboarding is
  worth being aware of, though it is common for PE-backed SaaS companies and not necessarily a red
  flag.
- **Thin engineering-blog trail**: claims about "AI-first development model" and engineering
  practices rely on the job posting itself and press coverage of the Chief AI Officer
  appointment, not on a dedicated engineering blog archive. Treat any interview prep on their
  actual internal tooling as something to verify live rather than assume from this dossier.
- **Compensation**: no salary band is published on the Greenhouse posting or in the DB record for
  job id 21119 (`salary_min`/`salary_max` are null); anchor to `config/profile.yaml`
  `comp.min_usd_latam_scope` and confirm range early in process.
