# Abstract (Abstract3D / InstaLOD group): Company Dossier

**Disambiguation note:** this is NOT abstract.com (the design/version-control tool) and NOT
Abstrakt Marketing Group. Per the WeWorkRemotely posting (job id 6681, HQ "Stuttgart, DE", URL
`http://theabstract.co`), the employer is the Stuttgart-based 3D/AI deep-tech company that owns
InstaLOD, InstaMAT, RSX Engine, and Polyverse. Its live site is `theabstract.co`, which redirects
to `abstract3d.com`. Confidence: high (site redirect, matching HQ, matching product list, matching
"3D pipeline" language all line up with the job post's "About Us" section).

## 1. Snapshot
Stuttgart, Germany deep-tech company building an integrated 3D content pipeline platform
(InstaLOD, InstaMAT, RSX Engine, Polyverse) for games, VFX/film, automotive, and enterprise/XR
clients; privately held (InstaLOD GmbH last published balance sheet total ~3M EUR, fiscal year
2023, per Northdata); fully remote/distributed team hiring contractor roles worldwide; this
specific role is a worldwide-remote contractor position, not an employee role.
[theabstract.co redirect](https://abstract3d.com/), [Northdata InstaLOD GmbH](https://www.northdata.com/InstaLOD+GmbH,+Stuttgart/HRB+756693)

## 2. Core narrative
Abstract grew out of InstaLOD, a 3D geometry-optimization tool that became the industry standard
for shrinking and automating game/VFX asset pipelines (used by Sega, Activision, Bandai Namco,
Wargaming, and others). CEO and founder Manfred M. Nerurkar then broadened the bet: instead of
staying a single optimization tool, Abstract is consolidating the historically fragmented,
manual 3D production pipeline (optimization, material/texture authoring, real-time rendering,
asset management) into one connected platform, explicitly positioned at "the intersection of 3D
and AI." Their own tagline is blunt about the thesis: "The 3D pipeline is broken. We're building
the platform to fix it."
[Abstract homepage](https://abstract3d.com/), [About Abstract](https://abstract3d.com/about-abstract/)

## 3. Mission & product
- **Products (four, integrated):**
  - **InstaLOD**: 3D optimization, polygon/geometry reduction (up to 99% claimed), remeshing
    (including a new Quad Remesher), CAD-to-3D conversion and tessellation, workflow automation.
  - **InstaMAT**: AI-powered generative materials and texture authoring.
  - **RSX Engine**: browser-native, real-time 3D engine with multiplayer/cloud collaboration.
  - **Polyverse**: cloud-based 3D asset management, versioning, and delivery ("data processing as
    a service").
  [Abstract homepage](https://abstract3d.com/)
- **Integrations**: ships as plugins/SDKs inside third-party tools; as of July 2026 added native
  integrations for Maxon Cinema 4D and Nvidia Omniverse (USD pipelines), alongside existing
  Autodesk/Unity/Unreal Engine support. CEO quote on the July 10, 2026 release: "This release is
  about removing the friction of getting to InstaLOD in the first place, meeting artists and
  engineers inside the tools they rely on." [Abstract news, InstaLOD Cinema 4D/Omniverse](https://abstract3d.com/2026/07/10/abstract-expands-instalod-across-cinema-4d-and-omniverse-ships-new-quad-remesher/)
- **Business model**: software licensing, including a free "Pioneer" tier for studios under $100K
  revenue, plus SDK/REST API access for pipeline integration. [Abstract homepage](https://abstract3d.com/)
- **Customers/industries**: game development (AAA and indie), VFX/film, automotive design,
  aerospace/defense (training/simulation), enterprise, XR/metaverse. Named clients across sources:
  Sega, Activision, Phoenix Labs, Accenture Song, Kakao, Ford, Daimler/Mercedes-Benz, Tesla, NASA,
  Bandai Namco, Volvo, Mattel, NCSOFT, Wargaming, Polyphony Digital.
  [WWR job post](https://weworkremotely.com/remote-jobs/abstract-full-stack-web-developer-remote-contractor), [Abstract homepage](https://abstract3d.com/)
- **Legal entity**: InstaLOD GmbH, HRB 756693, District Court of Stuttgart, registered at
  Breitscheidstrasse 65, Stuttgart. [Northdata](https://www.northdata.com/InstaLOD+GmbH,+Stuttgart/HRB+756693)
