# Landing (hellolanding.com)

Draft dossier for Santiago Coronado. Researched for job id 13098, "AI Builder" (aggregator
posting: https://www.adzuna.com.mx/details/5809757560, location shown as Miguel Hidalgo, Ciudad
de Mexico). This is a draft: review before use and refresh if it goes stale. Note: this is
**Landing / hellolanding.com**, the flexible-stay furnished-apartment company (HQ Birmingham,
Alabama), not LandingAI (Andrew Ng's computer-vision company) or Landing.Jobs (a European tech
recruiting marketplace); several search results mix these three up.

## 1. Snapshot

Privately held, VC- and debt-backed flexible-stay furnished-apartment company; founded 2019 by
Bill Smith (previously founded and sold grocery-delivery company Shipt) [highperformr.ai company
profile; Landing press releases]. Dual/multi-site footprint: HQ Birmingham, Alabama, with
additional offices in San Francisco, New York, and Mexico City [hellolanding.com/p/about;
highperformr.ai]. Roughly 300 to 450 employees depending on source and date (about page: "300+
team members"; LinkedIn company page: ~410; a third-party aggregator: 317 as of an April 2025
snapshot) [hellolanding.com/p/about; linkedin.com/company/hellolanding/life; highperformr.ai].
Raised $305M+ across equity and debt since founding, including a $125M Series C (equity plus
debt, led by Delta-v Capital, Aug 2022) and a $180M debt facility (May 2025) [Crunchbase profile
summary via search; globenewswire.com, May 20 2025 press release]. Network spans 5,000+
furnished apartments (about page) across 250+ cities (about page) to 400+ cities (homepage
claims 407), the discrepancy likely reflects different counting methods across pages
[hellolanding.com/p/about; hellolanding.com homepage].

## 2. Core narrative

Landing spent 2019 to 2022 building a capital-intensive, membership-based network of
self-operated furnished apartments, raised a $125M Series C in August 2022, then two months
later cut 110 US jobs and restructured around a leaner, field-based "Mission Control" service
model [shorttermrentalz.com; globenewswire Series C coverage via search]. In December 2023 it
relaunched its core business model around Autopilot, an asset-light, B2B platform that furnishes
and fills otherwise-vacant units for multifamily property owners (Landing takes a cut of revenue
instead of carrying the lease risk itself), which by late 2024 had become the company's
"exclusive business model" [globenewswire.com, Dec 7 2023 Autopilot launch; globenewswire.com,
Oct 30 2024 "Doubles Down" release]. It backed that pivot with a $30M acquisition of Barsala
(March 2024) and a fresh $180M debt facility (May 2025) to keep scaling the platform, and is now
layering on adjacent partnerships (Gopuff in-stay delivery, Feb 2026) and a new C-suite (CFO,
EVP Strategic Accounts, April 2026) it explicitly frames as entering "its next phase of growth"
[globenewswire.com Barsala, May 2025 financing, Feb 2026 Gopuff, and Apr 28 2026 new-hires
releases]. The AI Builder role sits inside that same leaner, post-reset company: the JD describes
the internal AI function as "like AI special ops," a small team embedded across the business to
find and automate gaps, not a research lab [job posting, Vz18GJp7St/AI-Builder].

## 3. Mission & product

- Product: fully furnished apartments bookable for anywhere from a few nights to open-ended
  stays, with no security deposit and the ability to transfer between network apartments with 30
  days' notice; positioned as a "third option" between hotels and traditional leases
  [hellolanding.com homepage; globenewswire.com Apr 28 2026 release].
- Three stay types: Short Term, Flexible Term (no fixed end date, ~30% savings vs. a fixed
  term), and Fixed Term [hellolanding.com homepage].
- Two customer sides: (1) renters, including corporate relocation, project-based teams, and
  travelers, served through the consumer Landing app/membership and a "Homes for Healing"
  discounted-stay program for families needing medical-related housing [hellolanding.com
  homepage; globenewswire.com, Dec 3 2024 Homes for Healing release]; (2) multifamily property
  owners/partners, served through Autopilot, which furnishes units, drives demand from Landing's
  guest base, and handles guest support in exchange for a share of revenue on units that would
  otherwise sit vacant [globenewswire.com, Dec 7 2023 and Oct 30 2024 releases].
- Recent product extension: a February 2026 partnership with Gopuff giving guests at 2,500+
  Landing apartments across 42 US markets discounted on-demand delivery via an in-apartment QR
  code [globenewswire.com, Feb 18 2026 release].

## 6. Engineering & AI direction

No public engineering blog or open-source presence was found for Landing, so this section leans
on the two live engineering-adjacent job postings plus limited third-party leadership data.

**The AI Builder role and team.** The JD frames the function directly: "This team is like AI
special ops, we are working with all areas of the business to help them create automated
solutions to problems," looking for someone "excited about finding gaps in the business,
validating them with data, and prototyping solutions rapidly with AI" [job posting,
Vz18GJp7St/AI-Builder]. Responsibilities: build internal tools and customer-facing features on
top of LLMs (OpenAI, Claude, open-source models); design and maintain prompt chains, RAG
pipelines, and agent workflows; prototype from idea to working demo "in days, not sprints"; wire
AI into existing systems (APIs, databases, CRMs, Slack); own the full stack of a feature
(frontend, backend, model integration, deployment) [same JD]. The sibling Remote posting adds
more concrete technical bar: hands-on LLM API experience including function calling and
embeddings, vector databases, prompt engineering, and lists LangChain/CrewAI-style agent
frameworks as a nice-to-have [job posting, eyWj4a8cXk/AI-Builder]. Both ask for a "fluent English
speaker" and both are one-person-deep job ads (no team size, manager, or reporting line named).

**On-site vs. remote, worth confirming directly.** The Mexico City AI Builder listing gives the
location as "Mexico City, Mexico, Full Time" with no explicit remote/hybrid language, and lists as
a nice-to-have "Willing to relocate in the future to Mexico City," odd phrasing given the role is
already located there, which suggests the JD text may be a lightly-adapted copy of the Remote
version rather than a CDMX-specific rewrite [job posting, Vz18GJp7St/AI-Builder]. Landing's
careers page states company-wide that all roles are "in office" positions "unless otherwise stated
in the job description" [hellolanding.com/p/careers]. Taken together, the reasonable read is that
the CDMX AI Builder posting is on-site in Landing's Mexico City office by default; this should be
confirmed directly with the recruiter rather than assumed, especially given the parallel fully
Remote posting for the identical title exists at the same time.

**Adjacent data/engineering stack signal.** The separately posted Senior Analytics Engineer role
(Birmingham and Remote) names a concrete stack: dbt (expert-level, production models), Snowflake,
Stitch, SQL, with Heap, Clarity, and GA4 as data sources and Sigma, Looker, MetricFlow, and the
dbt Semantic Layer as nice-to-haves; this role reports into building a "finance and operations
data spine" for executive/board/investor reporting [job posting, pXH4BQa2ys/Senior-Analytics-Engineer].
It is not the AI team, but it is the closest public signal of Landing's broader data
infrastructure.

**Leadership.** Company-data aggregators (not confirmed on Landing's own site) list Tyler K.
Vaughey as CTO as of an April 2025 snapshot; treat this as directionally useful but unverified
against a primary Landing source [highperformr.ai company profile; Crunchbase person profile
search]. No head of AI, VP of AI, or similar title was found publicly, consistent with this being
a small, newly-forming function rather than an established org.

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago

**Best single hook to lead with:** Landing's AI Builder role is explicitly not a lab role, it is
an embedded generalist who finds a real operational gap in a non-technical business unit, ships a
working prototype in days, and owns it end to end (frontend to deployment). Santiago has run that
exact loop repeatedly and independently: at Eugenia.ai (also proptech, also building for property
administrators and HOAs) he owned the full product lifecycle and led on-site client deployments,
translating messy non-technical requirements into working apps; at Online Basics he designed and
owned an LLM workflow (Anthropic and OpenAI APIs, custom prompt engineering, multi-stage
validation) that had to cut real content-creation time, not survive a demo; and at Pragma AI
(current) he is doing internal-AI-enablement as the actual product, a RAG "company brain" plus
conversational agents and automations for quoting, reporting, onboarding, and collections
[resume/master_resume.yaml]. That is close to a direct rehearsal of "AI special ops," just
previously delivered to outside clients rather than one company's internal teams.

**Supporting fit points:**
- Fluent English (explicitly required in both AI Builder postings) plus native Spanish and a
  Mexico City base, which matches the CDMX listing directly rather than needing the "willing to
  relocate" nice-to-have [job postings; config/profile.yaml].
- Hands-on RAG pipelines, prompt chains, agent workflows, and API/database/CRM integration work
  (Pragma Brain's scoped retrieval and citation gating, Kairos's MCP tool layer, the personal-
  finance agent's tool-use/function calling) line up closely with the JD's named building blocks
  [resume/master_resume.yaml].
- Full-stack ownership (React/TypeScript frontends through Python/FastAPI backends to deployment)
  matches the JD's "own the full stack of a feature" requirement without needing a larger
  engineering org behind him [resume/master_resume.yaml].

**Honest gaps and watch-outs:**
- No salary is listed on either AI Builder posting, and unlike the adjacent Senior Analytics
  Engineer listing (which explicitly names "competitive salary + stock options" and a $750/year
  Landing Stays credit), the AI Builder postings' benefits section is shorter and does not mention
  equity [job postings, Vz18GJp7St and eyWj4a8cXk vs. pXH4BQa2ys]. Per profile rule, missing pay is
  not a dealbreaker, but this gap is worth a direct question early rather than assuming parity with
  the analytics role.
- The CDMX posting does not explicitly confirm on-site vs. hybrid; Landing's default company
  policy is in-office unless stated otherwise, so plan for an on-site role in Landing's Mexico City
  office and confirm this directly [hellolanding.com/p/careers; job posting].
- This is a one-person-deep posting with no named manager, team size, or reporting line; combined
  with no public engineering blog or open-source presence, there is real uncertainty about how
  mature the internal AI function is versus how much of it Santiago would be building from
  scratch. Worth asking directly in-interview how many people are on the team today.
- Landing's business had a real reset: a $125M raise followed two months later by 110 layoffs
  (2022), then a full business-model pivot to the asset-light Autopilot platform (2023), which is
  legitimate context for "why is this company hiring for a brand-new internal AI role right now,"
  not necessarily a red flag given the company has since raised $180M in debt financing (2025) and
  added a new CFO (2026), but worth having an informed, honest view on company stability if asked
  [shorttermrentalz.com; globenewswire.com releases cited above].

## 8. Application-question angles

**Why this company:** Lead with the pivot, not the apartments. Landing rebuilt its core business
model in 2023 into a leaner, asset-light platform (Autopilot) after a real reset, and it is now
building a small, hands-on internal AI function inside that same lean, revenue-accountable
company rather than staffing a research lab. The JD's own language, "AI special ops," signals a
team meant to ship things that move real operational numbers, which is the same bar Santiago has
built his whole career around.

**Why you fit:** Do not checklist engineering skills, lead with the embedded-builder pattern.
He has already run "find the real gap in a non-technical team's workflow, prototype fast, ship it,
own it end to end" at Eugenia.ai (also proptech, also with non-technical property managers), at
Online Basics (an LLM workflow that had to hit real revenue and time-savings numbers), and now at
Pragma AI, building internal AI enablement (RAG brain, workflow automations) as the actual
product other companies buy. This role asks for exactly that motion, just inside Landing instead
of for an outside client.

**Anything else:** Mexico City based already, so the on-site expectation in the CDMX posting is
not friction, and fluent in English and Spanish, which the JD calls out directly. Genuinely drawn
to companies that had to fight through a real business-model reset and come out leaner, which
mirrors bootstrapping Eugenia.ai to an acquisition without outside capital and scaling Online
Basics to $8M in revenue with a lean, 7-person team.
