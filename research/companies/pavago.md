## 1. CRITICAL QUESTION: is Pavago an employer or an intermediary?

**Pavago is a recruiting/staffing intermediary, not the employer for this role.** This is the
single most important fact in this dossier.

- Pavago LLC is a staffing and recruitment firm headquartered in Meridian, Idaho, founded in 2022
  and co-founded by Parker Cox, whose business is sourcing, vetting, and placing offshore talent
  (Latin America, Pakistan, South Africa, and elsewhere) with U.S. small and medium businesses
  (SMBs) [markets.financialcontent.com PressAdvantage release, linkedin.com/in/parker-cox-43b75368].
  Its own homepage frames the company as a marketplace: "Hire Remote Talent: 300,000+ Vetted
  Pros," industry-tagged "Staffing and Recruiting" [pavago.co homepage; LinkedIn search result].
- A parallel Workable posting for a Pavago "Virtual Assistant" role is explicitly described (in
  third-party job-board summaries of the live posting) as Pavago "hiring on behalf of one of their
  clients" [web search summary of apply.workable.com/pavago/j/DE10AAFFA2]. This is Pavago's
  standard framing across its open roles, not a one-off; the AI Systems Engineer posting itself
  never names an end client, referring only to "the founder" the hire will work directly with
  [adzuna.com.mx/details/5817699429], consistent with an anonymized client placement rather than a
  role at Pavago's own product.
- Pavago's public pricing confirms the mechanics: clients pay Pavago roughly $2,000/month for a
  mid-level developer and $3,000 to $4,000/month for a senior developer, plus a $329/month platform
  fee, an effective client-side cost of about $12 to $15/hour; Pavago states the developer works
  "directly for you" (i.e. for the client) once placed, positioning itself as a sourcing and payroll
  layer rather than the employer of record for day-to-day work [blog.pavago.co/outsource-software-development/].
  Pavago also states it provides "compliance assurance" and "payroll management" as ongoing services
  after placement [markets.financialcontent.com PressAdvantage release], which reads as Pavago
  handling payroll administration on behalf of the client relationship, not as Pavago employing the
  worker in the ordinary sense.
- Engagement model for the hire: based on the above, the realistic structure is an independent
  contractor or Pavago-administered payroll arrangement working for an undisclosed U.S. SMB client,
  not a direct employment relationship with a single named company. The posting itself does not
  state "contractor" or "employee" outright, and does not name the end client
  [adzuna.com.mx/details/5817699429]; this is a genuine gap that should be asked about explicitly
  and early, not assumed.
- This is materially the same shape as the referral-marketplace pattern Santiago has previously
  rejected (an undisclosed end client, a placement fee/markup business model, and Pavago itself as
  the visible "employer" on the job board rather than the company doing the actual work), and it
  should be weighed accordingly before investing further time.

## 2. What Pavago does (as a company, not as this role's real employer)

- Core business: recruit and place vetted remote professionals from Latin America, Pakistan, South
  Africa, and other regions with U.S. SMBs across sales, marketing, admin, finance, IT/engineering,
  and product roles, via a five-step process (discovery call, candidate search, screening,
  placement) typically completed within about three weeks
  [markets.financialcontent.com PressAdvantage release; pavago.co homepage].
- Value proposition to clients: staffing-cost reduction of "up to 70%" versus U.S. domestic hiring,
  positioned against agencies ($15,000 to $40,000/month), Upwork freelancers ($5,000 to
  $12,000/month), and staff augmentation ($6,000 to $12,000/month)
  [blog.pavago.co/outsource-software-development/; markets.financialcontent.com PressAdvantage release].
- Scale: advertises "300,000+ vetted remote candidates" available in its talent pool
  [pavago.co homepage]. Company size estimates vary by source: LinkedIn shows 2 to 10 employees in
  one listing, other data aggregators show roughly 21 to 50; founded 2022
  [web search: LinkedIn Pavago company page; company-size aggregator results]. No public funding
  round was found for Pavago itself; it appears to be a self-funded/bootstrapped staffing business,
  not a venture-backed startup, though the absence of a funding announcement is not proof none
  exists.
- Client testimonials cite named small businesses such as Celebrate Dental, Eversite, Tenant
  Planet, and Zen Dental Studio [pavago.co homepage], i.e. Pavago's own customers are small local
  service businesses, not tech companies; this is useful context for what kind of "founder" this
  posting's end client is likely to be.
- Reviews: Trustpilot shows a mix of client-side praise (pricing, hiring speed, contractor quality)
  and at least one placed-worker complaint about payment issues and an "aggressive and dismissive"
  staff interaction, per third-party review summaries; the review platform itself returned a 403
  when fetched directly, so this could not be independently verified beyond the search summary and
  should be treated as a single, unverified data point, not a pattern [Trustpilot review summary
  via web search; direct Trustpilot fetch blocked].

## 4. Pay: what can be inferred (since the posting lists none)

No salary figure appears in the posting itself [adzuna.com.mx/details/5817699429]. Two indirect,
lower-confidence signals both point toward low pay relative to Santiago's floor:
- Pavago's own published client pricing: $2,000/month for a mid-level developer and $3,000 to
  $4,000/month for a senior developer (plus the client's separate $329/month platform fee), an
  effective client-paid rate of roughly $12 to $15/hour, i.e. a client-side annual cost around
  $24,000 to $48,000/year for the developer's time before any margin the worker themselves nets out
  of that [blog.pavago.co/outsource-software-development/]. This is the amount the CLIENT pays for
  the engagement, not necessarily what the placed engineer receives, but it caps what is realistic.
- Aggregated, self-reported Pavago compensation data (Himalayas, based on roughly 376 listed
  Pavago-posted remote roles across all functions): median base pay of about $30,000/year across
  all roles, with a small number of senior/specialist roles (e.g. a Costa Rica-based QA Automation
  Engineer) reported as high as $80,000 to $150,000/year [himalayas.app/companies/pavago/salaries].
  Sample size and methodology are not disclosed by Himalayas; treat as directional only.
- Conclusion: both signals point to Pavago-sourced roles typically pricing well under Santiago's
  profile floor of $70,000/year for LATAM-scoped roles [config/profile.yaml comp.min_usd_latam_scope],
  though a senior, technical, agentic-AI-specific role (this one) is plausibly closer to the upper,
  outlier end of Pavago's range rather than its median. This is unverified for this specific role
  and must be confirmed directly and early, ideally before investing time in a Spark Hire video.
