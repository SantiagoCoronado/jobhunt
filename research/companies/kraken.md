# Kraken (Payward, Inc.)

Draft dossier for Santiago's review. Not a final claim of fact beyond what is cited below.

Verified identity: this is Kraken the cryptocurrency exchange (legal entity Payward, Inc.),
not Kraken Technologies / "Kraken" the Octopus Energy platform (engineering.kraken.tech), which
is an unrelated UK company that also publishes an engineering blog under the "Kraken" name. All
sources below are from kraken.com, blog.kraken.com, payward.com, jobs.ashbyhq.com/kraken.com, or
press coverage of the crypto exchange.

## 1. Snapshot

Kraken is a global cryptocurrency exchange (spot, futures, margin trading, staking, plus stock
and tokenized-asset trading) founded in 2011, with 6.6M funded accounts as of Q2 2026 (up 42%
year over year) and $40B in platform assets (payward.com Q2 2026 press release, August 14, 2026).
Parent entity Payward, Inc. posted $508M in adjusted revenue for Q2 2026 (up 17% year over year,
$23M adjusted EBITDA) and is a private company preparing for an IPO, now expected in 2027 rather
than 2026 after a Deutsche Borse stake purchase in April 2026 valued it at $13.3B, down from an
earlier $20B raise (payward.com press release; financemagnates.com, May 18, 2026; coindesk.com,
May 8, 2026). Remote-first, hires in 70+ countries (blog.kraken.com). This role (Software Engineer,
Frontend, Payments, job 21657 / Ashby req 4e8d9070) lists Mexico as an eligible secondary location
alongside Colombia, Argentina, Romania, Cyprus, Hungary, Czechia and others (see Section 7).

## 2. Core narrative

Kraken is a 14+ year old crypto exchange (old by industry standards, not a startup) mid-transition
from "crypto native" to full-spectrum financial infrastructure. Under the Payward umbrella it now
describes itself as a "unified financial infrastructure platform" spanning Kraken (exchange),
NinjaTrader (futures), Breakout (crypto prop trading), xStocks (tokenized equities), Payward
Services (B2B infrastructure) and CF Benchmarks (indices) (payward.com/about). It holds a Federal
Reserve master account via its Wyoming bank charter (first crypto-native firm to do so), has
applied for an OCC national trust bank charter, and closed or announced four major acquisitions
in a six-week stretch in spring 2026 alone (Deutsche Borse investment, Bitnomial derivatives,
Reap Technologies stablecoin payments, Dubai VARA authorization), followed by a Magic Labs wallet
infrastructure acquisition in July 2026, all while IPO prep slipped to 2027
(financemagnates.com, June 8, 2026 and May 18, 2026; theblock.co, July 27, 2026). The Payments &
Blockchain team specifically, which owns this req, is described in its own posting as responsible
for "the first and last mile of every dollar (digital or fiat) that goes in or out of Kraken"
(jobs.ashbyhq.com req 4e8d9070). That combination, a legacy-stable regulated company still moving
fast on M&A and infrastructure build-out, with a frontend team explicitly tasked with the
money-movement layer, is the distinctive story here.

## 3. Mission & product

- Stated mission: "accelerate the adoption of crypto globally, so everyone can achieve financial
  freedom and inclusion" (kraken.com/careers).
- Products under the Payward umbrella: Kraken (spot, margin, futures, staking, xStocks tokenized
  equities), Krak (a "global money app"), NinjaTrader (equity/futures derivatives), Breakout
  (crypto prop trading), Payward Services (B2B infrastructure), CF Benchmarks (regulated crypto
  indices) (payward.com/about).
- Revenue model: trading commissions and fees; Q2 2026 adjusted revenue $508M, up 17% year over
  year, $23M adjusted EBITDA (payward.com press release, August 14, 2026).
- Regulatory build-out: Fed master account through its Wyoming SPDI bank charter (April 2026),
  OCC national trust bank charter application, Dubai VARA preliminary authorization for
  broker-dealer and investment-management licensing (May 21, 2026), and European growth credited
  to MiCA authorization (coindesk.com/business/2026/05/14; financemagnates.com, June 8, 2026;
  payward.com press release).
- M&A since late 2025 broadening the product surface: NinjaTrader ($1.5B, retail futures),
  Bitnomial (up to $550M, derivatives exchange, closed May 1, 2026), Reap Technologies (up to
  $600M, stablecoin payments and card issuance across APAC/MENA/LatAm, closed July 1, 2026),
  Magna (token management, February 2026), and Magic Labs' embedded wallet business (signed
  July 27, 2026, infrastructure behind 60M+ wallets and $10B+ stablecoin volume, folded into
  Payward Services) (coindesk.com/business/2026/02/18; financemagnates.com, June 8, 2026;
  theblock.co, July 27, 2026).

## 4. Culture & voice

- Present-day public voice (kraken.com/careers, kraken.com/culture) emphasizes: "globally remote
  company driven by independent thinkers who challenge the norm," mission-driven work, and a
  "borderless community." The Payments role posting directs applicants to the culture page before
  applying (jobs.ashbyhq.com req 4e8d9070).
- Remote-first is structural, not a perk: "Krakenites can build their careers from virtually
  anywhere... seniority is earned through impact, not proximity to a headquarters," with
  Krakenites in 70+ countries speaking 50+ languages (blog.kraken.com/news/industry-news/
  remote-is-how-kraken-operates; krakens-workforce-forever-remote-first).
- Benefits noted publicly: stock grants on most roles, flexible PTO, wellness stipend/fitness
  coaching, remote workspace setup bonus, and an option to receive part of pay in crypto
  (kraken.com/careers).
- Hiring language explicitly invites applicants who do not meet every listed requirement if they
  are "passionate or knowledgeable about crypto" (jobs.ashbyhq.com req 4e8d9070, "Our commitment"
  section).
- Reputational watch-out (older, but material to "know before you apply"): in 2022, co-founder
  and then-CEO Jesse Powell circulated an internal 31-page "culture document" asserting
  libertarian values and rejecting mandated DEI language/pronoun policies, and offered a
  severance package ("Jet Ski program") to employees who disagreed and wanted to leave; this drew
  significant press (thedailybeast.com; fortune.com/crypto/2022/09/21). Powell stepped down as
  CEO in 2022/2023 and is now board chair; Dave Ripley and Arjun Sethi are co-CEOs as of 2026
  (kraken.com/press/releases/kraken-announces-leadership-succession-plan; cryptoslate.com/people/
  dave-ripley). Worth knowing the history exists, even though current public-facing culture
  material (2026) does not repeat that framing.

## 6. Engineering & AI direction

- Frontend stack: primary customer web app uses server-rendered Django templates with HTMX and
  vanilla JS for some interactive features, plus many React (TypeScript) single-page apps spread
  across the product (himalayas.app/companies/kraken/tech-stack, sourced from Kraken's own stated
  stack). Backend: Python/Django, Django Rest Framework and GraphQL, heavy AWS usage, HashiCorp
  tooling (Packer, Consul, Terraform) for continuous deployment (same source).
- Mobile: three production apps built in React Native, with custom native modules in Swift
  (iOS) and Kotlin (Android) and a Rust backend layer; the team is moving toward Expo modules over
  some community packages for maintainability and performance (blog.kraken.com/product/
  engineering/how-kraken-fixed-performance-issues-via-incremental-adoption-of-the-react-native-
  new-architecture).
- Performance engineering is a real, demonstrated practice, not just a job-posting buzzword:
  Kraken's data-intensive Pro app (charts constantly updating over WebSockets) shipped a
  React Native New Architecture migration that measured 1.3x faster full-app rendering, 2.5x
  faster home screen, and 5.3x faster trading-flow screen, with the largest gains on low-end
  Android devices (same blog post). Testing stack includes Maestro Cloud (E2E), React Testing
  Library (component), and Sentry/Mixpanel for real-world monitoring, with staged Play Store
  rollouts (same source).
- The Payments & Blockchain team's own req (job 21657 / Ashby 4e8d9070) specifies React.js,
  React Native, Next.js, REST APIs and WebSockets as core requirements, 5+ years of web/mobile
  development experience, and frames the mandate as building "high-quality, fault-tolerant
  crypto payment systems" that communicate with various blockchains while "protecting client
  funds" (jobs.ashbyhq.com req 4e8d9070). This is a distinct team from Consumer or Pro, focused
  on the money-movement layer: integrating with an expanding set of payment providers, funds
  safety/security, and onboarding friction reduction.
- No verified public claims found (in this pass) about a specific generative-AI product bet.
  Press coverage of the May 2026 layoffs attributed them partly to an "AI efficiency drive"
  (financefeeds.com headline: "Kraken Cuts 150 Staff Due to AI Delay, IPO 2027"), but that is a
  press characterization of the layoff, not a confirmed engineering roadmap detail; treat as
  directional color, not a hard fact about Kraken's AI stack.

(Eugenia.ai: 20+ buildings, 10,000+ users with 75% resident adoption; Nextia: 50,000+ users across
iOS/Swift, Android/Java, and React web) and has hands-on experience with the exact technical

Next.js, REST APIs and WebSockets, which lines up closely with that Plutus work.

  (Plutus, a trading-and-portfolio product) plus his personal history as a crypto investor since
  2017 give him a genuine, non-generic reason to care about payment-rail correctness.

  caps many roles; Kraken's own material explicitly frames seniority as impact-based, not
