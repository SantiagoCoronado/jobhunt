# Cresta

## 1. Snapshot
Enterprise AI platform unifying human and AI agents for contact centers (AI Agent, Agent Assist, Knowledge Agent, Conversation Intelligence, Quality Management). Series D stage, ~$1.6B valuation, $270M+ raised total, surpassed $100M ARR in April 2026. HQ Sunnyvale, California; 10 global offices (SF, Sunnyvale, NYC, Toronto, Berlin, London, Cluj-Napoca, Bucharest, Tokyo, Taipei, Melbourne), hybrid model with many fully remote roles. 600+ employees. [cresta.com/about-us](https://cresta.com/about-us), [PR Newswire Series D](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cresta-closes-125m-series-d-to-accelerate-adoption-of-human-centric-ai-in-the-contact-center-302309858.html), [Cresta board/ARR press release](https://cresta.com/press/cresta-names-sequoias-doug-leone-as-board-chair-carl-eschenbach-rejoins-board-as-growth-accelerates)

## 2. Core narrative
Cresta was born in 2017 when Stanford PhD students Zayd Enam and Tim Shi (an early OpenAI researcher), under professor and Google X/Waymo founder Sebastian Thrun, dropped out to build AI that made human contact-center agents better rather than replacing them; that "human-centric AI" thesis is now led by CEO Ping Wu, who built Google's Contact Center AI and Vertex AI before joining. Eight years later the company is riding the agentic AI wave from that same premise: AI agents and human agents on one unified platform, not a chatbot bolted onto a call center. It just crossed $100M ARR (April 2026) nearly doubling its customer base and quadrupling ARR over two years, and its own engineering team is now publicly writing about cutting Forward Deployed Engineer deployment time roughly in half with an internal tool (Conductor) built specifically because generic AI agent frameworks weren't good enough for enterprise-scale, real-conversation-grounded deployments. [CNBC founding story](https://www.cnbc.com/2018/09/16/cresta-ai-enterprise-software-zayd-enam-sebastian-thrun.html), [Series D press release, CEO bio](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/cresta/jobs/4759347008), [ARR/board press release](https://cresta.com/press/cresta-names-sequoias-doug-leone-as-board-chair-carl-eschenbach-rejoins-board-as-growth-accelerates), [Cresta Conductor blog post](https://cresta.com/blog/cresta-conductor-the-agent-for-ai-agent-development)

## 6. Engineering & AI direction
- Agents are treated as "versioned configuration bundles," first-class software artifacts with code as a peer to prompts, not a single prompt string; this is a deliberate reliability choice for enterprise deployment. [Production AI agents blog post](https://cresta.com/blog/building-and-deploying-production-grade-ai-agents-crestas-end-to-end-approach)
- Simulation-first testing: "Simulated Visitors" are persona-based virtual customers derived from de-identified real conversations, used for high-volume scenario and edge-case testing before production; both dynamic (conversation-level) and static (turn-level) evaluation are used, explicitly rejecting one-size-fits-all metrics. [Production AI agents blog post](https://cresta.com/blog/building-and-deploying-production-grade-ai-agents-crestas-end-to-end-approach)
- Voice-latency engineering is a stated technical differentiator: streaming ASR at 200-300ms, semantic turn detection to keep responses under 300ms, first-token LLM latency budgets (250ms to 1s+ depending on provider), hedged calls across multiple LLM providers for reliability, and explicit note that reasoning models are currently too slow for live voice response loops. Tools referenced include Silero VAD and Deepgram Flux. [Latency blog post](https://cresta.com/blog/engineering-for-real-time-voice-agent-latency)
- Cresta Conductor (shipped June 2026, authored by an "Engineer, AI Agent" on the team) is purpose-built for the Forward Deployed Engineer workflow: natural-language interface across scoping, implementation, testing, deployment, and post-launch refinement, grounded in each customer's real conversation and business data rather than generic prompts. [Conductor blog post](https://cresta.com/blog/cresta-conductor-the-agent-for-ai-agent-development)
- The Senior FDE (AI Agent) job posting itself specifies Python and Golang, LLM/prompt engineering, agent frameworks with function calling and RAG, cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure) and DevOps (CI/CD, containerization, monitoring), and API/webhook/data-pipeline integration work, plus a self-paced CoderPad technical assessment as the first interview step. [Greenhouse JD](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/cresta/jobs/4759347008)
