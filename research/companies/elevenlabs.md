# ElevenLabs

Draft dossier for Santiago, prepared for two roles: "Enterprise Solutions Engineer - Mexico"
(job 3625) and "Adoption Strategist - LATAM" (job 3663). Review before use; flag anything that
needs a second check.

## 1. Snapshot

AI research and product company building voice, audio, and agent AI (TTS, STT, conversational
voice/chat agents). Growth-stage, pre-IPO: raised a $500M Series D at an $11B valuation in
February 2026 (3x its prior valuation), $781M raised to date, over $330M ARR at end of 2025.
Headquartered in New York with hub offices in London and Warsaw; remote-first across 30+
countries. Actively building out a Mexico City-based LATAM go-to-market team as part of this
raise's international expansion. [elevenlabs.io/blog/series-d](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/series-d),
[careers page](https://elevenlabs.io/careers), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/04/nvidia-backed-ai-startup-elevenlabs-11-billion-valuation.html)

## 2. Core narrative

ElevenLabs was founded in May 2022 by two Polish engineers, Piotr Dabkowski (ex-Google ML
engineer) and Mati Staniszewski, who was a Deployment Strategist at Palantir before this,
embedding with enterprises and governments to get new technology actually adopted. The founding
insight came from watching badly-dubbed films in Poland and wanting machines to sound as natural
as people. [Wikipedia (Mati Staniszewski)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mati_Staniszewski),
[a16z: Where You Build Is Who You Are](https://a16z.com/where-you-build-is-who-you-are-the-elevenlabs-story/),
[Wikipedia (ElevenLabs)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ElevenLabs)

Four years later the company is scaling that same "get it deployed, not just demoed" DNA into an
enterprise motion: three product lines (ElevenAgents, ElevenCreative, ElevenAPI), $330M+ ARR,
enterprise logos like Deutsche Telekom, Revolut, Meta, Salesforce, Cisco Webex, NVIDIA, and an
explicit IPO trajectory. The company runs with no job titles, "impact not title" and a stated
"founder mindset" hiring bar, i.e. people who identify a problem, scope it, and ship it end to
end. [Job descriptions (3625/3663)](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/elevenlabs), [Series D
blog](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/series-d), [careers page](https://elevenlabs.io/careers)

## 3. Mission & product

- **Mission**: "make communication and creation with technology seamless," originally framed as
  making high-quality audio/dubbing universally accessible in any language and voice.
  [elevenlabs.io/about](https://elevenlabs.io/about)
- **Three platforms** (per both job descriptions and the enterprise page):
  - **ElevenAgents**: build/deploy voice and chat agents at scale, with integrations, testing,
    monitoring, reliability. Consolidates the older "Conversational AI" product and 11.ai; added
    MCP support and Git-style branching. [enterprise page](https://elevenlabs.io/enterprise),
    [Medium 2026 guide](https://medium.com/the-ai-entrepreneurs/elevenlabs-in-2026-the-complete-guide-to-v3-agents-music-and-scribe-7f3c3bdfd201)
  - **ElevenCreative**: generate/edit speech, music, image, video across 70+ languages, aimed at
    creators and marketers.
  - **ElevenAPI**: raw developer access to the foundational audio models (TTS, STT).
- **Business model**: freemium + usage-based credits for individuals/creators ($6-$99/mo
  consumer tiers, $299-$990 team/business tiers), custom-priced enterprise contracts with SSO,
  dedicated support, Zero Retention Mode. [pricing page](https://elevenlabs.io/pricing)
- **Customers**: "10,000+ businesses," named enterprise logos include Deutsche Telekom, Meta,
  Revolut, Square, Salesforce, Cisco Webex, NVIDIA, Duolingo, Harvey, MasterClass, TIME, the
  Ukrainian government, and (regionally) TELUS Digital, Traba, Bertelsmann, Gaia.
  [enterprise page](https://elevenlabs.io/enterprise), [Series D blog](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/series-d)
- **Compliance/security**: SOC2, GDPR, HIPAA-compliant configs with BAAs, regional data
  residency (US/EU/India), SSO/RBAC. Directly relevant to enterprise SE conversations.
  [enterprise page](https://elevenlabs.io/enterprise)
- **Recent flagship models**: Eleven v3 (expressive TTS with inline emotion tags, June 2025),
  Scribe STT (Feb 2025, low word-error-rate) and Scribe v2 Realtime (sub-150ms latency, 90+
  languages, Nov 2025), an AI music generator cleared for commercial use (Aug 2025).
  [Eleven v3 blog](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/eleven-v3), [Medium 2026
  guide](https://medium.com/the-ai-entrepreneurs/elevenlabs-in-2026-the-complete-guide-to-v3-agents-music-and-scribe-7f3c3bdfd201)

## 4. Culture & voice

- Explicit cultural pillars from the JD and careers page: "high-velocity" (rapid experimentation,
  lean autonomous teams, minimal bureaucracy), "impact not job titles" (no formal titles), "AI
  first" (using AI to move faster company-wide, not just in engineering), "excellence
  everywhere," "global team" (talent over location). [job descriptions
  3625/3663](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/elevenlabs), [careers page](https://elevenlabs.io/careers)
- Hiring bar is explicitly framed as looking for "founders who happen to be looking for a job,"
  people who narrate ownership as "I decided, I built, I shipped" rather than purely
  collaborative stories. Interview loop (recruiter screen, async coding, behavioral, practical
  coding on real customer scenarios, product-decomposition round) reported to run 3-5 weeks.
  [jobsbyculture interview
  guide](https://jobsbyculture.com/blog/elevenlabs-interview-prep-2026) (third-party, unverified
  against an official ElevenLabs source; treat interview-process specifics as indicative, not
  confirmed)
- Team composition: "researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders."
  [job descriptions](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/elevenlabs)
- Remote-first, distributed across 30+ countries with hub offices in New York, London, Warsaw;
  annual company-wide offsite (past locations Croatia, Italy), co-working stipend for people not
  near a hub, social-travel stipend. [careers page](https://elevenlabs.io/careers), [job
  descriptions](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/elevenlabs)
- Social impact angle: the "1 Million Voices" impact program, a stated $1B in-kind commitment to
  give people with permanent voice loss free lifetime access to voice-restoration tech.
  [elevenlabs.io/impact-program](https://elevenlabs.io/impact-program)
- Work intensity: third-party (Glassdoor-sourced, via jobsbyculture) signals report 60-70+ hour
  weeks are common and work-life balance is rated low (3.6/5). This is not from an ElevenLabs
  primary source; treat as a data point to sanity-check in interviews, not a confirmed fact.
  [jobsbyculture](https://jobsbyculture.com/blog/elevenlabs-interview-prep-2026)
- Headcount: no official current figure published by the company found. Third-party trackers
  disagree widely (roughly 500 to 1,000+ depending on source/date in 2026); do not cite a
  specific number without flagging it as an estimate. [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/elevenlabs/__Tvkv2vcQvT5RiO80KqXicawZyFtA-r7-J533YWuiDrM)

## 6. Engineering & AI direction

- Trains its own foundational TTS/STT models in-house (not just wrapping third-party LLMs);
  NVIDIA GPU-backed training infrastructure and now a NVIDIA strategic investor relationship.
  [tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2025/09/23/nvidia-to-make-strategic-investment-in-elevenlabs/)
- Stated operating principle: research and product move in lockstep. If a research problem can't
  be solved within roughly three months, product ships a bridge/interim solution rather than
  waiting (the concrete example given: after 9 months researching voice-speed control, they
  shipped a manual slider). [a16z](https://a16z.com/where-you-build-is-who-you-are-the-elevenlabs-story/)
  (single secondary source; treat as a reported anecdote, not an official engineering-blog post)
- "AI first" is a stated company value applied company-wide, including growth and operations,
  not just engineering: "we use AI to move faster with higher-quality results." [job
  descriptions](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/elevenlabs)
- Product org is organized into small, autonomous product teams (reported as roughly 20 teams of
  5-10 people) to keep high-velocity shipping. [jobsbyculture](https://jobsbyculture.com/blog/elevenlabs-interview-prep-2026)
  (secondary source, unverified against an ElevenLabs primary source)
- Forward Deployed Engineering is an explicit, named function: FDEs embed directly inside
  customer infrastructure from day one (not a ticket-queue support model), own system design,
  integration, scaling, change management and compliance through the full implementation
  lifecycle, and stay engaged post-launch. This is the closest existing role to what "Enterprise
  Solutions Engineer" and "Adoption Strategist" plug into organizationally.
  [elevenlabs.io/blog/forward-deployed-engineers](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/forward-deployed-engineers)
- Integration surface for enterprise/solutions work: MCP (Model Context Protocol) support in
  ElevenAgents, Git-style branching for agent configs, and pre-built integrations with
  Salesforce, Stripe, Zendesk, Twilio, HubSpot, Cal.com, Zapier, and (as of March 2026) IBM
  watsonx Orchestrate. [enterprise page](https://elevenlabs.io/enterprise), [IBM
  newsroom](https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-03-25-enterprise-ai-finds-its-voice-elevenlabs-and-ibm-bring-premium-voice-capabilities-to-agentic-ai)
- Required technical bar for the Solutions Engineer role (from the JD itself) is Python
  proficiency plus real understanding of software architecture and common integration patterns,
  used to help enterprise customers architect integrations, not just present demos. [job
  3625](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/elevenlabs/81f53be2-dd0e-433d-94bf-d506973be1ed/application)

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago

straight out of a "Deployment Strategist" role at Palantir, embedding with enterprises to make
new technology actually stick, not just demo well. Santiago has spent his career doing exactly
that as a founder: building AI-powered products (Eugenia.ai, Online Basics, Pragma AI) and then
personally getting them adopted by real customers on the ground, from HOA administrators to
Mexican SMBs. That is a direct match to what both the Solutions Engineer and Adoption Strategist
roles are actually asking for: someone who can go from "here is the API" to "here is a customer
running this in production and getting value," and who has done that self-directed, end to end,
before. [Wikipedia (Mati
Staniszewski)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mati_Staniszewski), [master_resume.yaml]

**Supporting hooks**:

  Online Basics, and at Pragma AI built "Pragma Brain" (a RAG "company brain") plus conversational
  agents with tool-use/function calling. That is the same category of work ElevenAgents customers

- **Why this company**: Lead with the Palantir-Deployment-Strategist-to-founder throughline: this
  is a company whose CEO built his career on getting enterprises to actually adopt new tech, and
  is now scaling that exact motion (ElevenAgents, forward-deployed engineering, a brand-new LATAM
  team) into Mexico City right as Santiago is based there. Avoid listing all four research areas;
  anchor on the deployment DNA plus the LATAM timing.
- **Why you fit**: Lead with concrete deployment reps, not credentials: he has personally taken
  AI-powered products (a RAG "company brain," conversational agents with tool-use, an LLM content
  pipeline) from build to real customer adoption, as the person who owned both the code and the
  relationship. Frame that as directly transferable to architecting integrations (job 3625) or
  building scalable adoption playbooks (job 3663), and be explicit that the shift is from
  "founder owning one company" to "specialist owning many accounts."
- **Anything else**: Native Spanish plus Mexico City residency is not just a checkbox, it lines up
  with where the company itself says it is investing this funding round (Mexico City named
  explicitly in the Series D announcement). Worth naming plainly rather than assuming it is
  obvious.
