## 1. Snapshot

Headless/composable ERP platform for fast-growing omnichannel retail, manufacturing, and B2B
brands. Series A stage (total $37M raised as of November 2025). Roughly 50 employees as of mid
2025, dual HQ San Francisco and Tokyo, "a proud distributed team" with people in Japan, the US, and
"several other countries." Founded 2021, YC S22.
Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/tailor-a-headless-erp-startup-raises-22m-series-a/,
https://www.tailor.tech/about, https://www.crowdfundinsider.com/2025/11/255798-erp-fintech-tailor-completes-series-a-has-raised-37m-to-date/

## 2. Core narrative

Tailor is trying to unbundle the ERP: instead of selling a rigid all-in-one back office, it sells a
composable, API-first platform (GraphQL/REST schemas, 50+ pre-built integrations, workflow
automation primitives) plus a Forward-Deployed Engineering/Professional-Services arm that
implements it directly inside customer operations (accounting, e-commerce, logistics). The company
frames this explicitly as "AI Native Headless ERP": business logic redesigned so both humans and AI
agents can operate the system, with a stated goal of AI agents eventually handling most of the
operational load. Early customers are real operators with messy multi-marketplace and
multi-channel commerce problems (a jewelry brand selling across Shopify, Pinterest, Etsy, TikTok
and Amazon; a textile supplier to a large retailer). This is the throughline a cover letter should
lean on: they are not building generic SaaS, they are building the operating layer under
multi-channel commerce and shipping it hands-on with customers.
Sources: https://www.tailor.tech/about, https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tailor,
https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/tailor-a-headless-erp-startup-raises-22m-series-a/,
job description at https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/tailor/08796053-4fa8-48db-9a73-a977ae2c5434

## 3. Mission & product

- Product: "Tailor Platform," a headless/composable ERP with GraphQL/REST APIs generated from
  schema definitions, 50+ pre-built integrations (Shopify, QuickBooks, Xero, Sage, NetSuite, Odoo,
  3PLs), custom automation via Pipelines/Functions/Event Triggers, state machines for approval
  workflows, and an "ERP lite" packaged product called Tailor Omakase for retail operations.
  Modules cover inventory, omnichannel order management (Shopify, Amazon, Etsy, B2B, POS),
  production management, purchase management, cost accounting, and warehouse management.
  Source: https://www.tailor.tech/, https://www.tailor.tech/about
- AI angle: "agentic AI" features that compare purchase orders against supplier PDFs, reconcile
  data, update ERP records, and trigger workflows automatically; OCR and vector search are called
  out as platform capabilities. CEO Yo Shibata is on record framing the bet as AI agents handling
  "already around 50% and growing toward 90%" of operational load over time.
  Source: https://www.tailor.tech/, https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/tailor-a-headless-erp-startup-raises-22m-series-a/
- Business model: usage-based pricing, no per-seat fees, implementation/training/support bundled
  in (per the tailor.tech site; this is vendor-stated, unverified against a public pricing page).
  Source: https://www.tailor.tech/
- Customers named publicly: Universal Jewelry (custom jewelry, Shopify + Odoo connector, CEO James
  Kao quoted); a textile supplier to a $20B+ retailer; a Japan-based hospitality company generating
  close to $1B annually. Target profile is mid-market, fast-growing omnichannel retail, B2B+DTC,
  and manufacturing.
  Source: https://www.tailor.tech/, https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/tailor

## 6. Employment mechanism for a Mexico-based hire, and location restriction (the open question)

**Verdict: the evidence points toward the JSON-LD `applicantLocationRequirements: USA` being an
address-derived artifact, not a real hiring restriction, for THIS specific req. Confidence:
moderate-high, not certain.**

Reasoning, from the Ashby board's own data (pulled from the public posting API, not scraped
behind login):

- This req's own **location field says "Remote (Anywhere in the world)"** and the description says
  "Remote-first (anywhere with at least 6 hr overlap with US west coast)." Ashby's `address` field
  on the raw JSON is `{"postalAddress": {"addressCountry": "USA"}}`, i.e. it looks like a default
  tied to wherever the req's owning team/entity is registered, not a field anyone filled in to mean
  "must reside in the USA."
- Crucially, **other Tailor reqs on the same board DO carry an explicit, narrower location string**
  when the company actually means to restrict geography: "Forward-Deployed Product Manager - FDPM
  (US Remote)" and "Senior Bilingual Software Engineer – West Coast (U.S.)" both show
  `location: "Remote (North America)"`, and "Senior Frontend Engineer (Australia - AEST, Remote)"
  shows an Australia-scoped address. If Tailor wanted this Full-Stack Software Engineer req
  restricted to the US, the pattern on their own board strongly suggests they would have written
  "Remote (North America)" or similar, the way they did for the sibling US-team roles. They didn't;
  they wrote "Anywhere in the world."
  Source: https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/tailor (raw job board JSON, all ~52 reqs
  inspected for `location`/`address`/`secondaryLocations`)
- The company's own about page states it is "a proud distributed team" with "local presence
  wherever our customers operate," and press coverage independently confirms employees "across
  Japan, the U.S., and several other countries."
  Source: https://www.tailor.tech/about, https://techcrunch.com/2025/06/30/tailor-a-headless-erp-startup-raises-22m-series-a/

What I could NOT verify: the actual legal employment mechanism for a non-US, non-Japan hire
(direct foreign entity employment, contractor agreement, or an Employer-of-Record). No EOR
provider is named anywhere in Tailor's public materials, careers page, or press that I found. No
LinkedIn employee profile search surfaced a confirmed Latin America-based Tailor employee to use as
a hard precedent (search access was limited to public search snippets, not full LinkedIn People
search). This is a real gap: Santiago should ask directly in the intro call how they'd contract a
Mexico City-based hire, rather than assume EOR employment.

On comp: nothing in any source suggests the $120,000 to $160,000 band is location-adjusted. It is
posted as a single flat range attached to a "remote-first, anywhere in the world" req, with no
geo-tiering language anywhere on the board (contrast with the explicit "North America" and
"Australia" scoping used elsewhere, which is about eligibility, not pay tiers). Treat it as a flat,
US-anchored band that Santiago should ask for at or above the midpoint per the profile's
`comp.band_rule`, not something to expect a discount on. This is an inference from absence of
counter-evidence, not a confirmed statement from Tailor; flag as reasoned but unverified.
