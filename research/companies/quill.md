# Quill (quill.co)

Draft dossier for Santiago's review. Researched: 2026-09-02.

## Core narrative

Quill is a small, YC-backed (W20) team that has spent since 2022 quietly building a fullstack
SDK for customer-facing analytics, embedded reporting and dashboards, still under five people
and still prelaunch as of September 2026 [1][2]. The pitch to engineers is direct ownership:
a handful of paying customers (pre-IPO fintech, Series B healthtech, Series A govtech) already
depend on features that ship straight to production, and the team is now hiring ahead of a
public launch [1]. That combination, years of quiet technical depth plus an imminent launch
plus a tiny team where one hire's CLI work went to production the day it shipped, is the
distinctive hook here: real product-market signal without startup-scale bureaucracy [1].

## Mission & product

Quill builds a fullstack SDK (React component library plus data API plus management tooling)
that lets other software companies add customer-facing analytics and data features, in-app
reports, dashboards, and data export, to their own products without building that layer from
scratch [1][2]. YC's own one-line description: "Fullstack API and management platform for
dashboards and reporting" [2]. The quill.co homepage frames the same product as an "AI
Reporting SDK and API" [3]. Named customer use cases from the founder's own hiring post [1]:

- A pre-IPO fintech uses Quill to add custom reporting and data export to its money movement
  product.
- A Series B healthtech uses Quill to deliver custom in-product reports and dashboards to every
  enterprise customer it onboards.
- A Series A govtech uses Quill to add analytics and reporting inside its existing agent/chat
  product.

Stack detail beyond "React library, API, and management toolkit" [2] was not independently
verifiable: quill.co's marketing site is JavaScript-rendered and did not yield body content
through automated fetch, only the page title [3]. Treat specific frontend/backend framework
claims as unconfirmed until confirmed in a live conversation.

## Recent news

- YC batch: Winter 2020 (W20); YC lists the company as founded in 2022, based in San Francisco,
  with a team of 5 [2]. The founding-year versus batch-year gap is as YC's own page states it;
  not independently reconciled further.
- Funding: not verified. The company's own September 2026 hiring post says it is "backed by
  YCombinator & top-tier SV-based investors" but that "fundraising [is] not publicly disclosed,"
  with an offer to share details in a live conversation [1]. No public funding round was found
  via search; treat any specific amount as unconfirmed.
- Hiring push, September 2026: co-founder Rishi Raman posted the Fullstack SWE role (the target
  role below) in the HN "Who is Hiring" thread dated 2026-09, explicitly to grow the team ahead
  of a product launch and to keep up with customer demand for new features [1]. YC's own jobs
  board separately lists a Quill Software Engineer opening at a $100K to $300K base range with
  0.50% to 2.00% equity; that figure is YC's general listing, not the HN post's stated band, and
  the two should not be conflated [2].

## Culture & hiring signals

- Team size: under 5 people as of the September 2026 posting [1], matching YC's listed
  headcount of 5 [2]. This is a very small team; every hire has outsized product surface.
- Stated culture, in the founder's own words: "small, focused, talented team on a highly
  technical product (by developers for developers)"; high product ownership with tight feedback
  loops; the company wants people who "bring opinions, expertise, and experience," not just
  execute existing ideas [1]. The team cites a recent hire who built the company's CLI product
  from zero to one, shipped to production customers the same day [1].
- Remote policy: the role is listed as remote with "PT/ET hours preferred," implying real-time
  overlap with US Pacific/Eastern hours is expected, but nothing in the sources gathered
  explicitly confirms or rules out hiring engineers based outside the United States [1]. This is
  genuinely unknown from public sources; worth a direct question rather than an assumption.
- HQ is listed as San Francisco on the YC page [2], but the open role itself is posted as remote,
  not onsite or hybrid.

## Target role

- **Title:** Fullstack SWE, full-time, remote with PT/ET hours preferred.
- **Pay:** $150,000 to $210,000 USD base plus equity (as stated in the specific HN posting) [1].
- **Posted by:** a co-founder of Quill, in the September 2026 Hacker News "Who is Hiring" thread.
- **Source posting:** https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49522912 (job id 42304 in the local
  jobhunt database).
- **How to apply:** email rishi@quill.co directly, per the founder's own post; that is the only
  application path stated in the posting itself [1]. A separate, generically-worded Quill
  Software Engineer listing also exists on YC's own jobs board (workatastartup.com), but the HN
  post gives a direct email as the fastest path for this specific opening [1][2].
- **Scope per the post:** growing the team to keep up with customer feature demand while
  preparing for a public launch; explicit invitation to bring opinions and experience rather
  than just execute a pre-set roadmap [1].
