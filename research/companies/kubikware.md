## 1. Snapshot

Kubikware is a US-based nearshore software development agency and staff augmentation firm founded in 2003, headquartered in Aventura, Florida with distributed engineering talent across Latin America (Argentina among them) [1][6]. It is a five-time Inc. 5000 honoree and placed No. 146 on Inc.'s 2026 Regionals Southeast list with 38 percent two-year growth [4][8]. Roughly 47 to 49 employees as of mid-2026, spread across three continents [4][6]. No public funding history found; this looks like a bootstrapped, profitable services business, not a VC-backed startup (unverified beyond the absence of any funding record).

## 2. Core narrative

Kubikware's story is 20-plus years as a nearshore dev shop that has quietly compounded through repeat Inc. 5000 recognition rather than headline funding rounds, and it is now retooling its staff-augmentation model around AI: placing engineers on client projects to build LLM-driven workflows and automation (Claude, n8n, Supabase, Whisper) instead of traditional web/mobile staffing alone [1][3][7]. This is an agency pivoting its bread-and-butter offering (staff augmentation, nearshore engineering) toward embedding AI engineers directly inside client teams.
