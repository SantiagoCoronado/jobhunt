# Wizeline (dossier draft for Santiago, review before use)

## 1. Snapshot
Global technology services and consulting company (nearshore software delivery plus AI advisory),
founded 2014 in Guadalajara/Zapopan, Mexico, by Bismarck Lepe (ex-Google, Ooyala founder), now
positioning itself as an "AI-native technology solutions provider." Majority owned by CDPQ
(Canadian institutional investor, majority stake acquired September 2021 alongside Apax Digital
Fund as minority holder); ~1,400 to 1,600 employees (1,462 as of March 2026, 1,637 reported April
2026) across 11 countries and 50+ cities (Americas: US, Canada, Mexico, Colombia, Argentina;
Europe: UK, Spain, Romania; APAC: Vietnam), serving 100+ enterprise clients. New CEO Ines Casares
took over May 6, 2026. Role in context: **Senior AI Agent Engineer**, Mexico, fully remote-eligible
per posting, no salary listed.

## 2. Core narrative
Wizeline spent a decade as a nearshore staff-augmentation and product-engineering shop out of
Guadalajara, then in 2025 to 2026 re-launched itself around a single bet: AI agents embedded
directly into the delivery model, not just AI as a service line. Its flagship offering, "Agentic
Pods," pairs a senior engineer with a roster of ten purpose-built agents (code review, unit
testing, security scanning, incident resolution, tech-debt discovery, QA, documentation, business
analysis) inside each client engagement, and the company claims 20 to 50 percent lower delivery
cost by month 12 and up to 3x delivery velocity from the model. That bet is now backed by external
validation: in August 2026 Wizeline earned AWS's Agentic AI and Generative AI Competency, a
distinction the company says only a small group of AWS partners hold, on top of 120+ enterprise
AWS projects and 50+ individual AWS AI certifications on staff. A new CEO (a 20-year IT-services
veteran promoted from Chief Services Officer, where she ran AI strategy) took over in May 2026
explicitly to push this positioning: "As an AI-native services company, we're not catching up to
this moment. We're leading it." This Senior AI Agent Engineer requisition sits squarely inside
that Agentic Pods build-out: designing the production-grade, tool-augmented agents Wizeline sells
to clients, not internal tooling.

## 3. Mission & product
- Business model: nearshore consulting and delivery. Three named offerings on the current site:
  Studios (nearshore, dedicated innovation teams for client transformation), Agentic Pods
  (cross-functional agile teams augmented by AI agents), and Frontier Partnerships (deep alliances
  with AI-native technology vendors). [wizeline.ai homepage](https://www.wizeline.ai/)
- Agentic Pods: each pod is led by a senior Wizeline engineer who owns technical implementation
  and client-team adoption, pairing that engineer with a defined set of AI agents (documentation,
  security, unit testing, incident resolution, code review, QA/visual regression, tech-debt,
  UX research, project management, business-analyst/requirements agents). Positioned as
  model-agnostic (no proprietary LLM lock-in) with a "REACT framework" for auditable
  reason/act logging aimed at compliance review. Claimed metrics: 20 to 50 percent delivery-cost
  reduction by month 12, up to 3x delivery speed, 92 percent automated QA coverage, 60 to 80
  percent reduction in time-to-ticket, first agents live "next sprint."
  [Agentic Pods page](https://www.wizeline.ai/agentic-pods/),
  [Agentic AI agents detail page](https://www.wizeline.ai/agentic-pods/agents/)
- Customers: enterprise logos shown on the marketing site include Netflix (via True Digital),
  Walmart, Warner Bros., HBO Max, AirAsia, and WeWork, spanning industries such as broadcast,
  fintech, publishing, sports, healthcare, CPG, and life sciences.
  [wizeline.ai homepage](https://www.wizeline.ai/)
- Also runs a separate marketing-AI product line, "Marketing ^ AI," launched August 2025 for
  agent-driven marketing and automated content operations.
  [Wizeline unveils Marketing ^ AI (Yahoo/press)](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/wizeline-unveils-marketing-ai-170000723.html)
- Longstanding community/education arm: Wizeline Academy, free tech courses in Guadalajara
  (4,000+ students trained), and a 2023 partnership with Tec de Monterrey to launch G.AI.L, the
  first Generative AI Lab in Latin America, backed by a $1M, five-year scholarship commitment.
  [Nearshore Americas: Wizeline and Guadalajara](https://nearshoreamericas.com/wizeline-guadalajara-mexico-bismarck-lepe-tech-transformation/),
  [G.AI.L launch (GlobeNewswire)](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2023/10/12/2759626/0/en/Tec-de-Monterrey-and-Wizeline-present-G-AI-L-the-first-Generative-Artificial-Intelligence-Laboratory-in-Latin-America.html)

## 8. Why-fit angles for Santiago (Senior AI Agent Engineer, Adzuna 5849692245)
**Best hook to lead with:** the JD is describing, almost line for line, what Santiago has already
shipped solo. He has built production RAG pipelines with deterministic no-answer and citation
gating (Pragma Brain), a typed MCP tool layer exposing dozens of tools to both an in-app agent and
Claude Code (Kairos, 52 tools; Pragma AI's MCP brain service), an autonomous agent with tool-use
and function calling on a dedicated agent framework (Personal-Finance AI Agent, Hermes), and an
autonomous LLM research agent that drafts memos and converts natural language into backtestable
rules (Plutus). That is direct, hands-on evidence against nearly every required skill in this
posting: LLM orchestration, RAG/embeddings, function calling, MCP, automated evals, and guardrails
(sensitivity labeling at ingest, per-operator token access control).

- **Agent orchestration and MCP, not just LLM calls**: Kairos exposes 52 tools through one typed
  layer (Zod schemas) to both a Claude Agent SDK app and a standalone MCP server; Pragma AI's
  internal platform is a hosted, authenticated MCP "brain" service with per-operator tokens and
  rate limits, plus 15 agent definitions and 10 reusable routines. The JD lists MCP and A2A as a
  nice-to-have and orchestration frameworks (LangChain/LangGraph, OpenAI Agents SDK, CrewAI) as a
  must-have; the concepts map directly even where the named framework differs.
  [resume/master_resume.yaml]
- **RAG with real evaluation and guardrails**: Pragma Brain ships scoped retrieval, deterministic
  no-answer and citation gating, multilingual ES/EN embeddings, LLM-at-ingest sensitivity
  labeling, and an automated eval harness, a direct match to the JD's "embedding pipeline design"
  and "automated evaluations" and "guardrails and PII protection" requirements.

- **Production engineering rigor around agents, not just prototypes**: Plutus runs Celery/Redis
  background workers with idempotent nightly ingestion, a Dockerized multi-service stack, and a

  SQLite migrations across 27 tables. This answers the JD's "software engineering rigor" framing
  and containerization/CI-CD ask better than a research-only AI background would.

- **E-commerce domain, named as a nice-to-have**: five years running Online Basics, an $8M USD

**Honest concerns / watch-outs:**
- **English requirement**: JD asks for B2 Advanced minimum English. Santiago's resume states
  fluent English, worth confirming this clears the bar comfortably rather than assuming.
- **This is a client-services agency role, not a product company**: Wizeline builds agents for
  its clients under Agentic Pods, meaning the day-to-day is consulting delivery (client scoping,
  sprint cadence, multiple concurrent engagements) rather than owning one product roadmap.
  Santiago's founder background is 0-to-1 product ownership; worth being honest in interviews
  about wanting the shift to a delivery/consulting cadence and multiple client contexts, and
  naming the client-facing work he has already done (Pragma AI client diagnostics, Eugenia.ai
  on-site HOA deployments) as the closest precedent.
- **Named frameworks are a partial gap**: the JD's specific must-have orchestration tools
  (LangChain/LangGraph, OpenAI Agents SDK, CrewAI) and vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate) are
  not named in Santiago's master resume; his hands-on agent and RAG work uses different tooling
  (Claude Agent SDK, MCP, custom retrieval, Hermes agent framework). This is a real gap to name
  honestly (concepts transfer, specific framework hours do not) rather than imply direct
  experience with the named tools.
- **Company stability signals are mixed**: reported layoff waves in 2023 to 2024, Glassdoor
  reviews citing thin raises and slow promotion, and a staffing-model "bench" dynamic between
  client engagements are real, if dated, watch-outs worth a direct question in interviews about
  utilization and job security on this specific team.

- **Why this company**: Lead with the Core narrative, Wizeline's pivot from a decade-old nearshore
  staffing shop into an AI-native delivery company that just earned AWS's Agentic AI competency
  and is putting agents directly inside client engagements via Agentic Pods, not just selling AI
  advisory on the side. Frame the interest as wanting to build the production agents behind that
  pivot (this specific role), for a company that is being externally validated on it right now
  (August 2026 AWS competency), rather than a generic "I like AI companies" answer.
- **Why you fit**: Anchor on the direct-match evidence: Santiago has already built the components
  this role owns end to end and solo, MCP tool layers, RAG with eval harnesses and guardrails,
  agent tool-use/function calling, and production infrastructure around all of it (Docker, Celery,
  typed APIs, real test suites). Name it plainly: "the agent components this role is responsible
  for are ones I have already designed, built, and shipped to production," then be candid that
  the specific named frameworks (LangChain/LangGraph, Pinecone/Weaviate) are ones he would ramp
  into quickly given the conceptual overlap.
- **Anything else**: Use this slot to be direct about the agency-versus-product shift (client
  delivery cadence, multiple concurrent engagements) as the honest adjustment from founder-owned
  product work, paired with the closest real precedent he has for it (Pragma AI client
  diagnostics and onboarding, Eugenia.ai's on-site HOA deployments), rather than repeating more
  technical selling points already covered above.
