# Bevy Labs

## Snapshot
Enterprise community management SaaS (forums, events, engagement analytics, and an AI Engagement Hub), founded 2017 in Palo Alto, roughly 70 employees, 100% remote/distributed, raised at least 61.4M USD through a Series C (Series A 6.4M in 2018, Series B 15M in 2020, Series C 40M in 2021).

## Core narrative
Bevy is a mid-stage, founder-led community platform (founded 2017) that has grown almost entirely by acquisition, folding in CMX (2019), Eventtus (2021), and most recently Intros AI (July 2025) to build out an AI Engagement Hub. The company is trying to reposition a mature events/forums product as an AI-native platform, layering agents (knowledge, gamification, content, moderation, sentiment, matchmaking) onto a decade-old core product used by enterprise customers like Google, Salesforce, Slack, Atlassian, and Etsy.

## Mission & product
Bevy states its mission is to help brands "end the loneliness epidemic" by enabling communities to create meaningful connections at scale (bevy.com/about). The core platform includes forums, events and group management, an ROI/analytics suite, gamification, and (as of July 2025) an AI-powered Engagement Hub. Business model is enterprise SaaS; customers span startups and nonprofits up to large enterprises, with named logos including Google, Salesforce, Slack, Atlassian, Etsy, UiPath, and Morning Brew (businesswire.com press release via bevy.com blog). Bevy says it supports more than 10,000 communities globally (bevy.com/about).

## Culture & voice
Bevy is fully remote/distributed ("100% remote company... work from anywhere," bevy.com/b/jobs) with benefits including unlimited PTO, an $500 home-office stipend, internet/mobile stipends, 85% company-paid US health coverage, parental leave, a wellness program, and a six-week paid sabbatical after five years. Stated values include Community First, Stand Up (defend your principles), clear communication, and a growth mindset (bevy.com/about). The careers page explicitly invites people who "do not feel qualified" to apply, and frames hiring around "proactive and authentic, motivated learners" (bevy.com/b/jobs). Public/press voice around the AI launch is upbeat and strategic rather than technical, leaning on customer-facing language ("AI toolkit to grow engagement," CEO Derek Andersen) rather than engineering specifics.

## Recent news
- July 8, 2025: Bevy acquired Intros AI (its third acquisition, after CMX in 2019 and Eventtus in 2021) and launched the AI-powered Engagement Hub, announced at the RAISE Summit in Paris. The Hub bundles a community knowledge agent, gamification agent, content creation agent, moderation agent, pulse sentiment agent, plus Intros AI's 1:1 matchmaking engine (businesswire.com, martechvibe.com, bevy.com blog).
- Intros AI, founded 2020 by David Kobrosky, had facilitated over 1 million introductions across roughly 1,000 communities before the acquisition (businesswire.com).
- As of the careers page check on 2026-08-27, Bevy's public jobs board shows no open roles ("No items found"), so current hiring activity could not be confirmed beyond the historic Torre posting (bevy.com/b/jobs).

## Engineering & AI direction
Bevy's most recent, and only publicly detailed, AI move is the Engagement Hub built on the acquired Intros AI matchmaking engine plus a set of purpose-built agents (knowledge, gamification, content, moderation, sentiment). No engineering blog, public stack write-up, or technical deep dive was found; the company's public AI narrative is customer/product-facing (personalizing community engagement at scale) rather than a developer-facing engineering blog. The 2020 "Software Engineer, Internal Tools" posting (see below) described a Production Quality team building internal tooling and automation for a community platform serving millions of end users, which is the only first-party description of internal engineering priorities found.
