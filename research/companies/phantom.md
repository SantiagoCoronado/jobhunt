# Phantom (phantom.com)

Draft dossier for Santiago's review. Target role: job id 26975, "Senior Software Engineer,
Frontend" (Ashby req e7b83c02-55c2-4037-9209-93deb3b7492c).

## 1. Snapshot

Self-custodial crypto wallet and, increasingly, consumer money app (trading, spending, a Visa
debit card), founded January 2021, San Francisco HQ with a distributed remote team of roughly
50 to 100+ people. Series C closed January 2025: $150M at a $3B valuation, led by Sequoia and
Paradigm with a16z and Variant participating ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/335305/phantom-wallet-raises-150-million-at-3-billion-valuation),
[Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-01-16/crypto-wallet-startup-gets-3-billion-valuation-in-sequoia-paradigm-deal)).
20M+ users per the company's own copy as of this posting ([phantom.com](https://phantom.com/),
[job posting](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/phantom)), up from 15M MAU and $25B
in self-custodied assets at the Series C in January 2025 ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/335305/phantom-wallet-raises-150-million-at-3-billion-valuation)).

## 2. Core narrative

Phantom started as the wallet built for Solana and grew into the default self-custodial wallet
across Solana, Ethereum, Bitcoin, Polygon, Base and (announced, launch date unconfirmed) Sui,
riding Solana's usage cycle to #1 in Google Play's finance category and a top-50 all-category
app ranking ([job posting](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/phantom); Sui
announcement: [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/333118/crypto-wallet-phantom-quashes-airdrop-rumors-after-social-feature-launch-sui-support-announcement)).
It is now deliberately repositioning from "wallet" to "money app": it shipped social profiles
and friends/usernames (December 2024) and, in December 2025, started early access to Phantom
Cash, a Visa debit card funded by its own USD stablecoin CASH on Solana, issued through Lead
Bank and Bridge Ventures ([crypto.news](https://crypto.news/phantom-wallet-on-chain-debit-card-u-s-users-2025/),
[The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/333118/crypto-wallet-phantom-quashes-airdrop-rumors-after-social-feature-launch-sui-support-announcement)).
That is the story worth leaning into: a wallet company betting its next stage of growth on
becoming a spending and trading app for mainstream users, not just a crypto power-user tool.

## 3. Mission & product

- Self-description: "the modern money app used by tens of millions," combining wallet, trading
  (spot, perpetual futures, prediction markets, tokenized equities), sending money, and spending
  via a Visa-linked card, built on self-custody ("we never have access") ([phantom.com](https://phantom.com/),
  [job posting](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/phantom)).
- Multi-chain: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Polygon and Base are live; Sui was announced in
  December 2024 as a fourth L1 alongside Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana, no confirmed launch date
  found ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/333118/crypto-wallet-phantom-quashes-airdrop-rumors-after-social-feature-launch-sui-support-announcement)).
- Company is Solana-heavy in practice: reported ~39% share of the Solana wallet market and deep
  Solana-native features (Jupiter swap aggregation, staking, priority fees) per third-party
  market trackers, treat as directionally accurate rather than exact
  ([CryptoSlate review, unverified precision](https://cryptoslate.com/crypto-wallets/phantom-wallet-review/)).
- Business model: not explicitly disclosed by the company in what I found. Public reporting
  points to swap/trade fees and, going forward, revenue tied to Phantom Cash card spend and
  stablecoin float; no confirmed revenue figures found (mark as unverified).
- Founded January 2021 by a group of engineers, designers and former founders; built with about
  40,000 beta testers before public launch, grew to 3M users by mid-2021
  ([phantom.com/about](https://phantom.com/about)).
- Corporate entity for the card product is "Phantom Technologies, Inc." per the job posting's
  data-handling notice ([job posting](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/phantom)).

## 4. Culture & voice

- Team is deliberately lean for its user base: reported at roughly 50 to 100+ people serving
  20M+ users, a "high-caliber people, very selective hiring" model rather than headcount scaling
  (third-party careers writeup, not a primary Phantom source: [gm.careers](https://gm.careers/blog/working-at-phantom);
  official leadership team framed as "100+ person team" as of the Series C period:
  [phantom.com/about](https://phantom.com/about)).
- Engineering is generalist by design, not siloed by platform. Phantom has no dedicated mobile
  team; a small platform team owns foundational infrastructure and full-stack product engineers
  across the company ship mobile features directly. Quote from an engineer: "We don't have a lot
  of engineers with background in React Native, and yet nearly every engineer at Phantom is able
  to contribute to the mobile app" ([Expo engineering blog](https://expo.dev/blog/how-phantom-ships-a-secure-high-performance-crypto-wallet-with-expo)).
- Hiring bar leans on genuine product usage: expect candidates to have actually used the wallet
  and to show up with informed opinions on it, per a third-party careers profile describing 4 to
  5 interview rounds over 2 to 4 weeks covering background, technical and system-design stages
  (not independently confirmed by Phantom; [gm.careers](https://gm.careers/blog/working-at-phantom)).
- Public voice is deliberately irreverent and meme-literate on X/Instagram/Reddit, a departure
  from formal crypto-company marketing (secondary source, not Phantom-authored:
  [Medium writeup on Phantom's Web3 marketing](https://medium.com/@onyekaadedayo/how-to-do-web3-marketing-in-a-bear-market-lessons-from-phantom-wallet-73ae7acd282d)).
- Job posting language emphasizes "raising the bar with each new hire" and wants engineers who
  interface directly with end users and developers, not just ship in isolation
  ([job posting](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/phantom)).

## 6. Engineering & AI direction

- **Frontend stack (this role's surface)**: React, TypeScript, React Query on the extension and
  website; Solana web3.js and ethers.js for chain integration are explicitly named as core
  qualifications for this exact req ([job posting](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/phantom)).
- **Mobile stack**: React Native and Expo SDK in a monorepo, TypeScript, React Query, Signalium
  (their own open-source signals-based state library), FlashList for list performance, custom
  Expo modules (Secure Store, WebBrowser, proprietary native modules for things like WebView
  patches and Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter) ([Expo engineering blog](https://expo.dev/blog/how-phantom-ships-a-secure-high-performance-crypto-wallet-with-expo)).
- **Web/marketing site stack**: Next.js, TypeScript, Emotion, Jotai, Framer Motion, Lottie
  ([npm/docs signal via search aggregation, treat as directional](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@phantom/wallet-sdk)).
- **CI/CD**: EAS Build triggered from GitHub Actions on PRs, weekly dev-client builds shared via
  Slack QR codes, EAS Submit for weekly production releases, Maestro for end-to-end testing, EAS
  Update and Expo Fingerprint used to cut roughly 15 minutes off a 60-minute test cycle
  ([Expo engineering blog](https://expo.dev/blog/how-phantom-ships-a-secure-high-performance-crypto-wallet-with-expo)).
- **Developer platform**: ships its own public SDKs (Browser SDK, React SDK) with full
  TypeScript typing for third-party developers building on Phantom
  ([Phantom developer docs](https://docs.phantom.com/sdks/browser-sdk)).
- **Security posture and incidents**: markets itself on "industry-leading security" and
  self-custody where "we never have access" ([phantom.com](https://phantom.com/)). Two notable
  incidents to know before the interview: (1) an August 2022 drain of ~$4M across roughly 9,230
  wallets was traced to a vulnerability in the Slope wallet app, not Phantom's own systems, per
  Solana developers and outside auditors ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/162673/solana-wallet-provider-phantom-says-its-systems-were-uncompromised-in-4-million-hack)); (2) a
  still-pending lawsuit (reported January 2025) alleges Phantom stored decrypted private keys in
  browser memory and that a compromised developer laptop led to a $500K theft, this is an
  allegation in litigation, not an established fact ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/314783/phantom-wallet-sued-500k-meme-coin-theft-alleged-security-flaw)).
  Phantom also confirmed it was unaffected by the December 2024 Solana web3.js supply-chain
  attack because it never used the compromised library versions ([Mitrade](https://www.mitrade.com/insights/news/live-news/article-3-506371-20241205)).
- **AI**: no engineering-blog evidence of an AI product bet found. The job posting does disclose
  that Phantom "may use AI-powered tools and third-party service providers for transcription,
  note-taking, scheduling" in its hiring process ([job posting](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/phantom)); that is a hiring-ops detail, not a product signal, and should not be
  overstated as an "AI direction."

## 7. Why-fit angles for Santiago

Best hook to lead with: Santiago has been a personal crypto investor since 2017 and has used
self-custodial wallets across market cycles, including the ones Phantom is explicitly trying to
win (Solana-native users, multi-chain holders). That is a real, specific reason to want to build
this exact product, distinct from "I like blockchain" generalities. Pair it with the React 18 or
19 plus TypeScript frontend work he has shipped and owned end to end (Plutus: React 18, TS,
Vite, Tailwind, WebSocket and SSE real-time layers; Kairos: React 19, TypeScript, Electron), which
matches the stated stack (React, TypeScript, React Query) for this specific req
(source for stack claims: `resume/master_resume.yaml`; job requirements: [job posting](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/phantom)).

Secondary hooks:
- Solana web3.js and ethers.js are named qualifications; Santiago's Plutus project already
  integrates multiple external market-data provider APIs and a WebSocket real-time layer, which
  is the same shape of problem (external chain/data integration plus a real-time frontend), even
  though it is not blockchain-specific (`resume/master_resume.yaml`).
- Phantom's generalist, no-silo engineering model ("nearly every engineer... contribute[s] to
  the mobile app") rewards the kind of full-stack range Santiago has: frontend, backend, and
  product ownership across Online Basics, Eugenia.ai, and his own shipped products
  (`resume/master_resume.yaml`; culture claim: [Expo blog](https://expo.dev/blog/how-phantom-ships-a-secure-high-performance-crypto-wallet-with-expo)).
- The role explicitly includes "interfacing with users" and leading new-chain initiatives, close
  to the 0-to-1, direct-client, product-owning work he did founding Online Basics and Eugenia.ai
  ([job posting](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/phantom); `resume/master_resume.yaml`).

Honest concerns and watch-outs:
- No blockchain-specific production experience on the resume. The posting lists "experience in
  a blockchain based project" as nice-to-have, not required, and lists React/TypeScript/React
  Query as the primary bar, so this is a real gap to name plainly in an interview, not paper over
  ([job posting](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/phantom)).
- Comp: the posted target base is $180,000 to $220,000 ([job posting](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/phantom)), well above `config/profile.yaml`'s
  target_usd of $100,000 and min_usd of $70,000. Per the profile's band_rule, a posted band
  overrides the default anchor: an ask near the midpoint (about $200,000) is the right target if
  this stage is reached, not the $100,000 default.
- Location: verified 2026-09-01 that this exact req is tagged plain "Remote" in Phantom's Ashby
  posting API with no geographic clause in the body, while Phantom explicitly tags other reqs
  (payroll, brand, design, release management) "Remote-US." That is a real distinction the
  company draws elsewhere in its own req list ([Ashby posting API, confirmed 2026-09-01](https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/phantom)), so it is a positive signal, not a
  confirmed "yes" for Mexico-based hires. Do not claim it as settled in application text.
- Security incident history (the pending 2025 lawsuit in particular) is worth being aware of but
  should not be raised unprompted; it is unresolved litigation, not a fact to cite as company
  criticism.

## 8. Application-question angles

**Why this company**: Lead with being a self-custodial wallet user since 2017, across the market
cycles Phantom is now trying to convert into "money app" users with Phantom Cash. State plainly
that this is a product he has opinions about because he has used competing and prior-generation
wallets, not a company he is discovering through the job posting.

**Why you fit**: Lead with the frontend stack match (React, TypeScript, real-time data layers)
and name one concrete thing built at that level of complexity (Plutus's WebSocket quote feed and
SSE streaming client, or the design-system and testing discipline on the Hydrel portal), then
name the blockchain-API gap honestly and frame it as the kind of external-API integration work
he has already done (Amazon, Mercado Libre, market-data providers) rather than claiming
blockchain experience he does not have.

**Anything else**: Only if asked, note the location question directly: this req reads as plain
"Remote" with no country restriction in Phantom's own posting data, while he is Mexico
City-based and not relocating. State it as a fact to confirm early in the process, not as a
settled assumption.
