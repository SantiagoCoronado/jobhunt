## 2. Core narrative

Primero's pitch is that Latin America's largest enterprises (banks, insurers, manufacturers,
retailers, governments) still run core operations on fragmented, often on-prem legacy software,
sometimes unchanged since the 1990s, with critical business rules and know-how living in
employees' heads rather than in any system. Their product, Primia, connects to those legacy
systems (ERP, CRM, inventory, finance, warehouse management, document repositories), unifies the
resulting data into a shared enterprise ontology, and deploys AI agents on top of that foundation
to execute real operational work (credit decisions, pricing, fraud, invoice reconciliation,
dispute investigation). Deployment is module by module, with Primero's own engineers embedded
inside the client organization to build the first workflow alongside the client's team before
expanding. The company frames this explicitly as "sovereign AI": model-agnostic, deployed on the
client's own infrastructure, so no model provider sits between an institution and its competitive
advantage.
[Primero LinkedIn About](https://www.linkedin.com/company/primero-ai/); [Jose Murillo LinkedIn
post, launch day](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jos%C3%A9-murillo-34256317a/); [PR Newswire launch
release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/primero-launches-as-the-ai-transformation-company-for-latin-america-302859100.html)

## 3. Mission & product

- Product, Primia, in three stages per the company's own site: (1) Systems Integration, connect
  legacy tools, spreadsheets, and internal APIs, named integrations include SAP, Excel,
  Salesforce, Oracle, Snowflake; (2) Semantic Layer, build a shared enterprise ontology as
  operational context; (3) AI Workflows, deploy agents and internal applications inside business
  processes with human approval checkpoints on anything consequential. Product categories:
  Business Intelligence (natural-language queries to dashboards), Agents (end-to-end process
  execution), Internal Apps (custom forms, tables, applications), Ontology (semantic definitions
  of business objects and their relationships).
  [primero.com, via WebFetch extraction](https://primero.com/)
- Enterprise governance is a first-class feature, not an afterthought: audit trails, traceable
  model calls, access governance, SOC 2 compliance, data residency control, enterprise-grade
  authentication. This matches the "regulated LATAM blue chip" customer base directly.
  [primero.com, via WebFetch extraction](https://primero.com/); [Primero LinkedIn
  About](https://www.linkedin.com/company/primero-ai/)
- Named customers: gym operator SmartFit, fuel retailer Terpel, food company Verde Valle,
  Kimberly-Clark de Mexico, and Mexico City's Secretaria de Desarrollo Economico (SEDECO, a
  government agency). Combined, Jose Murillo states these customers generate more than $45
  billion in annual revenue. Concrete cited results: loan approvals cut from 30 days to 1, dispute
  investigations cut from 25 days to 1, and workflows already automating "tens of millions of
  dollars" for a single customer.
  [PR Newswire launch release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/primero-launches-as-the-ai-transformation-company-for-latin-america-302859100.html);
  [Jose Murillo LinkedIn launch post](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jos%C3%A9-murillo-34256317a/)
- Industries served per the company's own site: consumer packaged goods, insurance, health and
  fitness/recreational sports, financial services, manufacturing, retail; the company's own
  language also names banks, lenders, insurers, manufacturers, retailers, and governments as
  deployment targets. [primero.com, via WebFetch extraction](https://primero.com/); [Primero
  LinkedIn About](https://www.linkedin.com/company/primero-ai/)
